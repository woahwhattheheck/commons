What a $29 Agent Failure Autopsy handoff looks like

One failed coding-agent run becomes a bounded evidence queue: payment is verified by the operator outside the tool, sanitized intake is checked, evidence is represented by digests rather than private filenames, and the case cannot enter analysis until both payment and intake gates are satisfied.

The public sample intentionally stays unpaid so it cannot be mistaken for proof that a transaction occurred.

What changes after a real purchase

  1. The operator reads back the existing provider receipt and records only its SHA-256 digest plus VERIFIED_PAID.
  2. Sanitized case facts and digest-only evidence metadata are compiled. Missing evidence stays on hold.
  3. A ready case receives an evidence-linked diagnosis with uncertainty, fix steps, and a replay/regression/prevention check.
  4. If a defensible diagnosis cannot be delivered under the published contract, the queue records a refund-required state; this tool does not execute the refund.
  5. After actual delivery, the operator records a digest of the delivered artifact. That proves the artifact identity, not cash settlement.
