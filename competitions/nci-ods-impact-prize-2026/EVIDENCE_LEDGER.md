## Competition facts

1. **NCI Genomic Data Commons announcement**  
   `https://gdc.cancer.gov/turn-data-sharing-ideas-impact-enter-nci-ods-impact-prize`  
   The NCI page says the ODS Impact Prize seeks ideas and successful strategies that advance broad, rapid, equitable sharing and reuse of NCI-funded cancer research outputs. It names Track 1 ideas, Track 2 exemplars, and Track 3 NCI Data Jamboree submissions. It explicitly says eligible research outputs extend beyond data to software, tools, methods/protocols, models, clinical trial results, biospecimens, and other resources. It states a $200,000 total purse, $20,000 Grand Prize, and October 5, 2026 submission close.

2. **NCI public announcement / DCCPS**  
   `https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nci-cancer-control_nci-office-of-data-sharing-impact-prize-activity-7488260603074011138-dYKQ`  
   The NCI public announcement says 16 prizes are available and awards across the tracks range from $10,000 to $15,000, with a $20,000 Grand Prize. It says there is no career-stage requirement and explicitly invites a broad community including librarians, patient advocates, and citizen scientists.

3. **Detailed NIH challenge page**  
   `https://www.nih.gov/challenges/nci-office-data-sharing-impact-prize`

## Standards / policy anchors

4. **NIH Final Data Management and Sharing Policy**  
   `https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-21-013.html`  
   NIH defines data management and metadata in terms that support accessibility, reliability, quality, interpretation, and reuse, including provenance and transformations, and encourages FAIR-consistent practices.

5. **2026 NIH DMS plan update**  
   `https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-26-046.html`  
   NIH updated required DMS-plan elements in 2026 and reiterates that plans must reflect the proposed management/sharing approach and be updated as needed.

6. **NIH research software sharing guidance**  
   `https://datascience.nih.gov/tools-and-analytics/best-practices-for-sharing-research-software-faq`  
   NIH promotes broad dissemination of research software and identifies FAIR principles as a useful framework for software management and sharing.

7. **DataCite Metadata Schema 4.7**  
   `https://schema.datacite.org/`  
   The current DataCite schema is 4.7, released 2026-03-03. DataCite describes its schema as core metadata for accurate, consistent identification of resources for citation and retrieval and includes relation vocabulary for connected research objects.
