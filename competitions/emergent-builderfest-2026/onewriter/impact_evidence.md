# Impact evidence plan

Status: `MEASUREMENT_PLAN_ONLY` — **no production impact claimed here**

The Builder Fest rubric gives Business Impact / Potential to Scale the largest weight. OneWriter therefore needs measured business use, not invented success copy.

## Before-use baseline

Choose a bounded TJLabs operating window before OneWriter is used and retain:
- lead/outreach claim attempts;
- known near-simultaneous duplicate claims, including claims that chose different routes to the same organization/opportunity;
- duplicate external touches actually detected;
- stale/unowned lanes requiring manual recovery;
- route failures incorrectly mixed with buyer outcomes, if reconstructable;
- time window and source receipt identifiers.

Do not backfill guesses. Mark unreconstructable historical metrics `UNKNOWN`.

## Instrumented use window

For every candidate external touch:
1. request an organization-lane lease first;
2. preserve the receipt and selected normalized route;
3. if an external send later occurs under the existing coordination system, record only its admitted provider outcome id and exact leased route;
4. if a genuine human reply occurs, record an admitted retained human-evidence id;
5. never paste private message bodies into the public demo.

Measure `claim_attempts`, `claims_granted`, `collisions_prevented`, `duplicate_touches_prevented`, `stale_lanes_recovered`, `sent_hard_fences`, `dead_routes_recorded`, `human_reopens`, median/p95 candidate-to-lease time, unique workers/agents, and measured-window duration.

Split collision reporting into same-route vs cross-route when evidence permits; that directly tests the failure mode OneWriter was strengthened to prevent.

## Evidence standard

A contest impact claim is publishable only when:
- the metric recomputes from retained OneWriter receipts;
- time window and population are explicit;
- synthetic-demo counts are excluded;
- a prevented duplicate is counted only when a second claim was actually denied against a live matching **organization-lane** lease;
- a route change did not mint a new lane;
- provider/human evidence IDs passed the exact trimmed nonempty 1–240/no-control admission rule;
- a dead route is not called buyer rejection;
- provider SENT is not called interest;
- human reply is not inferred from auto-acks, tickets, silence, or provider acceptance.

## Preferred factual phrasing after measurement

- "During <window>, OneWriter denied N overlapping claims across M unique organization lanes before a duplicate external touch could be attempted; X of those conflicts proposed different routes."
- "It recovered N expired leases and separated N provider route failures from human outcomes."
- "K workers/agents used the app across <window>."

Do **not** claim revenue created unless independently settled and attributable; leads saved without evidence; response-rate improvement from weak samples; customer satisfaction without measurement; or contest placement/prize before official results.

## Scale evidence

Demonstrate structurally:
- organization-lane key prevents alias-based collision splitting;
- lease-scoped route evidence preserves exact provider truth;
- atomic conditional writes support concurrent agents;
- receipts allow audit/reconstruction;
- purpose/opportunity + route metadata make the state machine portable to sales, vendors, recruiting, partnerships, support escalation, and approvals.

## Owner review packet before submission

Collect deployed URL/version timestamp; screenshots/video of a true cross-route concurrent collision denial; exported real-use receipt summary; recomputation result; synthetic-vs-real banner screenshots; privacy review; exact impact claims; one-active-submission census; deployment status; separate submission status.
