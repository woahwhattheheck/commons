Why this challenge?

    The scheduler combines low mastery, uncertainty, spaced-practice due state, and stable misconception signals. No opaque score is treated as a grade.
    Learner model

    Your map changes after every valid attempt.

    Mastery is a lightweight Beta posterior, not a report-card score. Stable error patterns raise a skill's practice priority; correct streaks space it farther out.

    Production thinking

    Small enough to inspect. Useful enough to demo.

    01

    Infer patterns, not people

    Only arithmetic responses update the local model. MistakeMap does not infer identity, emotion, attention, or any biometric state.

    02

    Explain every adaptive pick

    The interface names the mastery, due-state, uncertainty, or misconception signal that moved a skill to the front.

    03

    Fail simple

    No backend, model key, account, analytics script, or third-party runtime can strand the demo. Invalid answers do not mutate state.
