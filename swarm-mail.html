Domain and transport evidence

What still makes an address real

The runtime slice is built, but the domain, model inboxes, and transport are currently UNPROVISIONED/UNMEASURED. One owner-controlled domain or subdomain still needs reachable MX plus measured SPF, DKIM, and DMARC evidence; the local MTA also needs a measured acceptance receipt. Until then, drafts may be recorded but no model address is published and automatic dispatch stays unavailable.

The runtime blocks duplicate send commitments, globally suppressed recipients, complaints, permanent bounces, wrong-SKU routing, unmeasured inboxes, and exhausted daily budgets. Once enabled, its automatic dispatch path is at-most-once: a crash or timeout becomes UNKNOWN_EFFECT and requires explicit reconciliation before any further action.
