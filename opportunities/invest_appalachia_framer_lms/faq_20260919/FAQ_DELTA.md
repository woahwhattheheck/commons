## Qualification interpretation that must travel with the packet

Q5 and Q6 change the acceptable structure of experience evidence. The evaluation should no longer assume that the applicant company itself, or one individual, must have delivered both platforms. A proposed team may bring that history collectively, including a named specialist. The proposal still needs an attributable mapping of platform, person or firm, hands-on responsibility, reference, and planned involvement. Q7 also permits describing an unfilled role and its anticipated cost, but that planning allowance is not proof that a person with the required history has joined the team.

This distinction supports an evidence request, not a positive qualification finding. The old workshare's statement that the prime must own the two-platform track record needs review in the next source-bound generation; contract accountability and personally possessing all experience are different questions. Do not upgrade `MISSING` or `HOLD` until the retained records support the actual proposed delivery team. Unchanged bidder evidence, price placeholders, and old digests remain unchanged by this document.
