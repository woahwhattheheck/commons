# RFP-127519 response architecture

This is a production outline, not a submission and not buyer acceptance. It maps the buyer's scored structure into an evidence-first response so a qualified prime can assemble a competitive proposal without generic AI marketing.

## Executive posture

- Route: **PARTNER-FIRST / PRIME-HOLD** for TJLabs.
- Buyer ceiling: **$250,000 fixed price**; $92,590 is identified by the buyer as contingent on GO Virginia Region 2 planning-grant approval.
- Addendum 1 deadlines: questions Sep 25, 2026 11:59 PM ET; proposal Oct 2, 2026 11:59 PM ET.
- Proposal page limit: 20 pages excluding resumes.
- Teams permitted; one prime must be identified.

## 1. Approach and methodology — 30%

### Phase 1: evidence and demand
1. Build a sampling frame across all four buyer target clusters, company size and AI-adoption maturity.
2. Conduct 32–40 company interviews, minimum 8 per target cluster, with in-person execution as required.
3. Conduct 8–14 in-person partner/institution interviews.
4. Conduct 3–5 interviews with comparable AI initiatives/researchers.
5. Quantify AI adoption readiness, prioritized use cases, blockers, compute/data/governance needs and current/programmatic demand.
6. Map occupation/task exposure, credential supply and gaps, workforce-provider roles and program formats.
7. Investigate each proposed site plus a no-dedicated-site alternative.

### Phase 2: integrated implementation case
1. Translate validated demand into programming by pillar/site.
2. Build site utilization, infrastructure and compute-access assumptions from Phase-1 evidence.
3. Build capex/opex and five-year pro forma from demand and cost evidence rather than independently.
4. Recommend governance/legal operating model and partner roles.
5. Produce implementation sequencing, sustainability/funding logic and risks.

### Method controls
- source/evidence registry for every major quantitative claim;
- interview provenance and sampling-frame completeness without publishing private respondent content;
- explicit confidence/limitation table;
- deterministic calculation workbooks/pipelines for reproducibility;
- human review for recommendations affecting governance, funding or institutional commitments.

## 2. Relevant experience and references — 25%

Prime inserts only real, reference-permitted comparable engagements. Each case should bind:
- client / project / dates;
- regional economic-development or innovation feasibility scope;
- employer primary research sample;
- site/real-estate/cost/pro-forma work where applicable;
- governance/implementation component;
- measurable outcome;
- reference-contact permission and contact details supplied by the actual performing organization.

TJLabs open-source/internal work can support an AI capability narrative, **not** substitute for the prime's customer references.

## 3. Team qualifications — 15%

Show one integrated RACI. Prime owns economic-development fieldwork, site/pro-forma/governance and contract administration. TJLabs is proposed only for bounded AI analysis/tooling if the prime agrees. Resumes and availability must be real and current.

## 4. Cost appropriateness and value — 15%

Use `budget.py` to validate arithmetic and the $250k ceiling. The owner/prime supplies actual rates, travel treatment, subcontract split and milestone values. Show the funding contingency explicitly; do not represent contingent funds as awarded cash.

## 5. Topic and region knowledge — 15%

Ground the response in the buyer's four target clusters and three-site concept, local workforce/higher-ed ecosystem, data-center/compute context, regional innovation institutions, and GO Virginia implementation logic. Avoid a generic national AI-center template.

## Required buyer deliverables

1. Regional AI Industry Demand and Use Case Study.
2. Workforce Analysis and Programming Recommendations.
3. Site Feasibility Analysis.
4. Business Plan, Governance Framework, and Sustainability Model.
5. One to three executive-committee/coalition presentations.

## Submission preflight

A human owner should not authorize submission until all are true:
- exact current official RFP + every addendum/Q&A retained and hashed;
- prime identity and team roles confirmed;
- mandatory firm/team evidence and similar references retained;
- interview logistics and in-region capacity confirmed;
- SCC/legal-position evidence and insurance plan confirmed;
- all resumes/current availability confirmed;
- exact budget validates and is commercially approved;
- proprietary-information treatment reviewed;
- authorized signatory and non-collusion/general-condition certifications reviewed;
- final 20-page compliance check complete;
- send route and deadline independently rechecked.
