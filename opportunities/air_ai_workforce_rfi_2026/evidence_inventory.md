## Material gaps for a direct RFI

The repository does not currently establish all of the following for the submitting legal entity:

1. an established workforce-development delivery track record;
2. a named 2027–2028 worker/learner population under the organization's delivery control;
3. an evidence-backed size for that population;
4. externally supportable workforce outcomes or early-result metrics;
5. a committed delivery/evaluation relationship with a workforce board, training provider, education institution, employer, or equivalent;
6. participant consent/data-governance arrangements for any real evaluation.

Those are evidence gaps, not negative claims. Owner-supplied private evidence can cure them if it exists.

## Partner cure

A credible `PARTNER_RFI` route requires a **committed**, evidence-bound partner that can truthfully supply:

- workforce-delivery history and population relationship;
- specific 2027–2028 population and expected service volume;
- staff/service context for human oversight and worker voice;
- lawful access to outcome/process data needed for the proposed evaluation;
- permission to describe its role and evidence in the AIR response.

A logo, public webpage, prospect name, or hoped-for relationship is not a commitment.

## Claim rules

- Do not call Commons engineering work a workforce outcome.
- Do not convert a procurement qualification into a delivered program.
- Do not claim users, placements, wage gains, retention, training completions, or partner consent without evidence.
- `NO_EARLY_RESULTS_CLAIMED` is preferable to invented evidence.
- The AIR RFI has no award; even a later invitation is not revenue.
