Our proposed engagement is deliberately vendor-neutral. The goal is to give RCAP a defensible decision about whether to stabilize and modernize its existing DCS, replace it, pursue a hybrid path, or take another course supported by the evidence gathered during discovery.

## 2. Proposed approach

### Phase 1 — kickoff and evidence inventory

We will confirm the decisions RCAP needs the assessment to enable, stakeholder availability, working cadence, known constraints, budget considerations, and the expected form of the final System Summary. We will inventory mutually agreed documentation and the current technology ecosystem before drawing conclusions.

### Phase 2 — structured stakeholder discovery

The fixed fee will include **[OWNER FACT REQUIRED: number]** discovery sessions. The proposed interview map covers program operations, data/reporting, finance and Unanet touchpoints, security/governance, system administration, and representative regional users. Sessions will focus on actual workflows, pain points, controls, reporting dependencies, integration needs, and nonfunctional requirements rather than product preference.

### Phase 3 — current-state assessment

We will produce an evidence-linked current-state map covering architecture and data flows, operational resiliency, aging components, scalability, permissions, auditability, reporting dependencies, integrations, security/governance, and user experience. Findings will distinguish observed facts from stakeholder-reported concerns and consultant recommendations.

### Phase 4 — modernization options and roadmap

We will compare at least four decision paths where supported by discovery: optimize/modernize existing applications, replace with a new platform, hybrid modernization, and a no-regrets stabilization/other path. Options will be compared using transparent criteria such as functional fit, migration and integration burden, operating model, security/governance, reporting, change risk, time-to-value, and likely cost drivers. Product names, if useful as market examples, will not substitute for requirements or predetermine a vendor.

### Phase 5 — System Summary and executive readout

The final written deliverable will summarize critical findings, major concerns, the decision matrix, recommended path, sequencing, prerequisites, and next steps. We will review it in a virtual presentation and discussion with RCAP's core team and appropriate stakeholders.
