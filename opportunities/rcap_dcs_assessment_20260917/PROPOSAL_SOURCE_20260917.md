## Executive summary

Token Junkie Labs proposes a four-week, evidence-first assessment of RCAP's custom DCS and surrounding technology ecosystem. The work is intended to give RCAP a decision-ready view of what should be stabilized, modernized, replaced, integrated, or deliberately left alone without steering RCAP toward a preselected CRM vendor.

## What RCAP receives

1. Kickoff, confirmed work plan, stakeholder map and evidence request.
2. Six structured 60-minute virtual stakeholder discovery sessions.
3. Current-state evidence pack covering system context, reporting/data flows, DCS-to-Unanet integration, permissions/auditability, resiliency/scalability, dependencies and evidence gaps.
4. Vendor-neutral modernization option matrix covering optimize/modernize, replacement, hybrid and other evidence-supported paths.
5. System Summary with prioritized findings, risk/effort/sequence, explicit assumptions and a 90-day decision roadmap.
6. 60-minute virtual findings presentation plus one consolidated written clarification cycle.

## Delivery model

Bryce is proposed as project lead and remains directly accountable for discovery synthesis, technical analysis, written assessment and final presentation. Client references and external work samples are not invented; only materials authorized for external disclosure would be provided during clarification.

## Six discovery sessions

1. Core team / kickoff — goals, constraints, known issues, decision context.
2. Programs / operations — business processes, field/program workflows and operational constraints.
3. Data / reporting — reporting, SQL dependence, ownership and data-quality questions.
4. Finance / Unanet — integration, reconciliation and handoffs.
5. IT / security — architecture, support, permissions, auditability and resiliency.
6. Executive / decision owners — priorities, tradeoffs and modernization scoring criteria.

## Schedule

- Week 3 — risk analysis, option design and decision matrix.
- Week 4 — System Summary, 90-day roadmap, findings presentation and clarification cycle.
