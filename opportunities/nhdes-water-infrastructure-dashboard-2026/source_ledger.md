## Source-bound facts

- Scope: a public interactive dashboard communicating New Hampshire water-infrastructure investment through narrative, graphics/widgets, maps, project-level media and filtering/search.
- Funding programs named in the RFP include ARPA, BIL, DWSRF and CWSRF. Initial dashboard scope is current projects plus 2021-present; NHDES states it has roughly 35 years of historical project data that may be added later.
- Addendum 1 states the initial 2021-current data set is approximately **400 funded projects** and that NHDES will provide project narratives and edits/additions to project data.
- Addendum 1 says the dashboard does not have to be hosted in NHDES ArcGIS Online. The selected firm may host it, with an option for NHDES to take over hosting at contract conclusion.
- Contract horizon: five years. Year 1 is initial dashboard + user guide/presentation. Years 2-5 cover maintenance, possible historic-data incorporation and outreach-material updates. Maintenance updates are expected no more than twice yearly.
- Proposal format: one electronic PDF; basic firm info; primary contact; personnel/qualifications; subcontractors; previous interactive-dashboard work; project vision; total/itemized cost and hourly rates; schedule.
- Evaluation weights: **40%** project understanding/vision/methodology/innovation, **25%** relevant firm experience/qualifications, **25%** cost, **10%** schedule.
- Communication restriction: from release through award, communication with NHDES personnel regarding the RFP is prohibited unless approved by the RFP sole point of contact. The inquiry period ended **2026-09-04**. Contract-term/RFP exceptions not raised during that inquiry period are waived under the published RFP.
- Proposal due date in the controlling RFP is **2026-09-25**. Before any submission, recheck the official State bid page for addenda, exact deadline clock/time zone and any changed instructions.
- Submission subject required by the recovered RFP: `NH Water Infrastructure Funding Dashboard RFP Submittal`; recovered RFP identifies `dwsustainability@des.nh.gov` and Kaitlin Curtis as the submission contact. These must be reverified against the current official posting immediately before any real send.
- Proposals must remain valid for 180 days after the submission deadline or until contract effective date, whichever is later.
- Award is contingent on Governor and Executive Council approval and continued appropriation.
