## Task-order evidence seam

- Data integration: source/target counts, transformations, schema lineage, rejects, value reconciliation and interface replay/idempotency.
- Modernization/API/cloud: exact dependency and deployment generations, compatibility, rollback, observability and failure-mode evidence.
- DevSecOps: artifact provenance, dependency/security scanning, secrets boundaries, deployment and recovery receipts.
- Automated testing: functional/regression/integration/performance/accessibility/API/mobile/security evidence.
- AI evaluation: accuracy/reliability/bias/explainability, prompt-injection, data leakage, retrieval/provenance and human-review controls.
- Migration/cutover: retained source generations, exception/retest lineage, rollback thresholds and explicit human acceptance.

No preference points are self-certified by this carrier. The source packet controls those rules.
