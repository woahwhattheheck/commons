# Evidence-first partner profile

A credible teaming route needs a prime that cures the exact clinical/product/commercial gaps rather than merely adding a large logo.

## Prime evidence to require

- production clinical pathology LIMS product with the disciplines required by the buyer;
- referenceable pathology/NHS or demonstrably comparable health-system deployments at hub/spoke scale;
- accountable clinical-safety and regulatory capability;
- migration of complex historical laboratory data with reconciliation evidence;
- EPR/national-system/automation/digital-pathology interfaces;
- security/privacy/service-management posture appropriate to the buyer questionnaire;
- implementation, training, support and long-horizon commercial capacity;
- willingness to name a bounded specialist seam in the future procurement if procurement rules allow it.

## Bounded specialist seam to test with a prime

Only where supported by evidence and buyer rules:

1. deterministic migration reconciliation and exception receipts;
2. interface conformance/replay/idempotency test fixtures across agreed non-production flows;
3. data-lineage/version/change evidence for acceptance;
4. synthetic failure injection for duplicate/stale/missing/out-of-order events;
5. human-controlled release/acceptance gates and reproducible handoff manifests.

These are engineering/evidence services, **not** claims of clinical validation, pathology product ownership, UKAS accreditation, NHS deployment history or release authority.

## Partner qualification questions

- What buyer-required pathology disciplines does your production LIMS support today?
- Which comparable hub/spoke deployments can be referenced and by whom?
- Who owns clinical-safety and regulatory accountability?
- What exact migration volumes/history and reconciliation methods have been delivered?
- Which EPR, national, automation and digital-pathology interfaces are production-proven?
- What questionnaire gates require direct prime evidence vs partner evidence?
- Is the specialist seam allowed at preliminary engagement and later tender stages?
- Who owns pricing, submission, contractual representations and final acceptance?

Until these are answered with evidence, `partner_prime_confirmed` remains false.
