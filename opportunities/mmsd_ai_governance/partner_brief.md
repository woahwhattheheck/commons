# Paid Teaming Brief — MMSD AI Governance RFP

## Commercial intent

This is a **paid procurement pursuit**, not an offer of free advisory work. If Token Junkie Labs / Commons cannot evidence all prime-qualification requirements from the current buyer packet, the preferred path is a commercial subcontract/workshare discussion with a qualified prime.

## Prime profile

A high-fit prime should be able to evidence most or all of:

- public-sector AI governance / technology-policy delivery;
- operational technology, critical infrastructure, water/wastewater, utility or comparable safety-sensitive environment experience;
- public-records / retention / privacy / security policy implementation;
- stakeholder discovery and enterprise technology inventory;
- vendor/procurement governance and incident-response process design;
- staff training/change management;
- required client references, legal standing, insurance and proposal/signature authority;
- capacity to lead the full engagement and accept contractual responsibility.

A public website saying “AI strategy” is not enough. Candidate evidence must be source-linked and checked against current buyer requirements.

## Bounded TJLabs specialist workshare

Subject to prime agreement and buyer requirements, TJLabs can propose a paid specialist scope around:

1. AI inventory/data model and evidence tooling;
2. GenAI + Operational-AI risk taxonomy and control mapping;
3. data-sovereignty / retention / vendor evidence matrices;
4. AI procurement questionnaire and change-notification controls;
5. AI incident-response control design;
6. human-oversight, validation and monitoring requirements;
7. training exercises and governance traceability tooling;
8. reproducible requirement-to-control / control-to-evidence pipeline.

This brief does not claim TJLabs has utility past performance, buyer acceptance, insurance, a partner, approved pricing, a subcontract or submission authority.

## Workshare discussion agenda

A real teaming call should close concrete unknowns:

- who primes and why the prime satisfies mandatory experience/reference gates;
- which proposal sections and deliverables each party owns;
- named staff and availability;
- public-record implications for proprietary material;
- subcontract commercial structure and payment milestones;
- price approval process and travel/expense treatment;
- IP/tooling reuse vs buyer-owned deliverables;
- insurance/legal flow-downs;
- proposal production calendar and review authority;
- who is authorized to communicate with MMSD.

## Outreach rule

Before any send, search the provider history and Slack/GitHub custody by exact candidate organization/contact/RFP identifier. Earlier outreach owns the recipient. A valid message must name the MMSD RFP, make the paid commercial intent explicit, describe the bounded specialist role, ask for a workshare/subcontract discussion, and avoid implying an existing relationship or buyer preference.
