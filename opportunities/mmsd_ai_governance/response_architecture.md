# MMSD RFP Response Architecture — Source-Bound Draft

**Current state: `SOURCE_REFRESH_REQUIRED`.** The buyer page was recently indexed but direct current retrieval returned 404 in the research harness. The following structure is useful for drafting; section names, page limits, pricing format and scored evaluation mapping must be replaced by the current buyer-controlled RFP/addenda/Q&A when acquired.

## 1. Executive fit and outcome

State the buyer problem in public-utility terms: MMSD needs a practical governance system that can cover employee Generative AI and Operational AI without slowing low-risk productivity or weakening safety, records, security, data-sovereignty or accountability controls. Do not claim buyer-specific incidents, architecture, tool inventory, legal conclusions or maturity gaps that are not in buyer evidence.

## 2. Understanding of the operating environment

Demonstrate the distinction between office/knowledge AI and systems that inform or affect treatment/infrastructure. Explain the boundary between policy governance and existing OT safety/change-control authority. Make explicit that policy work will not authorize live operational testing or control changes.

## 3. Methodology

Proposed six-stage delivery:

1. source/authority confirmation + kickoff;
2. stakeholder discovery and AI inventory;
3. risk/data/records/vendor control analysis;
4. policy and operating-model design;
5. validation workshops + implementation/training materials;
6. final policy, control toolkit, roadmap and executive handoff.

Each stage should identify buyer inputs, consultant outputs, acceptance evidence, named decision owner and unresolved dependencies.

## 4. Technical and governance approach

Map the delivery to the companion `delivery_architecture.md`: inventory, risk tiers, human oversight, data sovereignty, public records/retention, procurement/vendor controls, incident response, Operational-AI/OT boundary, training and governance metrics.

## 5. Project management

Show decision cadence, issue/risk log, source/change control, versioned deliverables, stakeholder schedule and a requirement-to-evidence matrix. Any six-month schedule remains provisional until current buyer bytes confirm term and milestones.

## 6. Team and qualifications

Evidence only. For every named person or corporate claim provide an evidence reference. If the current RFP requires public-sector, utility/wastewater, Operational-AI/OT, privacy/records or similar-project references that TJLabs cannot evidence, mark the prime gate failed/hold and route to paid teaming. Never convert adjacent technical experience into a claimed past performance reference.

## 7. References

Populate only after the controlling RFP specifies quantity/format/eligibility. Obtain permission where required. No synthetic clients, no inferred contact information, no claim that an open-source contribution equals a client engagement.

## 8. Commercial response

Use `budget.py` only after pricing structure is known from buyer bytes. Keep labor/workstream arithmetic separate from external price approval. Unknown fee caps, reimbursement rules, travel, taxes, subcontract treatment and invoicing terms remain explicit blockers.

## 9. Compliance and exceptions

Include a compliance matrix keyed to buyer paragraph/page identifiers once the RFP bytes are acquired. Explicitly list every exception/assumption. Public-record status means proposal narrative should avoid unnecessary confidential details and should not promise confidentiality the buyer cannot provide.

## 10. Submission preflight

A response cannot be represented `READY_FOR_OWNER_SUBMISSION_REVIEW` unless all of the following are true:

- current buyer-controlled RFP bytes are captured and hashed;
- addenda and Q&A checks are current;
- proposal deadline is bound to current authoritative buyer evidence;
- every mandatory proposal item has an owner and evidence;
- route-specific PRIME or TEAM qualification gates pass with evidence;
- required legal/insurance/reference facts are verified;
- pricing structure is known, arithmetic is valid and owner approval is recorded;
- signature/submission authority is explicit;
- no required attachment or certification is missing;
- final PDF/content is checked against public-record disclosure implications;
- outreach/partner state is represented literally rather than inferred.
