This is an owner-review structure, not a submitted proposal. Do not add unsupported experience, references, qualifications, outcomes, pricing, certifications, or Boston-community claims.

## 1. Organizational overview and mission — maximum 1 page

- Legal organization name — **OWNER INPUT REQUIRED**
- Mission — **OWNER INPUT REQUIRED**
- Website / basic organization description — **OWNER INPUT REQUIRED**
- Selected service track(s) — **OWNER INPUT REQUIRED**

## 2. Experience and qualifications — maximum 2 pages

For each owner-verified engagement, state the client type, scope, role, delivery evidence and outcome. At least one engagement must substantiate the RFP's public-health / nonprofit / government-funded-program experience requirement.

**Do not invent case studies.** Every included engagement needs an owner-supplied evidence reference.

## 3. Diversity, equity and inclusion approach — maximum 1 page

**OWNER INPUT REQUIRED.** Describe the organization's actual approach and evidence; do not infer policies from general product behavior.

## 4. Selected service track answers — maximum 5 pages per selected track

### Communications
1. Experience developing and implementing communication strategies.
2. Relevant projects and measurable outcomes/metrics.
3. Experience communicating with diverse audiences and communities.
4. Approach to evaluating communication effectiveness.

### Research, Evaluation & Assessment
1. Evaluation/public-health research experience.
2. Research and evaluation methods.
3. Similar projects and results.
4. Dissemination of findings to stakeholders.

### Strategy & Planning
1. Strategic-planning leadership experience.
2. Examples of strategic plans or similar engagements.
3. Stakeholder-engagement approach.
4. Implementation/progress monitoring.

### Grant Writing
1. Competitive grant-application experience and relevant funders.
2. Relevant applications, amount and outcome only where owner-supported.
3. Collaboration with program and subject-matter staff.
4. Management of overlapping deadlines and priorities.

> **Source ambiguity:** the RFP lists four tracks but one instruction says vendors may select one, two, or “all three” tracks. Owner must review this inconsistency before submission.

## 5. Team and staffing — maximum 2 pages

**OWNER INPUT REQUIRED.** Name/role decisions belong to the owner. Include only staff whose participation is confirmed.

## 6. Pricing — maximum 1 page

**OWNER INPUT REQUIRED.** Pricing is not inferred from prior offers or generated automatically. The package cannot commit pricing without explicit owner approval.

## 7. Two professional references — outside page limit

Store only a minimal private reference plan in the owner-controlled completion file. Do not commit personal reference contact details to the repository.
