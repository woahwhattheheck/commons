# Prime-partner evidence profile + response skeleton

## Prime evidence to require

A credible prime should be able to prove, not merely claim:

- a production openEHR-compliant platform or clinical repository product;
- referenceable health-system CDR/openEHR deployments at comparable operational criticality;
- accountable clinical-safety and information-governance capability;
- production healthcare interoperability across the standards and systems the buyer actually names;
- patient identity, terminology, provenance and access-control governance;
- migration/cutover/reconciliation at scale;
- HA/DR, observability, service operations and security evidence;
- five-year-plus commercial/support capacity;
- willingness and procurement ability to incorporate a bounded specialist seam.

Until these are evidenced, `partner_prime_confirmed=false`.

## Bounded specialist seam to test

Where buyer and prime rules permit:

1. interface conformance/replay/idempotency test harnesses;
2. migration reconciliation and exception receipts;
3. lineage/version/change evidence across data transformations;
4. synthetic duplicate/stale/missing/out-of-order event testing;
5. observability assertions tied to acceptance criteria;
6. human-controlled release/acceptance manifests.

These work products do **not** constitute clinical validation, clinical safety ownership or production clinical release authority.
