## Regulated-scope boundary

TJLabs may scope **technical evidence and systems/control implementation support** only after the controlling packet permits it.

TJLabs does not self-authorize:
- statutory audit or review engagements;
- assurance opinions;
- financial-statement certification;
- regulated accounting conclusions;
- tax/legal advice;
- professional sign-off reserved to CPAs/CAs or other licensed professionals;
- representations about Inuit/Nunavut business status;
- procurement certifications owned by a prime.

If the solicitation bundles regulated and technical categories, the responsibility matrix must make the separation explicit. A technical subcontract cannot be used to evade a professional qualification.

## Fail-closed rules

- A secondary procurement index cannot prove eligibility.
- A general consulting capability page cannot prove the prime satisfies this RFP.
- A hash proves byte identity, not truth or completeness.
- Missing evidence is not zero and is not PASS.
- Technical verification is not an audit opinion.
- An internal proposed fee is not accepted buyer pricing.
- No external message is released without fresh collision control and current single-writer authority.
