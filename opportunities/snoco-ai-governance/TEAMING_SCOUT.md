## TJLabs responsibility fence

### Reasonable subcontract claims

TJLabs can credibly pitch a bounded technical-assurance workstream only if a qualified prime owns the enterprise/government-cloud solution:

1. **AI inventory evidence adapter** — normalize model, agent, tool, key and application observations into deterministic records with stable identity/provenance.
2. **Design-vs-live drift receipts** — compare approved policy/control configuration to observed runtime state and emit value-free, tamper-evident deltas.
3. **Incident reconstruction** — correlate actor/tool/model/control events into deterministic, replay-safe timelines without claiming to replace the County SIEM/SOC.
4. **Control conformance testing** — exercise explicit guardrail/allow-block/identity-binding expectations and return exact pass/fail evidence.
5. **Audit/PRA evidence export validation** — prove a supplied export is complete, reproducible, provenance-bound and redaction-safe against prime/County-supplied rules.

### CANNOT_CLAIM / prime-owned responsibilities

Do **not** represent TJLabs as independently qualified for any of the following without new evidence:

- Microsoft 365 GCC/GCC High tenant architecture or licensing authority;
- Entra tenant/RBAC/Conditional Access/PIM implementation ownership;
- Microsoft Purview DLP, records, lifecycle, eDiscovery or information-protection architecture ownership;
- Microsoft enterprise/government-cloud managed services;
- Washington Public Records Act legal interpretation or final disclosure/redaction authority;
- Snohomish County enterprise architecture or cybersecurity prime responsibility;
- county-wide change management/training prime responsibility;
- final AI governance policy authority;
- any mandatory certification, insurance, past-performance, local-office, subcontracting, disadvantaged-business, security-clearance, or contract term not visible in the public notice.
