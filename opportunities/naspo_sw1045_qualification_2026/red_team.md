# Commercial red-team

Reject or repair the package if any condition below occurs.

## Source laundering

- A mirror document/name is described as the controlling RFP.
- A mirror snapshot supplies a mandatory requirement or score used to green a prime route.
- “No addenda” is inferred from silence.
- A digest is invented for bytes not actually acquired.
- The five mirror-discovered filenames are described as the complete packet.

## Category contamination

- Category 1 evidence satisfies a Category 2 requirement without an explicit Category 2 mapping.
- One category's pass is described as qualification for the other.
- Shared technical capability is confused with a buyer minimum.

## Past performance / staffing fiction

- Repository engineering is called public-sector past performance.
- A customer/prospect/logo is called a reference without evidence/permission.
- A person is named or committed without owner authorization.
- Years of experience or staff minima are guessed from biographies.

## Teaming fiction

- A prospect, outbound message, meeting or website is called a committed prime.
- A committed prime is treated as permission to use its references.
- Prime qualification is inferred from company size/reputation rather than evidence.
- A team-ready artifact is described as a buyer-ready proposal while the packet is incomplete.

## Commercial fiction

- Draft labor assumptions become submitted pricing.
- Mirror-described pricing units become the buyer's official model.
- The open RFP is described as award, pipeline value, booked revenue, cash or buyer acceptance.
- “Ready for owner review” is translated into external authority.

## Technical overclaim

- The SW1045 workflow acceptance core is called a production deployment.
- Internal reliability evidence is described as NASPO acceptance.
- Human-approval/idempotency controls are claimed to satisfy unknown buyer security/compliance requirements.

## Required language when facts are missing

Prefer explicit states:

- `HOLD_OFFICIAL_PACKET_REQUIRED`
- `TEAMING_DRAFT_READY_FOR_OWNER_REVIEW`
- `PROSPECTIVE_PRIME_IS_NOT_A_COMMITMENT`
- `NO_AWARD_YET`
- “Mirror discovery only; controlling source not captured.”
