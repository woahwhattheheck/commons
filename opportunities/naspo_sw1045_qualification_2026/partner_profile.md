# Prime-partner profile and commitment gate

## Target prime

Prioritize firms that can prove the pieces this technical seam should not fake:

1. **Public-sector prime history** — externally supportable state/local/higher-education technology engagements and references relevant to the category actually bid.
2. **Cooperative-contract administration** — evidence of running statewide / multi-jurisdiction vehicles, participating addenda, reporting, ordering and account operations.
3. **Category fit** — Consulting depth for Category 1 and/or implementation/managed-services depth for Category 2. Treat them independently.
4. **Named/key personnel bench** — sufficient senior delivery staff once the controlling packet's exact minimums are captured.
5. **Commercial capacity** — insurance, financial, legal, pricing, certifications and contract administration appropriate to a nationwide public-sector Master Agreement.
6. **Security / hosting delivery** — ability to meet confirmed data location, access, incident, privacy and infrastructure terms.
7. **Subcontracting compatibility** — willingness and legal ability to carry a specialist AI/agent subcontractor and include its evidence in the response.

## What is not commitment

None of the following may set `prime_partner.status=COMMITTED`:

- a public logo or partner directory;
- a company website;
- a prior cold message;
- a sent teaming note without acceptance;
- a meeting invitation;
- “interested” language without defined role;
- an agent's inference.

## Evidence for `COMMITTED`

The compiler requires all of:

- opaque organization reference;
- commitment evidence reference + SHA-256;
- at least one supportable public-sector-prime track-record record;
- cooperative-contract administration evidence reference + SHA-256.

Even then, `TEAMING_SUBCONTRACT_READY_FOR_OWNER_REVIEW` requires the controlling packet itself to be acquired. A committed prime cannot cure missing buyer rules by inference.

## Conversation objective if separate outreach authority is ever granted

Do not sell “AI agents” generically. Establish whether the prime needs an evidence-heavy specialist for:

- AI/ML/agent architecture and integration;
- responsible-AI / governance controls;
- model/workflow evaluation;
- provider integration reliability;
- acceptance evidence / replay / reconciliation;
- buyer-specific proof-of-concept or SOW delivery.

The next step should be a bounded workshare against exact RFP/SOW sections, not a vague partnership.
