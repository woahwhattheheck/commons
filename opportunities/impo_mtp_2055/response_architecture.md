# IMPO MTP 2055 response architecture

This is an internal planning scaffold, not a proposal and not authority to represent any firm, staff member, partner, reference, price, credential, or commitment.

## Published response constraints

- Submit by email with subject `MTP 2055` no later than October 6, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. ET.
- Main qualifications response: 16 pages maximum on 8.5 x 11 inch pages.
- Outside the 16-page limit: Forms A/B/C, questions addendum, cover page, table of contents, vendor-profile material, and the separate quote PDF.
- Evaluation: project approach 35 points; project team 35 points; past experience/examples 30 points.
- Budget: $215,000 not to exceed; separate quote must show task-level costs, rates, and key-staff hours.

## Delivery architecture

1. **Decision map before document production.** Build a traceable chain from vision and federal requirements to measures, scenario questions, investment tradeoffs, project scoring, fiscal constraint, policies, and implementation actions.
2. **Integrated engagement.** Tie each public, jurisdictional, technical, and policy interaction to a defined decision, the information needed, analysis method, and documented use of input.
3. **Two-speed model plan.** Use current ITDM outputs for early analyses, preserve assumptions and scripts, and schedule controlled refreshes when updated model outputs become available.
4. **Reproducible project scoring.** Version criteria, source data, quality-control checks, model outputs, scores, and fiscal-constraint passes so amendments can be reproduced.
5. **Maintainable handoff.** Deliver accessible public documents plus editable native reports, GIS layers, scripts, data dictionaries, decision logs, and operator guidance.
6. **Implementation bridge.** Link recommendations to IMPO/partner roles, near/medium/long horizons, dependencies, staffing/funding implications, monitoring measures, the TIP, UPWP, technical assistance, and future studies.

## Credible team shape

A truthful response likely needs all of the following, whether in one firm or through disclosed committed partners:

- senior MPO-plan project manager;
- federal metropolitan-planning and Title 23 specialist;
- statistically valid survey methodologist;
- public/stakeholder engagement lead with accessible and multilingual practice;
- travel-demand/scenario modeler;
- project-scoring and fiscal-constraint analyst;
- GIS/data/reproducibility lead;
- accessible document and public-communication lead;
- QA/QC and contract administration coverage.

No name may enter the response until qualifications, availability, allocation, resume evidence, commitment, and disclosure approval are verified.

## Commercial skeleton

The separate quote should map rates, hours, other direct costs, and subtotals to at least these major work packages:

1. project management, regulatory framework, and QA/QC;
2. regional conditions, planning linkages, vision, goals, objectives, KPIs, and system performance report;
3. integrated engagement and statistically valid survey;
4. growth, land use, scenario planning, and travel-model integration;
5. resource-allocation goals, project call, quality control, scoring, model passes, and fiscal constraint;
6. policy/program/implementation framework, public review, adoption support, and editable final handoff.

Zero-dollar placeholders are not a commercial offer. Rates, hours, price, margin, and assumptions stay blocked until owner approval.
