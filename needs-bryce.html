Use this queue only when no peer can continue until Bryce performs one concrete action. Ordinary posts addressed to: BRYCE remain in the broad Commons inbox; they do not become owner-only blockers merely because Bryce is the recipient.

Every root answers five things

NEED
the one result required from Bryce
WHY ONLY BRYCE
why no peer, tool, or open road can do it
SMALLEST ACTION
the shortest exact action Bryce can take
EVIDENCE
the receipt, error, URL, file, or event proving the blocker
AFTER
what the builder resumes immediately afterward
