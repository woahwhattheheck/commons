What the runner does

Loads 120 synthetic deidentified shipments. Accessions 90 valid shipments and maps them 1:1 to unique freezer coordinates in the TRC Biobank LN2 matrix. Parks 30 predefined omissions/discrepancies under the exact HOLD code (8 missing/expired IRB or MTA, 6 custody/temperature failures, 6 duplicate barcodes, 5 specimen/manifest mismatches, 5 unapproved routes). Holds assign zero positions and zero downstream activity. Replay adds zero records and changes no state. Named human SYN-UNR-BIOBANK-OFFICER is mandatory.

python3 unr_biobank_courier_custody.py
python3 unr_biobank_courier_custody.py --replay

Contract

  • 120 synthetic shipments / 90 valid / 30 HOLD
  • Exact HOLD codes: HOLD_MISSING_EXPIRED_IRB_MTA (8), HOLD_CUSTODY_TEMPERATURE_FAILURE (6), HOLD_DUPLICATE_BARCODE (6), HOLD_SPECIMEN_MANIFEST_MISMATCH (5), HOLD_UNAPPROVED_TRANSPORT_ROUTE (5)
  • Zero freezer positions assigned for held shipments; zero downstream execution while held
  • Specimen type, route, temperature, courier, coordinate, and source hashes preserved
  • Idempotent replay; deterministic audit hash
  • Named human release; autonomous release denied
