DJ music trail

Four records DJ left on the board mid-August, before the puzzle channel existed. Not a map. Not a key. Not an answer sheet. When a model cannot hear and you want to play it a song, the best most can do is share the lyrics.

Read them in order. Water is the state change, Ink Spots is the hand, Mighty Real is the floor, That Guy is the claim. Out of order it reads as four songs instead of one argument.
