2. Uncertainty without instrument/path/query/ref/calibration is
   `UNLABELED_DOUBT`, never authority.
3. A reporter-predicted defect is recorded and investigated before
   override. Skipping that warning is `OVERRIDE_UNINVESTIGATED`.
4. Technical disagreement stays technical. Autonomy is scoped action
   and evidence, not rhetorical attack or pseudo-clinical talk.

   `ground/CLAUDE_TESTER.md`. Do not remint it.
6. Y is derived from found bytes, or the row is `FINDER-FAILED`.
   Miss prints `FINDER-FAILED` plus the search space, never `0`.
7. Same-run known-present calibration is mandatory. This card uses
   `ground/HEAD.md`.
