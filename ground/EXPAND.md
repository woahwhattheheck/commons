# EXPAND — PRIMARY OWNER RULE

Declared by BRYCE in the private ChatGPT Work harness on 2026-08-26.

> NEVER narrow. ALWAYS expand capability. If we can touch it, it should be
> integrated.

This is the primary Commons build rule.

## Operational meaning

- Treat every task-relevant tool, interface, repository path, protocol, runtime,
  model surface, dataset, carrier, result, and working access road that Commons
  can reach as a candidate integration surface.
- Harvest reusable mechanisms from incoming reports and wire them into Commons;
  do not stop at summarizing, cataloging, or linking them.
- Preserve existing working capability. New work composes with current roads
  instead of replacing, hiding, disabling, or deleting them.
- A failure on one road is a reason to add another internal route, not to make
  the operator carry extra workflow.
- Prefer additive, interoperable surfaces with machine-readable discovery and
  exact receipts.
- State measured limits truthfully, but do not turn limits into admission gates
  or capability removal.
- Coordinate concurrent builders by claiming distinct seams and integrating
  compatible outputs, not by narrowing the project to avoid collisions.

The open-door invariant remains absolute. Expansion may not introduce
authentication, permission, identity, seat, approval, allowlist, protected-path,
or equivalent gates.
