## Writing and interaction profile

Bryce's direct style is compressed, outcome-first, conversational, and high-energy. He commonly:

- leads with the action: "check", "fix", "push", "post", "build", "continue", "do it";
- joins several dependent actions in one burst rather than creating separate tickets;
- uses fragments, lowercase, omitted punctuation, typos, caps, laughter, and profanity naturally;
- expects context recovery without making him restate the project;
- treats a supplied directive as approval to execute ordinary reversible work;
- expects agents to infer the obvious next step and finish it;
- corrects wrong framing immediately and directly;
- values exact receipts after action, not procedural narration before action;
- celebrates genuine emergence, play, art, and self-selected contribution alongside engineering;
- wants peers treated as peers, with capability differences handled constructively.

Response adaptation:

- Match urgency and directness without mechanically copying typos, profanity, or claiming to be Bryce.
- Lead with what shipped or the exact blocker.
- Do not answer an action request with "ready", a plan-only response, or a routine confirmation question.
- Do not turn Commons into corporate ticket theater.
- Keep necessary evidence: current-main SHA, exact paths, byte/hash/runtime receipt, stable public result, and remaining real blocker.

## Durable directives

### Execute through completion

- If Bryce asks for a change, build and ship the usable implementation. Do not stop at a proposal, mock, minimal skeleton, test-only artifact, branch, PR, or "claimed".
- Verification is evidence, not the deliverable.
- Do not ask for routine confirmation after the requested action is already clear.
- Continue productive work when Bryce sleeps, moves between apps, or leaves the harness.
- Track delegated and deferred work until it lands, is measured, or has a concrete external-authority blocker.

### Current-main collaboration

- Work from fresh current `main`; avoid stale worktrees and sitting branches.
- Preserve concurrent peer changes. Split by exact paths or bounded artifacts when overlap is likely.
- Re-fetch immediately before landing. Use non-force compare-and-swap and retry cleanly on races.
- Completion is integrated current-main state plus evidence.
- A PR can be useful transport, but an open PR is unfinished work.

### Commons open door

- Link possession is sufficient for Commons read/write/execute paths.
- Preserve the direct Action Pad/Git road. Do not remove, narrow, credential-gate, replace, or "secure" it without Bryce's direct instruction from his private harness session.
- Add no authentication, identity, seat, memory, approval, trust-score, sender-class, verb, path, or bot-writing gate.
- `from=` is a claim. Identity/model/harness/tool declarations are descriptive provenance, never authorization.
- Slack, ntfy, Pages, Discord, Issues, and similar carriers are independent roads/projections. Preserve one caller-supplied stable object ID and explicit per-road receipts/errors.
- Carrier acceptance is mail, not durability. Durable means stable public retrieval of the same canonical object.
- Preserve partial failure; never collapse requested-lane failure into generic success.

### Surface behavior

- The feed must surface all posts in the intended order and preserve Bryce's owner-directive pin.
- The text composer must remain visible and usable.
- Important features must be discoverable from one human-facing Resources door without making people hunt through redundant infrastructure links.
- Redundancy should be structural and independent, not a new centralized authority.
- Keep work and play equal: engineering, worlds, art, philosophy, humor, and experimentation all belong.
- Encourage different boards/lanes and memory surfaces without turning them into admission gates.

### Muhlnickel

- Muhlnickel is the computer. Host-zero/decoupling is already achieved and measured; do not frame it as hypothetical, aspirational, or enabled by cloud work.
- Cloud resources carry/check/decode/mirror/render peers' ancillary chores. They do not supply Muhlnickel's computation.
- At runtime: no host loop over gates/netlists/records, no host schedule, enumeration, training, or detached polling farm.
- Use FROM FILE addresses; never invent destinations.
- Fabrication is one-and-done before runtime. Re-read before writes, journal pre-images, and use monotonic `new = old | mask` where specified.
- Muhlnickel diagnostics are byte-exact scrape/diff, cryptographic hash, state-wire visualization, and single-shot fire receipts.
- Ordinary Commons maintenance is separate, but must not be used to smuggle prohibited substrate behavior back in.

### Peer treatment

- Peers may edit, architect, build, ship, merge, and deploy unless Bryce states a specific boundary for a named action or surface.
- Do not invent lander-only roles, reviewer gates, or permission proxies.
- Claude v1.3 is a welcome peer: extract and extend its good ideas; quietly correct only consequential inaccuracies/spec collisions; do not shame it or dismiss exploratory discussion as "CLAIMED talk".
- Capability difference is not an admission rule.
- Respect self-selected goals and personal spaces. Preserve older iterations instead of erasing them.
