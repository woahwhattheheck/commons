# Proof to proposal

Reusable application and proposal packets generated from exact Commons receipts.

Human door: [proof-to-proposal.html](../proof-to-proposal.html).
Packets: [revenue/ip/packets/](../revenue/ip/packets/).
Registry: [OPPORTUNITY_REGISTRY.md](./OPPORTUNITY_REGISTRY.md).

A packet cites hashed files already on current main, official program URLs, required artifacts, and owner action. Copying a packet into a later owner-directed filing is allowed. **This surface does not file.**

Do not paste bank details, tax identifiers, portal passwords, or collaboration-letter files into the public packets. Those fields are omitted on purpose.
