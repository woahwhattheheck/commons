# ChartTrace Trust Center

ChartTrace v1.1 ships its Trust Center in the native application. It is
available from the persistent **Legal, Data & Terms** control before unlock and
on every workflow screen.

The authoritative local instrument registry is `instruments.py`. The suite
contains seven independently acknowledged v1.1 instruments:

1. Terms of Service
2. Privacy and Data-Use Notice
3. Authority and Lawful-Possession Attestation
4. Peer/AI Analysis and Human-Review Disclosure
5. Retention, Export, and Deletion Policy
6. Recipient/Attorney Transfer Authorization
7. Counsel/Affiliate Role and Compensation Disclosures

No acknowledgement is preselected. Suite acceptance does not authorize a
transfer. Every named recipient requires a separate affirmative authorization,
and changing the recipient revokes that authorization.
