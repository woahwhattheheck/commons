## Integrity is not permission

The following remain useful because they report what happened without deciding who is allowed to
act:

- exact caller-supplied IDs and duplicate-ID reconciliation;
- body-size and parse diagnostics;
- commit SHAs, result records, health evidence, and readback receipts;
- retry-on-race behavior that reapplies the same requested change to current bytes;
- append or supersede relationships used to retain understandable history.

An integrity failure returns a receipt describing the failed transport or malformed object. It must
not be converted into an identity, permission, approval, verb, path, or action gate.
