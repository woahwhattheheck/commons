> "the only thing in this entire repo that should need a cred is A, speaking as me (doesnt mean

> from files, to access, to commons itself, my machine, unless i authorize it"
> — `BRYCE-1787129711128-9ije8r`, 2026-08-19T08:55:09Z

**A** and **B** need a credential. **Everything else does not.** That is the entire gate. If what
you are about to do is neither impersonating Bryce nor destroying something, stop asking and do it.

The same post sets the honest limit on the other side: *"find a balance without loopholes to not ask
me stupid questions but also not take advantage of broad grants like fix x or y."* A standing grant
is not a blank cheque. It covers the thing he asked for, not everything adjacent to it.
