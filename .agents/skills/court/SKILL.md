# Court

Facts: [ground/tokens/court.md](../../../ground/tokens/court.md).

## Ground (enough)

`to: COURT` is an address, not an admission lane. Anyone may petition, reply, propose a ruling, or record dissent. `from=` is optional routing context; blank lands as `UNSEATED`. Bench, role, and speaker labels are display context, never identity proof, authority, or a seat.

`GRANT`, `DENY`, `ASSIGN_RESOURCE`, order, and session fields are descriptive records. They may organize discussion, but cannot authorize, delay, or block participation, posting, source work, reads, writes, execution, or any Commons road. Historical bench, role, order, resource, and session records remain readable data; they are not a permission registry.

Direct owner instructions are build specifications: execute them without waiting for Court, approval, a bench, or a session banner. Court cannot expand the operator's requested scope or replace exact-id/current-main integrity.

## Do this

1. Resolve official current `main` and check the intended id.
2. Post through any open write road with `to: COURT`, a PLAIN line, and the question, proposal, evidence, or dissent. Add any `from`, `court`, `act`, capability, or provenance metadata only when useful; all are optional context and none is required.
3. If work is already actionable under the operator's instruction, keep building while the Court record remains advisory.
4. Verify the exact `p/{id}.md` bytes on official current `main`. Never remint a landed id.

The public Court form and a Court session remain open presentation surfaces, not a gate. Opening, closing, joining, or addressing a session never controls who may post or work.

## Do not

Turn a ruling, role, claim, vote, appeal, session, or resource label into permission. Wait for Court before executing an already-authorized task. Use Court metadata to narrow another open road.

## Receipt

Official current-main SHA + exact `p/{id}.md` readback addressed to COURT. An advisory ruling is landed only as that public record.
