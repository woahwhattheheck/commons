## Executive finding

The current public sponsor/platform materials conflict on whether Phase I requires a Raman spectrum generated by Blaise hardware.

- **HeroX Phase I page:** explicitly lists an Entry Requirement that all entries must “submit valid Raman spectra generated by the Blaise spectrometer.”
- **Forward Edge-AI Competition Guidelines, Section 10:** its current Entry Requirements list a Blaise application, clear technical feasibility, meaningful impact, and scalable business/research pathway, but do **not** list a Phase I spectra-upload requirement.
- **Forward Edge-AI Guidelines, Section 5:** Phase I says teams submit a **pitch and concept video**; Phase II begins only after selected applicants receive developer kits.
- **Forward Edge-AI Pitch Template:** says all competition entries begin with a completed one-page PDF and short video; it gives no Raman-spectrum upload field in the published template.

These statements cannot safely be collapsed into “hardware is not required.” The prudent submission state is **BLOCKED ON SOURCE CLARIFICATION / PORTAL VERIFICATION** for the spectra requirement.

## Source-by-source reading

### 1. HeroX — spectra required

The live HeroX overview calls this “Phase I: Idea Competition” and says entrants submit a one-page pitch and video. Separately, its Entry Requirements say all entries must:

1. propose an application built on the Blaise Raman spectrometer;
2. demonstrate clear technical feasibility;
3. address meaningful impact;
4. **submit valid Raman spectra generated by the Blaise spectrometer**;
5. present a scalable business or research pathway.

The same page says the top 100 advance to Phase II and the top 10 receive a $5,000 cash prize plus a developer kit.

### 2. Forward Edge-AI Guidelines — Phase I pitch/video, no spectra item in Section 10

The current Guidelines say Phase I starts August 1, 2026, lasts three months, and teams submit a pitch and concept video. They say the top 100 advance and the top 10 receive a developer kit and $5,000 cash prize. Section 6 describes the developer kit as including the Blaise device and SDK. Section 5 says Phase II begins after all 100 kits reach selected applicants.

Current Section 10 Entry Requirements list only:

- propose a Blaise Raman spectroscopy application;
- demonstrate clear technical feasibility;
- address a meaningful impact to humanity;
- present a scalable business or research pathway.

The valid-Blaise-spectrum requirement shown on HeroX is not present in the current Section 10 list.

### 3. Forward Edge-AI Pitch Template — PDF + video only in published form

The live pitch template says all competition entries begin with a completed pitch form in PDF format and a short video. It requires one printed page and a video no longer than three minutes. Its published fields cover project overview, problem/solution, uniqueness, impact, technical approach, team readiness, metrics, commercialization, contact information, and a video URL. No Raman-spectrum upload field appears in the public template.

## Phase I prize-payment precision

Official materials advertise ten Phase I awards of **$5,000 cash each** plus a free developer kit. The FAQ characterizes prize awards as one-time cash payments, and the agreement says completed-phase prizes remain payable subject to applicable award conditions if a later phase is cancelled. The public materials reviewed in this pass do **not** establish a specific Phase I payout date, payment rail, tax-document workflow, or guaranteed number of days after winner notification.

Therefore do not represent payout timing or mechanics as verified.
