## Classifications and evidence levels

**BUILD** means a bounded internal engineering or design contribution can be carried by the named working lane. It does not mean the complete buyer capability has been implemented, accepted, staffed or priced. **PARTNER** means the production platform, integration access, operational responsibility or domain authority must be independently established with a proposed prime/provider. No partner is appointed by that label. **CANNOT_CLAIM** means the assertion is unsupported by the present evidence, not permanently impossible.

Evidence levels are deliberately separate: **INSPECTED_SOURCE** is code/document inspection; **BUILDER_EXECUTION** is the attributed S4 record; **PLANNED_DESIGN** is proposed or in-flight work; **EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_MISSING** means the required outside observation was not supplied. These labels do not become interchangeable because a document digest is accepted by the compiler.

The source references S1–S6 are fully bound in SOURCE_REGISTER.md. Working owner names below reflect S6 coordination only; they are not an employment, availability or delivery commitment.

## What a reviewer can accept without overstating completion

For **R01**, a reviewer may accept that the cited implementation replays supplied manifests and that S4 records the builder's tests. The reviewer must not use that acceptance to close R02's live import and usability evidence. For **R06/R08**, accepting a design or receipt contract does not close R07/R09's actual interoperability. For **R04/R14/R15**, an approved proposed case remains unexecuted until a named application version is exercised and its outcome retained.

For **R20**, all twenty-two rows retain unresolved controlling-source authority even when their engineering classification is useful. For **R21**, absence of evidence is recorded per condition rather than turned into a global impossibility claim. For **R22**, an internal delivery milestone may be recorded accurately while contract, buyer and money states remain unestablished.

## Changes to this ledger

A revision should name the row, old and new classification, reason, exact new evidence, reviewer, source version and affected downstream design. Preserve the former row and its source binding in version history. Do not silently replace the historical source summary with a current-verified label, convert a planned scenario into a test pass, or reassign a working lane based only on a stale channel excerpt.
