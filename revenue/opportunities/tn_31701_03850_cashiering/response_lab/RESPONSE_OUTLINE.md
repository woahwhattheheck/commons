# 20-page response architecture — internal working outline

Hard constraints retained from the RFI: maximum 20 pages, minimum 12-point text, English, numbered pages, Word or PDF, no embedded external landing-page links, and numbered answers matching the Technical and Cost forms.

| Pages | Content | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover, legal entity/contact, response truth statement | Prime/OEM |
| 2 | Technical 3: comparable deployments and installed-product experience | Prime/OEM evidence |
| 3–4 | Technical 4: implementation approach, phases, timeline, governance | Joint; TJLabs specialist workshare |
| 5 | Technical 5–6: cooperative purchasing; product/workflow/interface material rendered inline | Prime/OEM |
| 6–7 | Technical 7–8: UAT/training and no-disruption rollout/cutover/rollback | Joint; TJLabs lead on acceptance evidence |
| 8 | Technical 9–10: maintenance/support and SLA standards | Prime/OEM evidence |
| 9 | Technical 11: historical uptime and availability evidence | Prime/OEM evidence only |
| 10 | Technical 12–13: release management, support partners, sustainability | Prime/OEM |
| 11 | Technical 14: differentiators tied to Attachment 1 | Joint, evidence bounded |
| 12 | Technical 15: RTO/RPO, immutable backup, redundancy and DR-test evidence | Prime/OEM evidence only |
| 13–14 | Attachment 1 crosswalk summary: audit/security, compatibility, interfaces | Joint; exact row appendix maintained internally |
| 15–16 | Attachment 1 crosswalk summary: reporting, documents, cashiering functions | Joint; acceptance-lab examples clearly synthetic |
| 17 | Attachment 1 crosswalk summary: accounting/posting/reconciliation | TJLabs specialist narrative + prime product contract |
| 18 | Cost 1–4: pricing units, implementation range/model, support tiers | Prime commercial owner; TJLabs workshare separately scoped |
| 19 | Additional considerations: phased pilot, evidence-driven migration, reconciliation controls | Joint |
| 20 | Assumptions, dependencies, evidence gaps, no-claim statement | Joint |

Do not spend pages reproducing screenshots or generic marketing. Every strong claim should either point to prime/OEM retained evidence or be explicitly described as a proposed implementation method. The synthetic acceptance lab demonstrates control behavior only; it is not evidence of Tennessee acceptance, product certification, production uptime, PCI/SOC status, hardware support or a deployed statewide cashiering system.
