# Go / no-go checklist

Proceed toward an external response only when a qualified prime/OEM can evidence every hard gate below.

- Legal respondent identity and an accountable response owner.
- Product fit for statewide cashiering, including cash/check/card/ACH and batch/deposit/accounting workflows.
- Current SOC 2 Type II applicable to the proposed environment.
- PCI DSS v4.0.1 responsibility model and current AOC where applicable.
- Continental-US production, backup and DR data boundary; US-based access to State data.
- Hardware/payment-processor/Check21 approach supportable with named products and interfaces.
- Historical uptime/SLA and current support/maintenance model.
- RTO/RPO, backup immutability/redundancy and recent DR-test evidence.
- ADA/WCAG evidence and supported-browser/platform statement.
- Credible State ERP integration contract plus migration and UAT staffing assumptions.
- Product and implementation pricing owner willing to answer Cost 1–4.
- Single consolidated question set reconciled against any State addendum before the 2026-09-25 2:00 PM CT deadline.
- Final response held to 20 pages / 12-point minimum / numbered answers / no external landing-page links before 2026-10-05 2:00 PM CT.

If any certification, installed-product, uptime or hardware gate is unavailable, keep the posture partner-first or do not submit; do not convert a synthetic demo into compliance evidence. The RFI itself states response costs are not reimbursed and that a response creates no contract rights.
