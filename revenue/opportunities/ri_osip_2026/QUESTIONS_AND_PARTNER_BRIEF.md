## Target-screening rubric

This is a research rubric, not a list of qualified firms. Score only after inspecting dated primary evidence; absence of evidence remains unknown.

| Dimension | Evidence to seek | Record as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prime eligibility | Firm-provided and official documentation addressing every minimum qualification in the dossier. | Supported / unresolved / contradicted, with source and date. |
| Relevant delivery | Identifiable public-sector pool engagements, assigned staff and operational responsibilities. | Verified relevance; avoid inferring capability from a generic service page. |
| Actual workshare need | Bid lead identifies an unfilled reconciliation/reporting requirement. | Named gap and accountable owner, or no demonstrated fit. |
