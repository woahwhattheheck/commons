## Workshare admission to a proposal

Before naming TokenJunkieLabs in H5, the prime and proposed subcontractor must establish the exact legal parties, work description, evidence of capability, staffing, data-access arrangements, dependencies, acceptance criteria and commercial terms. This dossier records a candidate role, not an existing teaming relationship.

Candidate work is limited to read-only data intake, lineage, comparison against prime-approved calculations, exception documentation, draft reporting and audit-evidence preparation. The prime supplies authoritative inputs and calculation policies. The prime or its separately appointed professionals retain fund management, custody, participant transaction execution, investment advice, tax/legal conclusions, audit opinions, accounting approval and report release.

An intake must define how a rejected source, ambiguous identifier, duplicate record, incomplete interval or unreconciled total is returned for prime review. It must distinguish a candidate computation from a booked balance. Acceptance should require the prime to reconcile selected outputs to authoritative records and resolve exceptions; a visually complete report is insufficient evidence of correctness. No production access or assurance level is claimed here.
