## How to use the map

The labels allocate proposed preparation work, not eligibility or delivery authority:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PRIME | Respondent supplies, owns, and approves the answer or performance obligation. |
| TJLABS-WORKSHARE-CANDIDATE | Potential read-only evidence, reconciliation, or reporting support for a qualified prime. Scope and capability remain to be established; prime retains accountability. |
| THIRD-PARTY | Evidence or professional output expected from a separately identified auditor, custodian, or specialist; prime remains responsible for the response. |
| HOLD | Allocation or a consequential commitment needs prime/specialist resolution before an answer can be offered. |

For compact tables, **P = PRIME; T = TJLABS-WORKSHARE-CANDIDATE; X = THIRD-PARTY; H = HOLD**. These are ownership classifications. The separate evidence status of every row begins **HOLD / NOT ASSESSED**, including rows marked P, T, or X. A proposed subcontractor cannot cure a respondent qualification gap by implication.
