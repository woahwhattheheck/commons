2. **Configured acceptance pack.** Use the working offline engine with owner/prime-approved comparison policy and a retained mixed corpus: known legitimate partial invoices, known duplicates, receipts already consumed by earlier invoices, unit/currency mismatches, tax/header differences, and changed source generations.
3. **Exception review and repair validation.** Deliver every submitted row's disposition, explanatory code, input digest and affected capacity group. Separate factual export defects from policy differences. Re-run against corrected retained inputs instead of overwriting the earlier report.
4. **Handoff.** Deliver the runnable command, schema, reproducible fixtures, regression suite, versioned evidence manifests and an operator guide. Acceptance is based on agreed expected outcomes and conserved source mappings, not the count of green tests alone.
