## What the companion adds

Each import probe uses a fresh child interpreter. Synthetic generic-name sentinels are installed only inside those children; production may neither overwrite them nor expose aliases under those names. The companion performs no network requests and creates its intentionally damaged modules and output collections only in temporary directories.
