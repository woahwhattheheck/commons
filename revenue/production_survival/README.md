# Production survival offer

This lane adds the missing first rung beneath the existing USD 12,000
GGUF diagnostic and USD 30,000 White Box engagement. It replaces
nothing.

The buyer starts with one non-confidential sentence:

> My agent should [outcome], but in production it [failure].

USD 2,500 buys one provider-hosted, no-login working proof in one
agreed business day. The proof includes a happy path, a stop or failure
path, a rollback/reset path, a durable receipt, and a written
keep/change/stop handoff. The buyer does not need to provide a GGUF,
runnable harness, NDA, repository access, credentials, or private data
for this entry SKU.

The public buyer page is [`agent-rescue.html`](../../agent-rescue.html).
Canonical terms are in [`offer.json`](./offer.json).

The page does not collect payment or private input. The public sender
and reply route is `tokenjunkielabs@gmail.com`. Payment, signatures,
private-system access, and any production integration stay in official
private systems after written acceptance.

## Positioning rule

Sell the outcome: an agent workflow that survives a real failure and
can be rolled back with a receipt. Do not lead with “MCP server,”
“agent framework,” “prompt engineering,” or another implementation
noun.

## Evidence boundary

The market numbers on the page come from primary publisher pages in
`offer.json`. The page deliberately does not repeat the weakly sourced
“89% of pilots fail” headline. Commons itself is the live demonstration;
it is not represented as customer proof or collected revenue.
