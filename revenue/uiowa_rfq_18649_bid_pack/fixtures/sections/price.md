Pricing is fixed-fee by phase, with the rate basis carried in the certified cost rate
schedule rather than restated in the body. The fee schedule covers the four phases in
[[S-SCOPE]] and the two readouts.

Expenses for onsite days are quoted separately and are not included in the phase fees. No
onsite day is assumed in the base fee; onsite days are priced only if requested.

The certified cost rate schedule is [[ATT-FIN-02]]. Our audited financial capacity evidence
is [[ATT-FIN-01]].
