The engagement runs in four phases. Phase one is intake: we collect the inventory of
applications and infrastructure services in scope, together with whatever operational
records already exist - change records, incident records, backup and restoration records,
and any existing architecture documentation. We do not require these to be complete. We
require them to be labelled, so that a gap is recorded as a gap.

Phase two is interviews. We speak with the service owners for each in-scope area. Where a
service owner is unavailable within the engagement window, the area is recorded as not
assessed and the reason is named in the report. We do not infer an answer from an adjacent
team and attribute it to the area we could not reach.

Phase three is analysis. Each finding is written with its source attached: the document, the
record, or the named interview it came from. A finding with no traceable source does not
enter the register.

Phase four is reporting and readout. The deliverable set is the written report, the
recommendation register, and the readout deck. The register orders recommendations by the
stated priority basis and shows the weights used, so the ordering can be argued with rather
than merely received.
