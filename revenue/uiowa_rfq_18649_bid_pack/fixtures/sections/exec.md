This proposal offers a four-area assessment - applications, infrastructure, operational
practice, and current AI use - delivered over an eight-week engagement with two scheduled
readouts. The assessment produces findings that are traceable to a named source, and a
recommendation register in which every recommendation cites the findings that motivated it.

We do not produce a single maturity score. Where evidence for an area was not collected,
the deliverable records that the area was not assessed. An area that nobody was asked about
is reported as not assessed; it is not reported as a low score. This is the difference
between an assessment and a spreadsheet that resembles one, and it is the reason our
reports survive a second reading six months later.
