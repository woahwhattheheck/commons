## Why this is strategically attractive

This is not a generic “write us an AI policy” engagement. MMSD expressly joins GenAI governance with **Operational AI in a critical wastewater utility**. A strong response can differentiate by treating policy as an executable operating system rather than a principles memo:

1. inventory every AI-enabled use/vendor/embedded function;
2. classify each use by operational, data, legal, safety and autonomy risk;
3. bind each tier to allowed data, human authority, logging, review and incident rules;
4. make public-record discoverability/retention an explicit design constraint;
5. create procurement controls for vendor data handling, model changes, subcontractors and incident notices;
6. ship practical employee guidance and scenario-based training; and
7. leave the District with repeatable registers, assessments and review cadences rather than consultant dependency.
