## Dormant TJLabs technical workstream

If a future **distinct** solicitation has materially similar needs, this prior design can be used
as a starting donor after fresh qualification.

### 1. AI inventory and evidence architecture

A practical schema and review method for supplied AI use cases/systems covering owner/process,
AI category, data class, external-provider handling, action/autonomy surface, record/retention
status, vendor/model identity, human override, fallback, monitoring, incident and validation
evidence.

The shipped buyer-neutral governance engine analyzes supplied rows only and does not claim
enterprise-inventory completeness.

### 2. Operational + agentic risk design

Technical criteria separating administrative GenAI from systems that can influence treatment,
infrastructure, enterprise actions or other operational outcomes:

- information/data risk;
- operational/physical consequence;
- autonomy/tool authority;
- recoverability/manual fallback;
- monitoring/validation;
- vendor/model drift;
- records/evidence preservation.

### 3. Vendor AI and data-sovereignty controls

A reusable questionnaire/control matrix for retention/training use, telemetry/support access,
subprocessors, residency, deletion/export, security/incident notification, model/feature changes,
audit evidence, termination/exit and integration privileges.

### 4. Action-authority and incident controls

Control language distinguishing suggest, draft/prepare, bounded execution, and nondelegable
named-human approval, with rollback/fallback evidence for data leakage, unauthorized action,
model drift/change and unsafe outputs.

### 5. Scenario validation

Structured technical table-top scenarios producing system facts, proposed tier, required
controls, human decision points, record requirements, incident/rollback routes and unresolved
owner/counsel decisions.

### 6. Technical policy traceability

One technical-policy review producing a control-traceability matrix across inventory, risk,
vendor and incident artifacts.
