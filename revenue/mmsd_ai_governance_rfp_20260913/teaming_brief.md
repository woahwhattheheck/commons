Use only if PRIME qualification remains unproven or a stronger public-sector consulting prime improves win probability. This is an internal partner brief, **not** authorization to contact anyone.

The official page sets proposals due 2026-10-16 16:00 CT. A current procurement index reports a six-month engagement and questions due 2026-09-28 16:00 CT; verify those details from the official PDF before reliance.

## What we can contribute without pretending to be the prime

A bounded **Operational + Agentic AI Governance Workstream** that plugs into a prime’s public-sector governance/change-management engagement:

### 1. AI inventory evidence model
A normalized register for use case, system/model/vendor, owner, input data class, outputs/actions, integrations, autonomy, record creation, vendor data use, operational dependency, fallback and current controls.

### 2. Operational-AI risk tiers
Risk criteria that add process/safety/availability, recoverability/manual fallback, model drift, change control and operator authority to normal GenAI privacy/security concerns.

### 3. Tool/action authority controls
A clear distinction between:

- AI may **suggest**;
- AI may **prepare** an action;
- AI may **execute** within bounded delegated authority; and
- actions that always require named human approval / are nondelegable.

### 4. Audit / evidence requirements
For material/high-risk uses: input/source provenance where feasible, model/system/version, tool/action logs, human approval, result/exception, incident preservation, material-change review and repeatable closeout evidence.

### 5. Vendor AI/data-sovereignty control set
Questions/contract requirements for retention, training use, telemetry, support access, subprocessors, residency, deletion/export, model changes, security incidents, audit evidence and termination/exit.

### 6. Scenario validation
Tabletops that cover ordinary GenAI and wastewater/operational cases so policy choices are tested before adoption.
