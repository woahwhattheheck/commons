# MMSD procurement question set

**Do not send automatically.** First verify the official PDF and addenda; delete any question already answered there. If the reported 2026-09-28 16:00 CT question deadline is confirmed, prioritize only questions that materially change bid/no-bid, scope, price, or legal/technical architecture.

## Priority 0 — qualification / bid mechanics

1. **Minimum qualifications and references.** Please confirm any minimum firm-experience requirements and the required number, recency, and type of comparable references, including whether public-sector / utility references are mandatory or simply evaluated.
2. **Teaming.** May a respondent use named subcontractors/subconsultants to satisfy specialized technical portions of the scope, and if so which qualifications/references must be held by the prime versus the team collectively?
3. **Required forms and signatures.** Please identify every mandatory form/certification/signature required with the proposal and whether electronic signatures are accepted.
4. **Insurance timing.** Must respondents evidence insurance limits with the proposal, at notice of intent to award, or only before contract execution?
5. **Pricing form.** Does MMSD prefer or require a fixed total fee, phase-based fixed fees, hourly not-to-exceed pricing, or another format, and should travel be included?

## Priority 1 — scope boundaries that materially affect effort

6. **Inventory population.** Approximately how many departments, business applications, vendors, embedded/operational AI systems, and staff interviews should respondents assume for the shadow/embedded-AI inventory?
7. **Operational technology boundary.** Does the engagement include review of AI/ML embedded in SCADA, process-control, laboratory, condition-monitoring, or vendor-managed treatment/infrastructure systems, or should the policy remain at a governance/control-design level without technical validation of those systems?
8. **Data-flow depth.** Is MMSD seeking a high-level data classification/data-flow inventory for AI use cases, or detailed technical mapping of model endpoints, subprocessors, residency, retention, training-use, telemetry, and vendor support access?
9. **Existing artifacts.** What existing IT/security, records-retention, acceptable-use, procurement, incident-response, data classification, vendor-risk, and operational-technology policies should the selected team harmonize rather than duplicate?
10. **Legal review.** Will MMSD counsel/records custodians provide authoritative interpretation and final approval for Wisconsin Public Records Law, retention, privilege/confidentiality, and disclosure language developed during the engagement?
11. **Incident framework.** Should the final work include executable incident playbooks/tabletop scenarios for AI events (data leakage, hallucinated public communication, unauthorized autonomous action, model/vendor change, operational anomaly), or policy-level incident requirements only?
12. **Procurement controls.** Is the vendor framework expected to include model/data-use clauses, subprocessors, residency, retention/deletion, security notification, material model-change notices, audit rights, exit/export, and human-override requirements?

## Priority 2 — adoption / deliverable acceptance

13. **Training audience.** What staff populations and approximate headcount should training cover, and is role-specific training expected for executives, general staff, IT/security, records custodians, procurement, engineering/operations, and laboratory personnel?
14. **Training format.** Should bidders price live sessions, train-the-trainer materials, recordings, scenario exercises, job aids, or some combination?
15. **Governance operating model.** Does MMSD expect the consultant to recommend a standing AI governance committee / accountable executive / use-case approval workflow, and should the team help pilot that process during the engagement?
16. **Risk tiers.** Does MMSD have an existing enterprise risk taxonomy that AI tiers must inherit, or may the consultant propose a dedicated tiering system mapped back to existing security/safety/records controls?
17. **Acceptance.** What constitutes acceptance of the final policy and implementation toolkit—Commission approval, executive approval, legal review, stakeholder signoff, staff training completion, or another milestone?
18. **Post-policy support.** Should respondents include optional post-adoption support for register maintenance, vendor reviews, training refresh, policy updates, or high-risk use-case assessments?
