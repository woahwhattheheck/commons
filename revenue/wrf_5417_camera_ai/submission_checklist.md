- [ ] Project Abstract — max 1 page; contains final PI/co-PIs/applicant/participating organizations plus exact WRF amount and cost-share/in-kind amounts.

- [ ] Application Potential — max 1 page.
- [ ] QA/QC — up to 20 pages; tailored to actual sites/reference methods/instruments.
- [ ] Management Plan — max 2 pages; actual people, organizations, roles, responsibilities, effort, accountability and org chart.

- [ ] Detailed project schedule with responsible party, milestones, deliverables and WRF/PAC review/revision time.
- [ ] CV/resume <=2 pages per key person.
- [ ] Current & Pending form for PI and each co-PI.
- [ ] Third-party contribution commitment letters, if applicable, with exact dollar value and required authorization.
- [ ] Co-Funding Support Form + commitment letter for any organization providing cash payable directly to WRF.
