## Public external research starter set

- Satoh H., Kashimoto Y., Takahashi N., Tsujimura T. (2021), *Deep learning-based morphology classification of activated sludge flocs in wastewater treatment plants*, Environmental Science: Water Research & Technology 7, 298–305. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/D0EW00908C
  - The public abstract reports >12,000 images from two WWTPs, about 95% training accuracy for aggregated/dispersed floc classification and retraining when recognizing flocs from other plants. This is useful evidence for both the opportunity and transferability problem.
- Tan et al. (2023), stationary RGB-camera HAB detection, Environmental Modelling & Software article URL identified during research: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1569843223002455
  - Before final submission, verify the full bibliographic record from a stable public index/DOI; do not rely on a search snippet for citation metadata.
- Additional spectral HAB and activated-sludge transfer/settleability literature was identified during research, but full bibliographic metadata must be verified before it enters the final References section.

## Source discipline

For every proposal claim that affects technical novelty, qualifications, costs, eligibility or a named partner:

1. prefer the Project 5417 RFP/current WRF guidelines for requirements;
2. prefer public WRF project pages for WRF prior-work characterization;
3. cite original peer-reviewed literature for external scientific performance claims;
4. never convert a subscriber-only project-update snippet into detailed factual claims beyond the public project page;
5. never treat earlier sales outreach as utility participation evidence;
6. never cite this repository's own proposal draft as evidence that an applicant capability exists.
