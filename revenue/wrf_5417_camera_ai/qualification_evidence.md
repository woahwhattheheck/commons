# WRF 5417 qualification evidence and gap map

The RFP allocates 20 points to qualifications/capabilities/management. This file exists to prevent a persuasive technical concept from turning into unsupported past-performance claims.

## 1. What current public repository evidence supports

The connected GitHub organization contains substantial evidence of **software engineering, evaluation harnesses, deterministic evidence receipts, fail-closed validation, multi-agent/automation infrastructure, reproducible test workflows and rapid technical iteration**. Examples include the Commons repository's evaluation/coordination systems and the merged `revenue/outbound_transport_reconcile` product, which validates provenance-bound cross-system evidence with hostile tests and explicit authority ceilings.

Those capabilities are relevant to:

- reproducible dataset/model/evaluation manifests;
- versioned evidence and provenance;
- test automation;
- failure-state design and explicit abstention/hold semantics;
- integration architecture;
- open-source packaging and operational handoff.

They are **not equivalent to water-sector field research or computer-vision past performance**.

## 2. What current searches did not establish

Fresh organization code/repository searches performed for this carrier did **not** surface a material current default-branch implementation using OpenCV/cv2/torchvision or a clearly named computer-vision/image-classification/segmentation research repository. Connected-mail/library searches did not surface a verified PI CV specifically establishing water-utility camera-AI research experience.

Therefore the final proposal must not claim that Token Junkie Labs has already:

- deployed camera-based water-quality monitoring at a drinking-water or wastewater utility;
- trained/validated water-sector computer-vision models in the field;
- operated a water laboratory or certified analytical method;
- published peer-reviewed camera-AI/water research;
- managed a prior WRF award;
- secured any utility site for Project 5417;
- acquired/operated hyperspectral water-monitoring hardware.

## 3. Qualification consequence

A solo submission with only the currently verified evidence would face a **material 20-point qualification/management weakness**, even if the technical approach scores well. The most credible recovery is not to inflate the applicant's résumé; it is to add real, committed expertise.

Priority partner/role classes:

1. **Water/wastewater process or utility research lead** with field methods and operational interpretation;
2. **computer-vision research lead** with image-domain adaptation/transfer and calibration experience;
3. **participating utility site lead(s)** providing real operational use cases and reference measurements;
4. **QA/QC/statistics support** appropriate to the selected analytes/reference methods.

One organization or individual may cover multiple roles if qualifications are documented.

## 4. Evidence needed for each named key person

Before adding a person to the final packet, obtain and reconcile:

- role and employing organization;
- eligibility as PI/co-PI/key person under WRF rules;
- <=2-page CV/resume content;
- relevant projects/publications/deployments;
- exact responsibility and time commitment;
- Current & Pending support information;
- subcontract/consultant or participation commitment where applicable;
- conflict/availability constraints.

No name goes into the management chart because "they would probably help."

## 5. Existing WRF knowledge base the team must demonstrate it understands

The narrative should explicitly differentiate Project 5417 from at least these public WRF research lines:

- **5154 — Autonomous in situ Monitoring of Harmful Algal Blooms:** low-cost autonomous microscopy/imaging, ML species identification/quantification and field testing.
- **5266 — Determining the role of spectral imaging as an Early Warning System for HABs:** geographically diverse historical/field/lab hyperspectral and multispectral comparisons.
- **5339 — AI-Based Early-Warning & Mitigation System for HABs:** transferable real-time AI combining laboratory, field, weather and satellite sources plus mitigation strategies.
- **5189 — Artificial Intelligence Adoption Framework for Water and Wastewater Utilities:** AI/ML use cases, benefits and adoption/value framework.
- **WRF ML Toolkit / Data-Driven Process Control:** practical ML workflow and process-control implementation guidance.

The 5417 proposal's value should be the **minimum camera/sensing requirements + cross-site/camera transfer/failure-envelope framework across drinking-water and wastewater use cases**, not a relabeling of those projects.

## 6. Bid/no-bid qualification gate

Before final submission, answer truthfully:

- Is there an eligible PI with a CV that can defend the scientific/management role?
- Is there demonstrable computer-vision/model-evaluation expertise on the named team?
- Is there water/wastewater domain and reference-method expertise on the named team?
- Are actual utility field sites/data/reference measurements committed or is the design feasible without claiming them?
- Can the team manage a 24–30 month, multi-site research project and the required grant accounting/reporting controls?

If the answer to the first three remains `no`, the package should be treated as **NO-BID / PARTNER-REQUIRED**, not converted to a fictional qualifications section.
