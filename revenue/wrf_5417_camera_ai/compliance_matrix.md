## Administrative risk finding

The largest current submission risk is **not the technical narrative**. It is administrative eligibility and grant-management readiness: PI interpretation, portal access, documented 33% contribution, organization financial/W-9 package, and truthful grant-management controls. The official Financial Grant Management Capabilities form expressly asks about project accounting, written policies, time/effort records, budgeting controls, separation of duties, ledgers, audits, indirect-cost support and federal-cost familiarity. Those answers must reflect the applicant as it actually operates.

## Bid/no-bid rule

Do not press `Submit` unless every non-`READY` item that is required for this applicant has been converted to verified evidence or a WRF-confirmed interpretation. A technically strong proposal with an invented administrative answer is worse than a truthful no-bid.
