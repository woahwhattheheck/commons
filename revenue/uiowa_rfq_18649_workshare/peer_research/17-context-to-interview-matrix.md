## Capture and handoff template

For each selected row record: **question ID; confirmed service; participants' roles; stated practice; concrete example; artifact ID/version/locator; observed fact; disagreement; follow-up; and status** (`unasked`, `stated`, `corroborated within scope`, `unresolved`, or `not applicable with reason`). These are note-taking labels, not maturity grades or replacements for the engagement's evidence method.

Assign follow-up to a proposed role until the engagement confirms responsibility. Preserve dissent and counterexamples. Do not fill absent answers from a peer card. A completed interview row should be usable by another analyst without exposing private records in this public pack.
