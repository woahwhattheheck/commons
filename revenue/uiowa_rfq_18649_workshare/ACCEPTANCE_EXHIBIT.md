**Opportunity:** University of Iowa RFQ 18649  
**Prospective prime:** Clark's Consulting  
**Technical workshare:** TokenJunkieLabs / TJLabs  
**Commercial status:** **PROPOSED / NOT ACCEPTED**  
**Base workshare:** **$24,000 fixed**  
**Optional final-readout support:** **$4,000 only if separately authorized in writing**

This exhibit turns the existing commercial workshare into an artifact-level handoff a prospective prime can inspect before deciding whether to commit. It is a planning and acceptance aid, not a University submission, subcontract, purchase order, invoice, award, teaming signature, or representation that Clark's Consulting has accepted the workshare.

The controlling RFQ, amendments, Q&A, eBid instructions, prime commitments, and any executed agreement supersede this exhibit wherever they differ.

## 1. Workshare objective

TJLabs proposes to produce bounded technical assessment artifacts that help the prospective prime evaluate and support the University-requested assessment across the three identified application/information-system groups:

- Enterprise Student Systems (ESS)
- Research Information Systems (RIS)
- Identity & Access Management (IAM)

The technical carrier evaluates each group across four dimensions already represented by the repository's deterministic 12-cell evidence model:

- software development
- security
- deployment / CI-CD / monitoring
- AI readiness

TJLabs owns evidence organization, deterministic technical compilation, traceability, draft technical findings/roadmap support, and reproducibility/currentness evidence for its own artifacts. The prospective prime retains bidder and University authority, professional judgment, benchmarking conclusions, final recommendations, staffing and onsite commitments, references, insurance, contracting, submission, and final readout authority.

## 4. Deliverables

### 4.1 Kickoff evidence plan and source register structure

TJLabs delivers a kickoff technical packet that defines the evidence and compilation frame for the workshare. It includes:

- a 12-cell scope matrix covering ESS, RIS, and IAM across the four technical dimensions;
- a source-register structure with stable source IDs, source kind, source owner/custodian, authorization/provenance field, observation/currentness field, and source-content digest field;
- an evidence-request map showing which source classes are expected to support which assessment cells;
- explicit markers for missing, stale, conflicting, untrusted, or not-yet-authorized evidence;
- the current artifact-generation / authority-generation identifier used by the deterministic carrier;
- a dependency list for prime or University inputs that remain outstanding.

This packet establishes what TJLabs needs to assess; it does not assign unsupported maturity scores or invent observations.

### 4.2 Draft technical work package

TJLabs delivers a draft package containing, to the extent supported by authorized evidence actually supplied:

- populated evidence register;
- deterministic 12-cell maturity/gap matrix or explicit HOLD states where authority is missing, stale, conflicting, or incomplete;
- draft technical findings tied to source IDs and the applicable assessment cell;
- prioritized, phased roadmap inputs suitable for prime review;
- reproducibility/currentness receipt material for the compiled technical package;
- a limitations/dependencies section identifying unresolved evidence gaps, source conflicts, stale observations, and scope assumptions;
- a change log identifying material changes from the kickoff frame.

A draft finding may be revised after prime review or additional evidence. Draft status is not a representation of final University-facing professional judgment.

### 4.3 Final technical work package

TJLabs delivers a reconciled final package for the authorized base workshare containing:

- final evidence register for the evidence universe actually authorized and received;
- final deterministic 12-cell technical matrix with supported states and explicit HOLD states where evidence authority remains insufficient;
- reconciled technical findings and prioritized roadmap matrices/inputs within TJLabs' workshare;
- final limitations, unresolved-dependency, and evidence-conflict register;
- final artifact change log;
- currentness/reproducibility verification material for the delivered generation;
- corrections to genuine nonconformance in TJLabs-authored artifacts identified during acceptance review.

TJLabs does not silently convert missing evidence into a positive score or remove a documented HOLD merely to make a final package look complete.

### 4.4 Optional $4,000 final-readout support

The optional final-readout support is **not included in the $24,000 base workshare** and does not begin automatically. It requires a separate written authorization defining the requested readout/support activity, timing, participants, and any additional inputs. The prospective prime retains final presentation and University-facing authority.

## 5. Artifact acceptance criteria

### 5.1 Kickoff / evidence-plan acceptance

The kickoff packet conforms when all of the following are true:

1. the scope matrix explicitly covers all three identified system groups and all four technical dimensions;
2. requested source classes are mapped to assessment cells rather than collected without purpose;
3. the source-register schema can identify source, custodian/owner, evidence reference, authorization/provenance, observation/currentness, and content digest;
4. missing or unavailable evidence is represented as a gap/HOLD dependency, not a fabricated observation;
5. known prime-owned and buyer/University-owned dependencies are separately identified.

### 5.2 Draft technical package acceptance

The draft package conforms when all of the following are true:

1. every populated technical conclusion can be traced to one or more source IDs in the delivered evidence register;
2. the report covers the full 12-cell assessment frame, using explicit HOLD states for unsupported cells rather than implied completion;
3. missing, stale, conflicting, or untrusted evidence is visibly bounded and cannot silently promote a cell to a supported state;
4. source-universe/currentness checks and deterministic recompilation checks complete for the delivered draft generation, or the package explicitly reports the exact blocker preventing a current trusted result;
5. draft findings and roadmap inputs remain within the technical workshare and do not claim bidder submission, contract, legal/compliance certification, award, or final University-facing recommendation authority;
6. prime review comments received during the agreed review window are either incorporated when they correct TJLabs artifact nonconformance, or recorded as a bounded decision/open item when they require prime judgment, new evidence, or scope change.

### 5.3 Final technical package acceptance

The final package conforms when all of the following are true:

1. the delivered evidence register, technical matrix, findings/roadmap inputs, limitations, change log, and reproducibility/currentness material identify one coherent final generation;
2. every supported technical conclusion remains traceable to the delivered evidence universe;
3. unresolved missing/stale/conflicting/untrusted evidence remains explicit rather than being rewritten as verified evidence;
4. corrections for genuine TJLabs artifact nonconformance identified during acceptance review have been applied or a specific unresolved blocker has been documented;
5. the final package contains no unsupported representation that the University accepted a conclusion, that Clark's Consulting has executed a subcontract, or that payment/revenue occurred;
6. the prime can independently identify what is complete, what remains held, what changed from draft, and which decisions remain prime-owned.

A disagreement with a technically supported conclusion is not by itself an artifact defect. If the prime identifies an unmet acceptance criterion, the rejection should identify the affected artifact and criterion so TJLabs can distinguish actual artifact nonconformance from a request for new evidence, changed scope, or a different professional judgment.

## 7. Change-control boundary

The $24,000 base workshare does not silently expand to cover materially new scope. The parties should separately authorize a change when the prime requests work outside the bounded technical package, including examples such as:

- additional system/application groups or assessment dimensions not represented by the agreed frame;
- material new evidence universes after an accepted final generation;
- substantial new interview programs or repeated interview cycles;
- onsite work or travel;
- implementation/remediation engineering rather than assessment and roadmap support;
- procurement, legal, audit, certification, insurance, or regulatory opinions;
- external tooling or services that require spend not already authorized;
- University-facing submission ownership or bidder communications;
- the optional final-readout support described in Section 4.4.

A small correction to a TJLabs-authored artifact that fails an agreed acceptance criterion is not treated as a new scope item merely because it occurs during acceptance review.

## 8. Evidence, privacy, and repository boundary

The public Commons carrier contains synthetic fixtures, schemas, deterministic compiler logic, and non-sensitive commercial framing. **Real University, prime, customer, credential, or confidential assessment evidence must not be committed to the public repository.**

Operational evidence should be handled using the minimum necessary content and stable source references/digests appropriate to the authorized environment. Secrets, credentials, private source material, and restricted evidence stay in an authorized private custody boundary. Public repository receipts are evidence of code/artifact state only; they do not prove buyer acceptance, payment, or permission to publish a customer's facts.
