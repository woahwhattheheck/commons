## Decision boundary

These files are research/evidence only. They do not authorize a feature default, runtime/controller change, archive mutation, or Kaggle submission. The durable conclusions remain:

1. random weed exposure scales with currently empty unlocked tiles, not exponentially with owned land;
2. blanket empty structures are not a weed-clearing action bank;
3. the literal no-hire 10-GOOSE Day-0 opener is impossible on the pinned engine;
4. a constructively certified 9-GOOSE + one-HIRE Day-0 frontier exists, but its first keep-alive cycle is not a Day-1 cash printer;
5. the all-empty Q1→Q4 land hurdle includes $7,000 of land purchases plus the measured random-weed service/travel term, while productive occupancy reduces the random-weed term.

Any future opening/expansion policy should consume this directory rather than creating another sibling research authority.
