# Gemini FERT floor-buy/apply — full-path source certificate

Status: **constructed full-interpreter research / no current-native engagement or activation claim**.

The all-Gemini convergence ledger correctly separated this historical proposal from the false `$1 FERT warehouse` idea. The warehouse claim is false because a floor-price SELL destroys shed custody instead of creating recoverable public stock. A different seam survives: official `BUY_PRODUCT` *can* buy public FERTILIZER, and at sufficiently high public inventory the one-unit post-buy quote reaches the engine's `$1` price floor.

That price observation alone is not an exploit. Bought FERT lands in the shed; `FERTILIZE` consumes a unit from the acting farmer's carried inventory. A usable path therefore has real custody and action costs:

`BUY_PRODUCT FERTILIZER -> shed -> PICKUP FERTILIZER -> FERTILIZE -> WATER/production -> HARVEST -> sale`.

`fert_floor_apply.py` runs that exact path through the already-authenticated full interpreter used by `opportunity_cost.py`. It does not implement another market model or donor policy.

## Two constructed witnesses

### Minimal one-WATER witness

The first fixture is deliberately short and legible:

- constructed day-3 CARROT state at the farmer's shed-access tile;
- one existing yield unit, normal day-3 WATER opportunity;
- rival PASS throughout;
- candidate buys exactly one FERT at step 90, picks it up at 91, fertilizes at 92, then shares the control's WATER/HARVEST/DROP+SELL/DIG path;
- control leaves 91/92 idle.

This asks only whether the complete local source path can buy, custody, apply and realize one incremental unit while converging back to equal farm/private physical state. It is not a claim that the abbreviated CARROT prehistory was generated by current V4.

### Reachable three-day amortization witness

The source makes FERT active for the current day plus the next two days. For annual crops, each WATER in the in-window period receives the fertilizer bonus, capped by that crop's `max_yield`. A one-WATER fixture therefore understates the strongest mechanically valid form, but the crop entering the window must itself be source-reachable.

The second fixture now uses a MELON planted on day 4 and executes its survival prefix through the pinned interpreter:

- the run begins at step 96/day 4 with the newly planted MELON;
- candidate and control share survival WATER callbacks at steps 96, 144 and 192 (days 4, 6 and 8). Those are ages 0, 2 and 4, before MELON's yield window begins at age 6, so they preserve the live plant without manufacturing pre-FERT yield;
- every callback from step 96 onward is executed. At step 239, after the day-9 EOD, both arms must still hold the same `PLANT` MELON with `yield_units == 0` and the expected unwatered streak;
- candidate buys at step 240, PICKUPs at 241 and FERTILIZEs at 242;
- candidate and control then share exactly the same production WATER callbacks at steps 243 (day 10), 264 (day 11), and 288 (day 12), corresponding to MELON ages 6, 7 and 8;
- both harvest at step 289 and DROP+SELL at 290;
- the same **two** candidate-only unit callbacks are therefore amortized across the complete three-day fertilizer window, with the crop's pre-window survival history included rather than teleported.

The official MELON cap is six units. The focused checker expects the pinned interpreter to establish candidate six versus control three only after the step-239 reachable-prestate assertions succeed. This remains an authored assertion to be validated by exact execution, not a pre-declared green result.

## Exact floor boundary

The source-derived floor threshold is defined as the **lowest pre-buy FERT market inventory whose post-buy quote is `$1`**. The helper searches the authenticated engine's own `market_price`, preserving source rounding and shape semantics instead of hard-coding a threshold.

The minimal declared panel is both seats × `{threshold-1, threshold, threshold+100}`. The amortized witness runs both seats at the exact threshold. This provides the immediately-above-floor boundary, the first floor cell, a deeper-floor control, and the reachable three-day value frontier without pretending those market inventory levels are naturally reached by V4.

## What a positive witness would mean

If the full interpreter shows the candidate buys for `$1`, custody moves shed -> farmer -> crop, the shared day-4/day-6/day-8 survival prefix reaches day 10 with a live zero-yield MELON, three fertilizer-active WATERs produce incremental crop units, final private/farm physical state is equal after liquidation, and own cash is higher, then the historical mechanism is **source-real under constructed idle callback capacity**.

The three-day witness tests an important improvement over the literal post: `PICKUP` and `FERTILIZE` are fixed setup costs, while the fertilizer benefit may span three common WATER callbacks. If three extra MELON units survive the full reachable prefix and interpreter, the constructed candidate-only callback cost is 2/3 of one extra unit action per incremental MELON rather than two actions per one boosted crop unit. The shared survival WATERs are real labor too, but they are identical in candidate and control and exist to make the crop prestate source-reachable rather than to hide candidate-specific cost.

It still does **not** establish field profitability. The two candidate-only setup callbacks can be worth far more than `$1` in a live route. Storage headroom, financing, route position, future WATER custody, crop saturation, alternative service, rival actions and the probability of seeing floor FERT all remain outside the constructed theorem.

The zero-cash controls for both witnesses verify the authored BUY row cannot manufacture the yield edge when the purchase is unfunded.
