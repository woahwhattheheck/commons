# Gemini / Antigravity post-merge refinements

`GEMINI-ANTIGRAVITY.json` remains the **single canonical coverage lock** for the recovered Gemini/Antigravity stream. This note is not a second ledger. It records the strongest corrected descendants produced by the convergence work and points each mechanism back to its existing V4 owner. A literal exploit may therefore end as a source theorem, a default-OFF admission gate, a durable falsifier, or a promoted candidate depending on evidence; “covered” never means “runtime-ON.”

## MELON — certified realization bound

The useful successor to the rejected sold-only / blind fixed lifetime cap is code under `repairs/gameplay/antigravity-melon-cap/liquidation_bound.py` from #13032. A caller must provide an exact nonnegative certificate for remaining MELON realization capacity on the chosen route. Already-held MELON plus maximum future yield of live MELON plants consumes that certificate before any new proposal capacity is admitted. Missing/malformed proof or custody returns the exact original proposal object, so uncertainty cannot silently fall back to the losing fixed cap. Promotion still requires an authenticated realization producer and current-native both-seat economics.

## FERT warehouse — destructive floor disposal only

The “$1 infinite recoverable FERT warehouse” literal is false. Official SELL consumes seller SHED stock, and a $1 floor sale does not add public market inventory. The useful residue remains the existing B7/FLOORDRAIN family: source-bound projected-EOD destructive disposal can be worthwhile only when sacrificing low-value stock frees capacity for more valuable incoming cargo. There is no recoverable external FERT cache.

## GANDER / Goose Printer — nine-geese frontier, not a cash printer

The source mechanism is real: placed surviving animals refresh fertilizer availability at EOD even before first product yield. The literal ten-goose/no-hire opener is not executable. HOMESTEAD/GANDER instead certifies the constructive Day-0 frontier of nine geese plus one $1 HIRE; its Day-1 service ledger remains materially below the cash-hold baseline, so the result is fertilizer-timing evidence rather than a proved printer. Opening/native assemblers retain any future economics gate.

## FASTING / STARVE — CARE-safe first miss only

Blanket alternate-day FEED was current-native negative because starvation destroys CARE bank and downstream value. The surviving descendant is the existing default-OFF FASTING admission: first missed feed only, no CARE bank at risk, guaranteed next-feed custody, actor-local WHEAT proof, and refusal of repeated/multi-day starvation. This is a narrow service-timing mechanism, not “starve animals for free.”

## PLANTGUARD / seed overshoot — preserve atomic collateral

Official PLANT preflight is whole-vector and atomic for same-crop seed demand. The best descendant is the existing UNITPIPE admission under `research/unit-phase-chaining/`: it may only reorder already-authored same-site pure `DIG/PLANT/WATER` rows, never invents rows or crops, and refuses engagement when whole-vector same-crop PLANT demand exceeds observed seeds. Inventory/service-dependent actions stay outside that helper.

## DEMANDVEL — absorption tails, not hard tiers

## PARALLAX / O01 — public pressure, not opponent archetype mutation

Previous-price evidence must be bound to exactly `step-1`, executable callbacks stop at `episodeSteps-2`, and `unlocked_quadrants` must be a reachable prefix of `NW, NE, SW, SE`. It never reads rival private stock, predicts hidden shop RNG, mutates an action, or emits allow/deny/choose authority.

## WEEDBANK / EXPANDTAX — reversible occupancy lease + TOWNRNG

The blanket empty-structure carpet remains rejected, but the engine exposes a narrower source-real mechanism. `BUILD_COOP` / `BUILD_PASTURE` can temporarily remove a `None` tile from weed RNG eligibility; a later `DIG` of the still-empty structure restores `None`; changing total empty tiles also changes the shared EOD RNG cursor that feeds public shop unlocks.

The best-form successor is implemented in `research/seat-asymmetry-audit/weedbank_occupancy_lease.py`. It requires current empty-tile custody, animal-free temporary structure, reachable BUILD and future DIG, an explicit reclamation obligation before productive reuse, and positive expected weed/routing-action margin after BUILD + DIG + temporary-tile opportunity cost. It deliberately accepts no hidden episode seed. Public shop divergence is classified through the existing TOWNRNG `ENVIRONMENT_PATH_DIVERGED` boundary, with equal total empty tiles as the negative control. At the default `.005` weed chance, a 30-EOD lease avoids only `0.15` expected weed events, so weed clearing alone cannot justify the carpet.

## ALTWATER / HYDRA — productive substitution plus recovery custody

The literal `WATER -> PASS` rewrite is field-falsified: PASS consumes the slot and current-native trials produced repeated day+2 WEED conversion and large negative margins. The source-real successor is productive substitution only. The live `astra/gemini-altwater-recovery-20260912` branch certifies an already-materialized two-day pair: eligible current WATER becomes effectful HARVEST only when stored yield is positive, and the same live ongoing plant is shown next day with a real WATER recovery. Same-site destructive DIG and terminal/non-executable recovery evidence fail closed. A final red-team requirement is semantic opcode custody: because the engine dispatches by `action[0]`, duplicate semantic WATER rows (including rows with trailing fields) must not let the helper certify a later no-op actor.

## HOIST / CARRYBANK — sticky actor-local consumption, not generic backpacking

CARRYBANK proves that hoisting WHEAT/FERTILIZER out of SHED can relieve capacity pressure, but its corrected current-route census found structural candidates with zero safe raw-route admissions after intervening acquisitions, DROP/EOD loss, and sink custody are accounted for. The shared live `titan-v4-gemini-sticky-obligations-20260912` branch is the right architectural successor: one immutable obligation primitive for HYDRA recovery WATER and CARRYBANK same-day FEED/FERTILIZE consumption across replans, rather than two post-hoc controllers.

That branch still requires sink-effect authentication before it can be a sound consumption proof: a bare projected FEED row is not itself capacity because official FEED silently no-ops once the animal is already `fed_today`. Future sink discharge must therefore come from an authenticated executable/postimage witness, while future acquisition rows may remain pessimistic burdens. This is a source-only contract until the real scheduler carries obligations across every relevant branch/replan and both-seat economics pass.

## RNG frame advance / seed crack — public path effect only

TOWNRNG establishes the real shared-stream theorem: EOD weed draws advance the same RNG stream later used for public shop unlocks, so different total eligible empty tiles can change the public environment path. That is not a hidden-seed oracle. Opening weed observations do not uniquely identify the episode seed, and same-total-empty redistribution is the required negative control. Any resulting shop divergence is an environment-path change, not a policy-only comparison.

These refinements preserve one V4: the 21-proposition coverage lock stays singular, gameplay/runtime ownership stays with existing canonical families, and corrected descendants move forward only through their current evidence gates. Mechanically false or economically dominated literals remain durable do-not-resurrect evidence rather than disappearing from the record.
