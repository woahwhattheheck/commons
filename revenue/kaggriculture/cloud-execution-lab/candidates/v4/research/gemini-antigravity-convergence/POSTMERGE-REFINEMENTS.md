# Gemini / Antigravity post-merge refinements

`GEMINI-ANTIGRAVITY.json` remains the **single canonical coverage lock** for the recovered Gemini/Antigravity stream. This note records stronger source-correct descendants that landed at, or immediately after, the parallel convergence carriers. It is not a second ledger and grants no runtime authority.

## MELON — certified realization bound

The useful successor to the rejected sold-only / blind fixed lifetime cap is now code under `repairs/gameplay/antigravity-melon-cap/liquidation_bound.py` (merged via #13032). A caller must provide an exact nonnegative certificate for remaining MELON realization capacity on the chosen route. Already-held MELON plus maximum future yield of live MELON plants consumes that certificate before any new proposal capacity is admitted. Missing/malformed proof or custody returns the exact original proposal object, so uncertainty cannot silently fall back to the losing fixed cap. Promotion still requires an authenticated realization producer and current-native both-seat economics.

## DEMANDVEL — absorption tails, not hard tiers

#13018 preserves Gemini's exact per-shop demand ratios but upgrades the brittle tier rule into source-bound absorption headroom. Unlock horizons, with-replacement shop variance, town-center depletion, and conservative panel tails are explicit. The primitive remains non-authoritative: it does not account for rival supply or sale timing and cannot by itself choose crop mix or production scale.

## PARALLAX / O01 — public pressure, not opponent archetype mutation

The unique source from stale #13031 is consumed here as `research/rival-route-pressure/gemini_public_pressure.py` plus focused contracts; its duplicate routing document is intentionally not copied. The report lets public early-expansion evidence and an adjacent one-callback price-drop witness coexist, joins them with visible rival standing yield/productive-source counts, and uses PARALLAX's exact already-unlocked town drain instead of Gemini's historical `1.5x` absorption multiplier. A prior price map is admissible only when bound to exactly `step-1`. The report never reads rival private stock, predicts hidden shop RNG, mutates an action, or emits allow/deny/choose authority.

#13024 already bridges canonical PARALLAX pressure into the existing sale-horizon scorer by stressing every bounded partial rival quantity `1..min(visible standing yield,100)`, each turn in the existing 1..16 window, and `before/paired/after` alignment. Those are adversarial stress scenarios, not predictions that the rival will sell.

## ROWSHED / STRATUM — only final-pressure-novel ordering survives

#13016 upgrades the Gemini/Apex row-shed follow-up so an intermediate ranking difference is insufficient. If downstream pressure collapses both paths to the same action, admission returns parent identity. Quantity, duplicate multiplicity, suffix/barrier indices, and non-market surfaces must remain intact; malformed or unauthenticated pressure evidence fails closed.

## Apex AMBUSH — authenticated COMEBACK correction

`research/market-baseline/COUNTER_AMBUSH.md` now consumes authenticated Apex source custody from #13027. The source-real FERT event is at most four units, not the old synthetic 40-unit sensitivity: at market inventory 10,000, Apex max-four followed by our buy-four changes cost from $402 to $399 (only a $3 direct quote subsidy); at the $1 sell floor Apex adds zero public inventory and creates $0 direct subsidy. The useful descendant is the public-state-conditioned Strawberry timing edge, not a giant fertilizer sponge or hard-coded folklore.

## EGG — legal town-clock scarcity timing

The direct EGG buyout / dark-pool short squeeze remains engine-invalid because `BUY_PRODUCT` accepts only WHEAT and FERTILIZER. #13033 preserves the useful residue under `research/sale-window-engagement/gemini_egg_timing.py`: move an already-planned literal `SELL EGG` only to later legal PASS/append destinations, measure every candidate with the pinned full-interpreter sale-window harness, and require an actually realized retiming. This captures public town-demand scarcity pulses without inventing an EGG purchase path or a blanket “always hold” rule; rival supply and row/cash effects remain visible.

## WEEDBANK — reversible occupancy lease

The blanket empty-structure carpet remains rejected, but the engine exposes a narrower source-real mechanism. `BUILD_COOP` / `BUILD_PASTURE` can temporarily remove a `None` tile from weed RNG eligibility; a later `DIG` of the still-empty structure restores `None`; changing total empty tiles also changes the shared EOD RNG cursor that feeds public shop unlocks.

The best-form successor is implemented in `research/seat-asymmetry-audit/weedbank_occupancy_lease.py`. It requires current empty-tile custody, animal-free temporary structure, reachable BUILD and future DIG, an explicit reclamation obligation before productive reuse, and positive expected weed/routing-action margin after BUILD + DIG + temporary-tile opportunity cost. It deliberately accepts no hidden episode seed. Public shop divergence is classified through the existing TOWNRNG `ENVIRONMENT_PATH_DIVERGED` boundary, with equal total empty tiles as the negative control.

These refinements preserve one V4: mechanism coverage stays in this package, gameplay/runtime ownership stays with the existing canonical families, and corrected descendants move forward only through their current evidence gates. Literal policies that were mechanically false or economically dominated stay recorded as do-not-resurrect evidence rather than being silently dropped.
