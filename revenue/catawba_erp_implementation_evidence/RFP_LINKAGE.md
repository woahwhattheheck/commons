## Why this is partner-first

The RFP minimum criteria require the proposed software/integrator to have implemented the proposed software for **three similar organizations of similar size and complexity within the previous five years**. This package does not claim that prime qualification. The RFP's scope expressly allows bidders to partner with other bidders to meet the software scope.

Catawba has used Oracle PeopleSoft for Financials, Supply Chain Management, and HCM for more than 25 years and seeks a modern cloud ERP plus implementation partner. The County calls for project/change management, training, knowledge transfer, technical guidance, data migration, integrations, and post-go-live support.
