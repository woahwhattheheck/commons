# Proposed paid specialist workshare — MCC 1017-27

**Commercial hypothesis:** **$24,000 fixed / PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED / $0 booked / $0 cash.**

This is an internal partner-first scope hypothesis. It is not a quote to MCC, an accepted subcontract, a commitment from any candidate prime, or free proposal labor.

## Objective

Provide a bounded implementation-and-assurance workstream to a qualified prime/platform vendor after the controlling MCC package is retained and the prime independently satisfies every buyer qualification gate.

## Deliverables

1. **Requirements-to-capability traceability**
   - Map each retained buyer requirement to product capability, configuration, evidence owner, acceptance method, gap, and dependency.
   - Unknown or unsupported items remain explicit HOLDs.

2. **Institutional-data and model-training control matrix**
   - Record tenant/data boundaries, model-training settings, retention/deletion controls, access roles, logging, subprocessors, and buyer-required privacy evidence.
   - No legal, FERPA, security-certification, or contractual conclusion is issued by TJLabs.

3. **Multi-model and assistant/workspace governance profiles**
   - Define owner-reviewed role profiles for model access, assistant creation, workspace sharing, publishing, knowledge access, and admin escalation.
   - Final authority remains with prime/buyer administrators.

4. **Knowledge-ingestion acceptance tests**
   - Build deterministic tests for document ingest, permission boundaries, retrieval behavior, deletion/retention behavior, provenance/evidence visibility, and failure handling using prime/buyer-approved non-sensitive fixtures.

5. **SSO/LMS integration QA plan**
   - Translate retained interface requirements into integration checks, dependency ledger, acceptance criteria, and evidence capture.
   - Prime retains architecture, credentials, production change, vendor support, and go-live authority.

6. **Eight role-specific pilot templates**
   - Candidate template set for faculty, student, researcher, administrator, student-support, IT/helpdesk, communications, and institutional-research use.
   - Templates remain examples until owner-reviewed and policy-approved.

7. **Administrative telemetry and governance acceptance pack**
   - Usage/role/reporting evidence matrix, exception ledger, admin checklist, and unresolved-risk register.

8. **Enablement package**
   - Administrator guide.
   - End-user quick-start material.
   - Two remote training sessions after separate scheduling and approved content.

9. **Deterministic pilot acceptance packet**
   - Requirements coverage.
   - Evidence references.
   - Passed/failed/held acceptance tests.
   - Open dependency and exception list.
   - Explicit no-inference boundary for unsupported claims.

## Acceptance criteria

The workshare is complete only when the prime can review a single evidence packet that:

- names the exact retained buyer-package generation it is mapped against;
- gives each in-scope buyer requirement a status of `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `HOLD`;
- identifies the exact evidence for every `PASS`;
- never treats missing evidence as success;
- keeps policy/legal/security certifications outside TJLabs authority unless separately supplied by an authorized owner;
- records all unresolved integration, data, accessibility, privacy, platform, or buyer dependencies;
- provides the agreed eight pilot templates and training materials;
- leaves buyer acceptance, bid responsiveness, award, production go-live, and payment authority with the prime/buyer.

## Boundaries / exclusions

Not included in the $24,000 hypothesis unless separately accepted:

- prime bid drafting or portal submission;
- software licenses or third-party fees;
- buyer/partner legal representations;
- accessibility certification;
- FERPA/legal opinion;
- SOC/ISO/security certification;
- penetration testing;
- production credential custody;
- production SSO/LMS mutation;
- data migration at scale;
- custom model training;
- 24x7 operations/support;
- additional training sessions;
- buyer negotiation, contracting, signature, invoicing, collections, or funds movement.

## Responsibility split

**Qualified prime/platform vendor retains:** bidder eligibility; five-year/reference proof; product licensing; platform warranty; pricing form; contractual/security/privacy representations; production architecture; buyer communication; proposal/submission; portal account; signatures; implementation authority; acceptance and support commitments.

**TJLabs proposed role:** deterministic requirement/evidence mapping, bounded implementation QA, governance/acceptance artifacts, pilot-template engineering, enablement material, and owner-review packet.

**MCC retains:** procurement authority, requirement interpretation, evaluation, award, contract acceptance, institutional policy, production authorization, and payment.

## Change control

This workshare cannot be represented externally until the literal current buyer packet is retained. Any buyer addendum, Q&A, scope change, selected platform change, or prime responsibility change requires re-diffing the scope and re-validating the price before acceptance.

No silence, internal note, issue, branch, email draft, or partner marketing statement converts this hypothesis into a contract.
