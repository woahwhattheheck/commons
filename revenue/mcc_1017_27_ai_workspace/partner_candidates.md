1. the current controlling MCC package is retained;
2. buyer-authoritative teaming/subcontracting rules are known;
3. the candidate independently evidences the exact experience gate;
4. the candidate independently evidences at least three buyer-compliant higher-ed references;
5. the candidate identifies the exact platform/product and requirements mapping;
6. the proposed responsibility split and $24,000 workshare remain economically justified.

This carrier selects no partner and grants no communication or submission authority.
