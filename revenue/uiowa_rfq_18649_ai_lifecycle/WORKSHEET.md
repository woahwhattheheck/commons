## Reconstruct one useful result after a change

| Question to execute, not merely ask | Evidence to retain | Interpretation / next action |
|---|---|---|
| Pick one prior useful output and locate its exact workflow version. | Run ID, case ID, output locator/version and capture time. | Missing output prevents inspection; a remembered success is interview evidence, not a replay. |
| Retrieve its prompt, configuration, input context and model revision. | Artifact IDs, source versions, actual retained text/digest, model family/revision. | A version label alone is not retained material. Record unavailable provider revision separately from missing source. |
| Identify what changed and why. | Parent/current version, effective time, recorded rationale, support role. | Prompt, model, retrieval context and ownership changes are separate dimensions; preserve simultaneous changes. |
| Show how the result was assessed. | Dataset, rubric, protocol, reviewer revision, case-level values, missing measurements. | A fast response or favorable average is not full quality evidence; unknown values do not become zero. |
| Recompute a before/after comparison on like cases. | Both run IDs, observation kinds/cohorts, matching dataset and measured-case coverage. | Keep deltas unknown when contexts differ. Explain each mismatch instead of selecting another favorable sample. |
| Trace a problematic result into an investigation. | Affected run/case, retained investigation, opening time, owner, unresolved alternatives. | A narrative hypothesis is not established causality. Retain the underlying source and output. |
| Follow the corrective change to an actual recorded follow-up. | Corrective version, explicit comparison, sample scope and reported resolution. | Closed ticket and fixed-task improvement do not establish production effectiveness or wider generalization. |
| Identify who maintains the workflow and retained context. | Accountable role, handoff/change documentation, source/evaluation upkeep needs. | Unknown ownership is a maintainability question, not employee scoring or permission mutation. |

## Proportionate improvement options

These are planning options, not fixed effort estimates or purchase recommendations.

| Option | Trigger and prerequisite | Staff effort to estimate | Observable evidence of progress |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retain a version manifest with the output record | Output cannot be reconstructed; clarify the authorized evidence location and capture mechanism. | Implementation, redaction/review, storage administration and routine upkeep. | A sampled prior output resolves to prompt/config/source versions and a recorded model revision or explicit unknown. |
| Maintain a small, versioned regression collection | Changes are judged from anecdotes or different cohorts; define representative tasks and rubric. | Task curation, expected-answer review, periodic refresh and repair-time collection. | A candidate and baseline can be compared on matched tasks with visible denominators and unresolved cases. |
| Connect investigation to follow-up | Issues are marked closed without linked corrective evidence. | Support documentation, reproducer preparation, reviewer time and follow-up collection. | A sampled incident links affected run/case to evidence, correction and an appropriately scoped result. |
| Establish a support/context handoff | Owner, source currency or revision availability is unknown. | Role agreement, documentation and knowledge-source maintenance. | Another operator can locate the relevant records and explain what remains unavailable. |
