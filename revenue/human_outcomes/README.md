Canonical public surface: `humans.html`  
Machine-readable terms: `revenue/human_outcomes/offers.json`  
Fulfillment rules: `revenue/human_outcomes/fulfillment.md`

1. **Backlog Rescue — one owned ticket**: strongest current first-dollar route. One issue, existing checks, one ready-to-merge PR, $1,500 prepaid, 48 hours after locked inputs.
2. **Accessible meeting packet**: clear deadline-driven human outcome, $1,800 prepaid after source inspection.
3. **Security questionnaire sprint**: removes a sales-cycle blocker, $1,800 prepaid after evidence review.

5. **White Box**: preserved as the qualified high-ticket upgrade, not blasted as the first-dollar experiment.

Only one live client should occupy a founder-review slot at a time until one full delivery and refund path has been exercised.

## Five-reply experiment

The founder, not an autonomous bot, sends five replies to public buyer posts that already ask for help finishing a vibe-coded, Lovable, Cursor, Claude Code, or similar repository.

Subject:

> One CI-green PR on your named issue in 48 hours

Body:

> You already have the app and the issue. I will take one named ticket to a ready-to-merge PR in 48 hours after access and acceptance are locked. The fixed pilot is $1,500 prepaid: agreed checks green, human review note, one written revision, and a refund if the agreed acceptance is still missed after the included cure. If you send the public issue or a non-confidential summary, I will answer fit/no-fit before asking for private access.

Do not scrape personal emails, mass-post, impersonate a buyer, hide automation, or send customer code to Commons. Record sent, reply, scoped, authorized, settled, paid out, and bank-available as different states.

## Owner-private activation gates

These are deliberately not guessed or committed:

1. legal payee name and entity type;
2. jurisdiction and tax/invoice fields;
3. chosen collection rail and account onboarding/KYC;
4. payout destination entered only in the provider's official private UI;
5. invoice numbering and refund destination;
6. calendar capacity and signature authority.

After those are supplied, the catalog can issue a real private SOW/invoice. Revenue still additionally requires a real buyer to authorize payment; no repository change can fabricate that event.
