# Proposed paid specialist workshare

**Commercial state:** `$24,000 fixed / PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED`  
**Counterparty:** separately qualified OSIP prime, not the State directly from this package.

## Outcome

A bounded operations-evidence sprint that helps a qualified investment-management prime prove the administration/operations side of its OSIP delivery without transferring investment discretion, custody, accounting-opinion authority, or buyer-contact authority to TokenJunkieLabs.

## Included

- source-of-record and participant-account data authority map for admitted extracts/interfaces;
- deterministic participant balance, contribution/withdrawal, and earnings-allocation reconciliation controls;
- fund-accounting support controls and exception ledger (evidence support only);
- Treasury/statutory-report field lineage and extract acceptance;
- audit/custody coordination evidence index with retained source references;
- transaction-processing QA corpus and fail-closed exception classes;
- technology/internal-control/BCP evidence matrix tied to prime-supplied controls;
- implementation/UAT acceptance package with exact input/output receipts;
- one corrected-evidence rerun for the admitted scope.

## Excluded

- investment management, asset allocation, credit research, trading, portfolio risk decisions or performance promises;
- participant contribution/withdrawal execution;
- custody, banking, ACH/wire movement, account opening or credentials;
- accounting policy, audit opinion, SOC opinion, legal/regulatory advice, Form ADV representation or licensing;
- prime qualification claims without retained evidence;
- buyer contact, RFP question submission, proposal submission, lobbying, signatures or contract acceptance;
- live production mutation;
- award, payment, savings, booked revenue or cash claims.

## Acceptance

Technical workshare acceptance means the admitted evidence generation reproduces its reports, every required control yields an explicit pass/fail/hold result, and unresolved exceptions remain visible. Business/buyer acceptance belongs to the prime/State and is never inferred by this package.
