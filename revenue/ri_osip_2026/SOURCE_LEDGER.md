# Source ledger - Rhode Island OSIP 2026

## Controlling buyer source

- Buyer: Rhode Island Office of the General Treasurer / State Investment Commission.
- Document: 2026 RFP for Investment Management, Recordkeeping, Operational and Administrative Services for the Ocean State Investment Pool (OSIP).
- Official URL: https://treasury.ri.gov/media/2171/download?language=en
- RFP issued: 2026-09-15.
- Questions due: 2026-09-25, 4:00 PM Eastern.
- Proposals due: 2026-10-20, 4:00 PM Eastern.
- Observed during build: 2026-09-17.

The buyer-hosted RFP was parsed through the public retrieval surface. Exact PDF bytes were not retained into this repository by this build.

## Prime minimums observed in the RFP

- at least 5 years in business as an investment-management organization managing institutional assets in the subject or similar strategy;
- authorized to conduct investment-management services in Rhode Island;
- same-day credit for transactions through 3:00 PM Eastern;
- directly involved investment professionals with at least 5 years relevant experience;
- at least $5 billion institutional AUM in the subject/similar strategy;
- at least one institutional public client in the subject/similar strategy;
- equal-opportunity employer.

These minimums apply to the RFP respondent. This build does not represent TokenJunkieLabs as satisfying them.

## Scope relevant to specialist teaming

The RFP includes participant-level books/accounts/transaction histories, earnings allocation, fund accounting/reconciliation, Treasury/statutory reporting, audit/custody coordination, participant transaction administration, information security/internal controls/business continuity, and custom/ad-hoc reporting.

The questionnaire also asks respondents to identify subcontractors intended for aspects of fund administration and separately asks about outsourced/subcontracted custody.

## Evaluation weights

- Investment Management: 25 points.
- Administration and Operations: 25 points.
- Organization and Experience: 20 points.
- Participant Services, Marketing, and Distribution: 15 points.
- Fees: 15 points.

## Contact boundary

The RFP identifies Cash_RFPs@treasury.ri.gov as the sole point of contact and prohibits procurement-related contact with other Treasurer/State personnel. No buyer contact occurred from this build.
