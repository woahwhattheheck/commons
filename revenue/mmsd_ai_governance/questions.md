# MMSD clarification queue

## One recommended procurement question

Use only after fresh Gmail/Slack dedupe and only through the RFP-authorized `rfp@madsewer.org` route before the September 28 question deadline.

> For proposal qualification and team structure, could the District please confirm whether the RFP requires (a) a minimum number/type of prior client references and (b) proof of professional/cyber liability insurance with the proposal or only upon award? If experience or reference requirements apply, may a prime consultant rely on the documented qualifications and references of named subcontractors/key team members, or must the prime independently satisfy them?

Why this question is worth asking: the public landing page and indexed solicitation facts establish the technical scope and schedule, but the source PDF's submission checklist/qualification gates were not retrievable through the current research harness. The answer determines `PRIME` vs `TEAM` vs `NO-GO` without asking the buyer to repeat scope already published.

Do **not** ask the buyer to endorse our capabilities, waive a requirement, estimate our score, or disclose competing proposals. Do not include private company evidence in the question because proposal responses are public record and buyer correspondence may also be retained.
