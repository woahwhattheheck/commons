# Commercial handoff — Redmond RFP 10915-26

## Saleable role

Position TokenJunkieLabs as an **evidence/governance accelerator and technical subcontractor** to an experienced ECM prime/vendor, not as the turnkey ECM platform.

The compiler turns a prime's actual product, implementation, security, support, reference, and roadmap evidence into a deterministic review package. That can reduce unsupported claims, expose blockers before submission, and give the proposal lead an acceptance-test plan tied to each retained requirement.

## Concrete handoff package

A prime/vendor can provide:

- its Exhibit A responses or working requirement matrix;
- product/security/DR/SLA evidence;
- integration and migration architecture;
- implementation methodology and staffing evidence;
- support policy;
- customer-reference evidence;
- AI strategy/governance material;
- exceptions and roadmap items.

The delivery team returns:

- evidence-bound compliance matrix;
- contradiction/blocker ledger;
- acceptance-test plan;
- stable JSON and Markdown review artifacts;
- explicit unknowns requiring vendor or buyer resolution.

## High-value review gates

1. **No unsupported Y.** A current-release `Y` without linked supporting evidence stays `unknown`.
2. **Critical future-only/not-supported claims block.** Required legal/submission/records/security items cannot be hand-waved.
3. **Contradictions surface instead of averaging away.**
4. **References remain evidence-bound.** No fabricated municipal experience.
5. **Exhibit A remains authoritative.** This package does not replace the City's Word template.
6. **Submission stays human-controlled.** This tool has no DocuSign, email, or buyer-contact action.

## Suggested paid engagement shape

Quote the prime/vendor for a bounded **ECM proposal evidence readiness sprint** based on the number of requirements, evidence sources, and review cycles. Scope and price must be agreed with the prime before work; this repository does not invent a fee or imply an accepted engagement.
