Interpretation boundary

Per attendee consumable: the entered quantity is multiplied by expected attendance. Shared/reusable: the entered quantity is fixed for the workshop and does not scale with attendance.

This planner does not infer inventory on hand, tool-sharing ratios, procurement lead times, prices, or replacement rates. Enter those quantities deliberately. No checkout, messages, analytics, or external requests are connected.
