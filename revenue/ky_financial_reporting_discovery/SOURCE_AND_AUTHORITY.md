## Current-environment facts to verify against the latest VSS package

Research copies of the RFI describe:

- an on-premises three-tier reporting environment;
- Oracle database / financial data repository;
- a data warehouse;
- SAP BusinessObjects 4.3;
- approximately 2,500 enterprise users;
- approximately 5,000 Web Intelligence documents;
- intent to retain Oracle as the core financial data repository;
- interest in traditional reporting, dashboards, visualization, self-service analysis, and modern enterprise reporting;
- a requirement for respondents to identify Microsoft Government Community Cloud compatibility, limitations, and dependencies;
- requested discussion of firm background, relevant experience, solution capabilities, architecture, security, implementation, and estimated costs.

These are response-planning facts, not proof of our qualifications.
