# Migration inventory contract

A reporting-modernization discovery should recover the operating contract for each legacy report, not merely count documents.

For each report or report family, retain:

- stable report identifier and generation;
- business owner and technical owner;
- purpose and business criticality;
- source objects and semantic lineage;
- cadence and scheduling;
- parameters, prompts, filters, and defaults;
- access class;
- dependent reports and downstream extracts;
- known reconciliation procedures;
- usage evidence;
- intended disposition: retain, convert, consolidate, replace, or retire.

## Classification

Migration planning should distinguish:

- statutory or audit-sensitive output from management analytics;
- fixed transactional output from interactive exploration;
- simple direct-source reports from multi-source transformations;
- high-use shared reports from low-use personal documents;
- reports with stable definitions from reports whose definitions conflict;
- independent output from reports that feed other deliverables.

## Readiness

A report is not ready to retire just because a new visualization exists. Retirement readiness requires known lineage, a mapped target disposition, dependency closure, comparison evidence, and authorized owner review.

Disagreements between legacy definitions are discovery findings. They must remain visible until an authorized semantic decision resolves them.
