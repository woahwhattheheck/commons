# SYNTHETIC FIXTURE - IAM privileged access standard (v4)

> Fictional document authored for the UIOWA-092 rehearsal. Not a University record,
> not a University policy, and not a finding about any real organization.

**Version:** v4 · **Effective:** 2026-02-01 · **Owner role (fictional):** IAM service owner

## 1. Recertification
Every privileged account is recertified **quarterly** by the owning service role.

## 2. Exceptions
An account that cannot be actioned within the campaign is recorded as an exception with
a named owning role and an **expiry date**. An exception without an expiry is not permitted.

## 3. Evidence
Each campaign produces a completion export listing every account and its disposition.
