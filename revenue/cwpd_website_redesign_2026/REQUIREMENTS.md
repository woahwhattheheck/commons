## Acceptance gates before any submission

1. All `[REQUIRED:*]` markers resolved with evidence, not marketing inference.
2. Final price approved by the owner.
3. Comparable work, named personnel and references verified.
4. RecDesk language accurately distinguishes prior experience from proposed discovery.
5. Hosting choice and recurring third-party/license costs disclosed.
6. Accessibility process includes manual as well as automated testing and an issue-remediation record.
7. Proposal is checked against any buyer Q&A/addenda issued after this package.
8. Separate explicit authorization exists for the actual email submission.
