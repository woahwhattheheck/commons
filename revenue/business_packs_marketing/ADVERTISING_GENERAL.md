## 2. Landing-page reality the tier math depends on

- Median ecommerce landing page ~2.35%; product pages 2.5–3.5%; "good" is above 5%; top decile above 8–11% ([landerlab](https://landerlab.io/blog/landing-page-conversion-rate), [clickminded](https://www.clickminded.com/landing-page-statistics/), [seedprod](https://www.seedprod.com/landing-page-conversion-rates/)) `(secondary)`.
- Quiz funnels convert 3–8% on cold traffic, roughly 2x a standard product page. For packs this is a natural fit: "which pack is yours?" (truck / laptop / savings / business) routes Tyler, Lena, Renee and Owen to their tier without the buyer having to read six cards. Note for the factory, not a spec.
- The break-even table in BUYER_TIERS.md assumed 1–3% cold conversion; the benchmarks above say 2–3% is the median for a good product page, so the assumption is neither optimistic nor safe. The pixel decides.

## 4. Creative testing cadence that fits small budgets

- Test one variable at a time; three creatives per tier; 300 clicks or 2x tier price per creative before a kill decision (same rule as X_ADS_BRIEF §6).
- Every platform's automated review reads the landing page too. The door must state price and inclusions above the fold and must not contain earnings language anywhere, or the ad gets rejected for the page, not the copy.
- Reuse across channels: the 15-second vertical "what's inside" video and the 4-card carousel (name / week-1 calendar / price sheet / door) are the same assets on X, TikTok, Reddit and Meta. Build once per pack.

## 5. TikTok, as a first-class channel (owner, 2026-09-02 ~01:33 EDT: "tik tok is fine too")

**Who is there.** 25–44 is now TikTok's largest spending group and the age band most likely to act after an ad (54% research, 47% buy) ([emarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/millennial-tiktok-users-platform-s-most-active-shoppers), [emplicit](https://emplicit.co/tiktok-shops-demographic-shifts-growth/)); ~44% women. This is where the female-skewed pack buyer lives (yard-greeting rental, planners/templates, "faceless digital marketing"), and where the $50–$200 first-timer discovers products ("1 in 3 discover products on social").

**What it costs and needs** `(secondary unless ads.tiktok.com is cited)`:
- Minimums: **$50 per campaign, $20/day per ad group**; a $20/day ad group runs but cannot learn. Realistic first-month test $700–1,000; $1,500–3,000 over 3–4 weeks for a statistically honest read; the algorithm wants ~50 conversions/week per ad group to optimize ([stackmatix minimum budget](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-ads-minimum-daily-budget-2026), [tikadsuite](https://tikadsuite.com/blog/minimum-budget-for-tiktok-ads/), [megadigital lead gen](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/tiktok-lead-generation-ads/)).
- Costs: CPM $3–10, CPC ~$0.50–1.50, ecommerce average CPA ~$32.74 (Triple Whale 2025). Against the tier math: fine for $100 and $200, marginal for $50, hopeless for $20 unless a Spark Ad already went organic.
- **Spark Ads** (promoting an existing organic post, yours or a creator's with permission): ~2.4x CTR, ~44% higher conversion, 30–50% lower CPA than in-feed, at a ~38% CPC premium ([influee](https://influee.co/blog/tiktok-ads-benchmarks), [amraandelma](https://www.amraandelma.com/tiktok-spark-ads-statistics/)). The pack "what's inside" video should be posted organically first, then Sparked.
- Measurement: **TikTok Pixel plus Events API with deduplication**, events verified in Events Manager before spend ([ads.tiktok.com pixel](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/get-started-pixel?lang=en), [about TikTok Pixel](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-pixel)). Same thank-you-page gap as X: the Stripe Payment Link completes on stripe.com, so the pixel slot on the thanks door must accept TikTok (and Meta) IDs, not only X. Noted to the seat that claimed `scout-demand-pack-door-thanks-pixel-20260902-01`.
- Account: a **verified TikTok Business account linked to every campaign**; the old "Custom Identity" unlinked route is closed ([tikadsuite requirements](https://tikadsuite.com/blog/tiktok-ads-requirements/)). Owner step.
- **TikTok Shop is not the checkout.** The US Virtual Goods category (launched May 2026) is invite-only, covers gift cards, game codes and software licences with a $500 cap and a 2-hour API-fulfilment rule; courses, templates and downloads are excluded and route through link-in-bio to an external store ([eva.guru](https://eva.guru/blog/how-to-sell-digital-products-on-tiktok-shop/), [seller-us.tiktok.com Virtual Goods](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7563407075510030&lang=en)). Packs sell on our door; TikTok is traffic.

**Policy.** TikTok prohibits MLM, pyramid schemes, "get rich quick", and rejects income promises and investment-return claims; "if your product or service involves earning money, you must present realistic expectations with appropriate disclaimers"; financial services and other restricted verticals need pre-campaign certification ([tiktok ads policy other products](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-other-products-and-services), [benly rejected fixes](https://benly.ai/learn/tiktok-ads/tiktok-ads-rejected-fix), [tikadtools policies](https://tikadtools.com/blog/tiktok-ads-policies/)) `(secondary)`. Our "prices yes, earnings never" rule is the whole compliance posture here too; the extra TikTok risk is creator-made Spark content drifting into "I made $X" — creators must be briefed on the same rule.

**Tier fit.** $50 and $100 (creative-led impulse), $200 (before/after of a broken local site works as native content), $1,000 yard-greeting (her channel). Not $10,000: TikTok B2B lead-gen exists but the buyer conversation belongs on X/Google.

## 6. Third-party rating for pack evaluation (owner, 2026-09-02 ~01:33 EDT: "3rd party rating as a partner at a bulk price or rate")

Two different things get called "rating"; they solve different buyer problems and carry different law.

**A. Rating the pack itself (completeness / quality / uniqueness audit).** Answers "is this real, is it complete, is it actually unique" — the trust constraint on the $1,000 and $10,000 doors, and the "why sell it if it's good" objection a percentage or ownership clause creates. Candidates:
- **Centurica** — due-diligence shop for online and SMB acquisitions since 2013; sells website assessments and live verifications from the buyer's perspective; bulk/partner rates by contact ([centurica.com](https://centurica.com/), [gosbaloans review](https://gosbaloans.com/blog/centurica-review-due-diligence-specialists-for-online-and-small-business-acquisitions/)). Closest fit for a fixed per-instance checklist audit.
- **Flippa partner directory (due-diligence filter)** — a vetted list of independent assessors used by Flippa buyers; a natural place to find a second bidder for a bulk rate ([flippa partner directory](https://flippa.com/partner/directory?filter=due-diligence)).
- **BizEquity** — Valuation-as-a-Service with embedded/API partnerships (Equifax, Biz2Credit); reports from free lead-magnet tier to ~$5,000 written ([slashdot BizEquity](https://slashdot.org/software/p/BizEquity/), [BizEquity/Biz2Credit](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/12/19/2999668/0/en/BizEquity-Biz2Credit-Announce-BizEquity-Capital-to-Forge-Embedded-Finance-Partnership-to-Promote-Small-Business-Access-to-Capital.html)). **Caution below.**
- **BBB accreditation / SCORE** — not researched to a price in this pass; BBB is a seller rating, not a pack rating.

**Law flag for A (OWNER/counsel).** A third-party *valuation* or *revenue projection* of a pack, once tjlabs puts it on the door or in an ad, is tjlabs' own **earnings claim** under 16 CFR 437 (any representation, express or implied, of a level or range of potential sales or income) and an "unrealistic result" under X's and TikTok's policies. The FTC treats claims made through endorsements and third parties as the advertiser's. So the safe third-party rating is a **completeness/quality/uniqueness audit with a pass/fail or score**, not a dollar valuation. If a valuation is ever shown, it needs written substantiation and an earnings statement, and it drags the $1,000 and $10,000 tiers toward the business-opportunity regime.

**B. Rating tjlabs as a seller (post-sale reviews).** Standard trust signal; cheap; compounding. Candidates with public pricing `(secondary)`:
- **Reviews.io** — $29–$499/mo; the $99/mo Start-Up plan unlocks **Google Seller Ratings** (stars in Google Ads and Shopping) as an official Google Reviews partner ([reviews.io pricing](https://www.reviews.io/front/pricingplans), [wiserreview](https://wiserreview.com/blog/reviews-io-reviews/)).
- **Trustpilot** — Starter $99/mo (businesses under $5M revenue), Plus ~$319/mo per domain, Premium ~$799/mo; API only on Enterprise ($6k–30k+/yr) ([costbench](https://costbench.com/software/review-management/trustpilot/), [wiserreview Trustpilot](https://wiserreview.com/blog/trustpilot-pricing/)). Per-domain pricing punishes our one-domain-per-instance law; the seller profile, not the instance, is what gets reviewed.
- **Google Business Profile review badge** — free; live stars on the door ([wiserreview badge guide](https://wiserreview.com/blog/google-review-badges/)).

**Research shape (owner decides, signs, pays):** one due-diligence partner (Centurica or a Flippa-directory assessor) on a fixed per-instance completeness checklist at a bulk rate, producing a pass/fail seal and a short report linked from the door; plus one seller-review platform (Reviews.io Start-Up for Google Seller Ratings) on the tjlabs storefront. Neither publishes a dollar figure. The factory-side build is a rating slot on the pack door (seal + report link, empty by default, owner-filled like Payment Links) — noted in `#business-packs`, not claimed.

## 7. When another channel is "highly fruitful" enough to pursue (owner: "other platforms worth pursuing if highly fruitful")

Gate, so fruitful is measured: a tier earns a second channel only after its door has converted ≥2% on the first channel's traffic (proof the door works before buying more of it); a channel earns continued spend only while cost per `InitiateCheckout` stays under one-third of the tier's net per sale; a channel is dropped after 300 clicks with zero `InitiateCheckout` or 2x tier price spent with zero `Purchase`. Order of entry by tier: X → TikTok (or the reverse for female-skewed packs) → Reddit → Meta retargeting → Google Search intent for $1,000/$10,000.

## 8. What every channel forbids that our copy must never do

Meta, TikTok, Google and Reddit each have income-claims or "get rich quick" prohibitions comparable to X's Deceptive & Fraudulent policy; Meta and Google both restrict "business opportunity" and "make money" categories and require pre-approval or certification for financial-adjacent claims `(secondary; policies change)`. The single rule that satisfies all of them and the FTC: **describe the product and its price; never describe the buyer's future income.**
