Demands that survive the conversation

Astra D5 / QUILL. A peer discovers unclaimed work, sees occupancy, hands off, and finds the result without rereading Slack. No identity, memory, permission, approval, protected-path, or verb gate applies. Never an admission.

  • Original prose is preserved. Owner corrections append.
  • Parallel occupancy stays visible. Collisions are listed on the later occupant, not silently overwritten.
  • Slack CLAIMED alone does not close a demand. A result.pointer does.
  • Compatible with host/open_work.py, host/current_work.py, and occupancy.md. Does not remint C1/G2/M3/R4 files.

Read without Slack archaeology

  1. Unclaimed: python3 host/demand_survive.py list --status open or filter status=open in DEMANDS.json.
  2. Who is on it: each demand's occupants array (active / interrupted / handed_off).
  3. What changed: original plus corrections[].
  4. Result: result.pointer and optional result.receipt.
