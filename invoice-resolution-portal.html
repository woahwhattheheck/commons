Exactly three bounded request actions

  • ACKNOWLEDGE_BALANCE — records the customer request; it does not itself change the invoice.
  • OPEN_DISPUTE — opens an evidence workflow; it does not adjudicate the dispute.
  • REQUEST_PAYMENT_PLAN_DISCUSSION — records a request for discussion; it does not approve a plan.

What you provide

  • One invoice ID and revision plus SHA-256 of the exact invoice snapshot.
  • ISO currency, invoice amount and balance in integer minor units, and source-observed UTC timestamp.
  • An opaque non-contact customer reference and one of the three allowed request actions.
  • SHA-256 of the customer statement and, when needed, evidence IDs/classes plus SHA-256 digests.
  • The prior event-chain checkpoint only when this generation extends an earlier delivered packet.

Do not send card/bank information, credentials, passwords, API tokens, customer contact data, unrestricted statement narrative, or source invoice documents through the compiler boundary. Start with synthetic or safely minimized fixtures.

What you receive

  • A deterministic JSON owner-review packet and buyer-facing Markdown review export bound to the exact invoice generation.
  • Replay-safe request and evidence handling: identical retries are idempotent; conflicting reuse fails closed to HOLD.
  • An ordered event hash chain and retained-prefix checkpoint so history mutation is detectable.
  • Explicit HOLD reasons for generation drift, chronology/future evidence, conflicting replay, evidence without a request, and out-of-scope multiple requests.
  • A semantic verifier and delivery receipt checklist for the accepted fixture.

Acceptance boundary

The pilot is source-ready only when one exact invoice generation is bound across every event; at most one canonical request is active; conflicting replay fails closed; evidence cannot silently transplant across invoice/request identity; retained history still matches its prior checkpoint; future/out-of-order evidence holds; strict input rejects ambiguous or private-field payloads; packet tampering fails verification; and the same focused suite survives normal and optimized execution.

Authority boundary

The shipped carrier cannot send buyer messages, take payment, approve a payment plan, adjudicate a dispute, modify an invoice, make accounting or legal conclusions, claim customer acceptance, claim payment, or claim revenue. OWNER_REVIEW_READY means only that the evidence package is coherent enough for a human owner to review.
