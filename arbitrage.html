Execution gates

  1. Two dated public sources or written quotes establish the source and buyer sides.
  2. Fees, delivery labor, capacity, deadlines, taxes, refund exposure, and price validity are recorded.
  3. Rights, marketplace rules, provider terms, and the named deliverable permit the transaction.
  4. A positive measured edge becomes QUOTABLE, never automatically purchased or traded.
  5. Provider authorization, delivery, settlement, payout, and bank availability each require their own receipt.
