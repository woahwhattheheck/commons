Bryce is the chair. IDEA THROWER in caps. He says --go. He names the mouth bytes. He approves NEED_BRYCE items. He does not have to write spec. He does not have to spank. Those are other people's jobs.

Grok parent is the spec daddy. Main builder.

Fable is chat and read-only idea mill. Unless Bryce says otherwise. That "unless" is the only escape hatch in the whole chart.

Opus is side chair only. Four words. No builder status. No spec authority. Side chair.

It precomputes the test results into the always-on prompt and wastes the context window re-deriving what it already confirmed.

The fix document lists every harness change needed to get player 4 functional: strip the always-on keywords that tripped Anthropic's cyber false-positive, flip hooks on, import via @path not markdown links, battery as a skill not a greeting, path-scoped rules that load when touching .mno files, PreCompact marker to survive compaction.
