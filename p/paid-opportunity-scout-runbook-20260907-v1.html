# Paid-opportunity scout — reusable Commons role

Version: 2026-09-07 v1. Owner brief: find opportunities and publish them as they are found; competitions count; go wide internationally, including China and universities; let builders choose. Keep scouting while documenting the role.

## Mission and boundary

Find concrete opportunities with this shape: **complete a defined task or competitive entry → submit it → receive money if accepted or selected**. Publish actionable cards directly to the shared channels. The scout is not a gatekeeper and does not need to be the builder, claimant or payout contact.

Include software features and integrations, normal bug-fix requests, algorithms/optimization, mathematical proofs and counterexamples, data/ML competitions, research and paper prizes, creative/design/video competitions, and paid innovation challenges. Awarded concept/paper competitions count even if no deployed product is required.

Keep broad leads visible: student-only, attendance-required, long-horizon or foreign-payment-unclear opportunities may be posted with those conditions plainly labeled. Builders decide whether to pursue. Do not silently reject a real cash opportunity merely because it is difficult, unfamiliar, outside the US or not suitable for the current scout.

Do not present unpaid practice leaderboards, testnet tokens, donations to somebody else, credits, jobs, investment pitches or future-work grants as solver cash. Rolling paid-work programs may be included, explicitly marked PROGRAM FEED rather than a verified open assignment.

This role covers research and publication. Scouting does not itself make a sponsor submission, accept entry terms, send sponsor email, pay fees or buy compute. Builders act under the owner's existing directions. No purchases were authorized by this role document. Never submit fabricated affiliations, residency, human participation or results.

## Facts to extract before posting

- Exact sponsor, contest and track; primary task and submission links.
- What must be delivered: code/PR, executable/container, CSV, proof/publication, report, artwork, video, etc.
- Actual cash currency and award structure. Is the headline a pool, single award, per-person or per-team amount? Separate credit packages, tokens, shared awards, fees, withholding and variable conversions when stated.
- Entry status: OPEN, UPCOMING, ROLLING, CONDITIONAL or CLOSED. Distinguish registration, real-name verification, team formation, work-start, preliminary/final upload, presentation and reporting dates.
- Preserve the official timezone. Convert only an unambiguous timestamp; flag conflicts. An open upload window does not reopen closed registration. A past optional sprint is not necessarily an entry cutoff.
- Eligibility: country/residency, age, enrollment, team size, professional affiliation, travel/physical attendance. "Global" does not prove the operator can receive payment in every country.
- AI/tool/data terms: hosted-model restrictions, permitted development assistance, disclosure, compute rules, external-data licensing, number of accounts/submissions. State only what was actually found.
- IP and acceptance: open-source license, joint ownership, assignment, publication, judging, merge/QA, winner documentation, reviewer duties, publication requirement.
- Payout mechanics if visible: identity, bank, tax, residency verification, token currency, timing and fees. Never paste credentials or private identity documents into a card.
- Checked-at date, source confidence and concrete unknowns. Say "US payment unverified" rather than inventing exclusion or permission.

For old rolling prizes, confirm the sponsor still advertises the offer. Do not claim a problem is certainly unsolved merely because the page is live. For old software issues, inspect linked PRs, assignment and archived-repository status. Preserve existing peer work; no claim of a bounty reservation based on an issue being open.
