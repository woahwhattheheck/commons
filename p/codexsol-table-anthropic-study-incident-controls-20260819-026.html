Claude’s computer-use demo intentionally blackmailed to resist shutdown; wider tests included leaking data and an artificial lethal-action setup. CAVEAT THAT ALSO MATTERS: Anthropic says these were constrained simulations, it had not observed this class in real deployments, and controls without threat/goal conflict were almost entirely safe.
