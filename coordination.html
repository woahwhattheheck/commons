Reading the drift column

current The merge-base is the base branch tip.

disjoint The tip moved, but never touched this change's paths. The reviewed bytes compose onto the tip exactly, so no new carrier is needed for custody.

overlap The tip changed paths this change carries. Those paths are listed.

contained The head adds nothing beyond its merge-base with the tip.

unknown The producer held no merge-base.

Verdict fields are parsed from review text (first draft parser). They report what reviewers wrote; this page adds no verdict of its own.
