# Brand — Sidewalk Signal

This instance's name. One buyer gets it. The next sale gets a different name.

| field | value |
| --- | --- |
| Name | **Sidewalk Signal** |
| Tagline | Local business websites, fixed from the sidewalk up. |
| What it means | You walk your own zip code (on foot or on a phone) looking for the signals a business is losing customers online: a quote form that errors, "online booking coming soon", a footer that still says 2018. Then you fix exactly that. |
| Domain | `sidewalksignal.com` is the suggestion. Availability, trademark and social-handle collisions are **UNMEASURED**; the buyer checks and registers under their own account before printing anything. Any domain the buyer picks is fine; the pack does not depend on this one. |
| Handle pattern | `@sidewalksignal` where free; otherwise `@sidewalksignal<city>` |
| Voice | Plain, specific, respectful of the business owner. You describe what you saw on their site in one sentence; you never call it embarrassing. |
| Text mark | `SIDEWALK SIGNAL` in a heavy grotesque; a single vertical bar `|` after the words as the "signal". No logo file is included; a text mark is enough for the door, invoices and email signatures. |
| Colors | Ink `#111`, paper `#fafaf7`, signal amber `#d98a00`. Amber is used once per page, on the one thing you want clicked. |
| Type | System UI stack (`system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto, sans-serif`). Zero web-font downloads. |

## Sentences you can reuse

- "I build finished websites for local businesses. Five pages, mobile-first, the quote form actually works, done in seven days after we agree on scope."
- "I noticed one specific thing on your site. Want me to fix it?"
- "You own everything: domain, source, accounts. I hand it over with a one-page operating guide."

## Sentences you never use

- Any sentence with a dollar figure describing what *you* will take home.
- Any word that promises income, a life change, or scarcity. If a sentence tells the reader what they will bring in, when they can leave their day job, or how few are left, delete it.
- The word for a licensed chain business with royalties. This is a package you bought and run under your own name; it is not that.

## Renaming

If the buyer prefers another name, they change this file, `index.html`, the outreach scripts and the invoice line, then re-run `host/business_pack_desk_instance.py --write` from the repository root so the manifest fingerprint follows the new brand.
