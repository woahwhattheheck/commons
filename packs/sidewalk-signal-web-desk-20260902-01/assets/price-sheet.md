## Rules that keep the prices honest

- **Default to #2 at $2,500.** $1,000 is defensible only for a single-page launch; a finished five-page site at $2,500 leaves room for design, QA and a direct handoff.
- **The seven-day clock starts** after the business says YES, you have supplied your payment rail (invoice, deposit link or PO), the deposit is verified, and the intake packet is complete. Not before.
- **One consolidated revision round** is included. A second round is a change order at a price you set before starting it.
- **Third-party costs are pass-through:** domain registration, hosting, booking or payment provider fees, stock photos. Say so in the quote.
- **Never put a price in an email subject line.** Prices are discussed after a YES to the fix.

## Launch acceptance — every site, every time

A site is done when all of these are true. Put this list in the contract.

- Exactly the agreed pages and features; no placeholder copy; no broken links.
- Responsive at 360, 768 and 1440 px wide.
- HTTPS on the business's own domain; the business owns domain, source and accounts.
- Every form, call, map and booking action reaches its verified destination (send a test and watch it arrive).
- Titles, descriptions, local schema, sitemap and robots present.
- Analytics ownership transferred to the business.
- Lighthouse on mobile: accessibility ≥ 95; best-practices and SEO ≥ 90; performance ≥ 85 unless an approved third-party embed is the measured constraint.
- Direct handoff with credentials and a one-page operating guide.

- 100% for #1 when the business prefers one invoice.

Deposits go through your own rail (your invoicing tool or payment provider).
