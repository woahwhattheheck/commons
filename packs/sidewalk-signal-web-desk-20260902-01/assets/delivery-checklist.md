# Delivery checklist — from YES to handoff in seven days

The clock starts only when all four are true: the business said YES, your
payment rail was supplied, the deposit is verified in your account, and the
intake packet below is complete. If any is missing, the clock has not
started; say so kindly and wait.

## Intake packet (ask for all eight in one message)

1. Legal business name, address, phone, hours (as they should appear).
2. Services or menu with prices, or the categories to show.
3. Logo (any format) or permission to use a text mark; brand colors if any.
4. Photos: exterior, interior, team, work samples; or permission to use their social photos.
5. Reviews they want quoted (with the source) and any awards/licenses to display.
6. Domain registrar login **owned by them** (or authority for you to register a domain in their name) and current hosting details if a site exists.
7. Where forms and bookings should arrive: e-mail address, phone for SMS, and the booking/ordering/payment provider they already use (for offer #3/#4).
8. The one person who approves the launch.

Copy or credentials never go through chat screenshots. Use the registrar's
delegate/collaborator feature where it exists.

## Day plan

| Day | Build | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Deposit verified, intake complete, scope and page list confirmed in writing | Both parties have the same page list |
| 1 | Site map, copy draft from intake, page skeletons, mobile-first layout; register or point the domain | Every agreed page exists with real copy, no placeholder text |
| 2 | Home and service pages, click-to-call, map, proof block; the form or booking flow wired to its real destination | A test submission arrives where the business said it should |
| 3 | Remaining pages, gallery/reviews, local metadata (title, description, schema), sitemap, robots, analytics with the business as owner | Metadata present on every page; analytics property is in their account |
| 4 | Integration for offer #3/#4 (booking, ordering, inventory or payment provider), or the one workflow for the web app | The integration completes one real test cycle end to end |
| 5 | QA pass: 360 / 768 / 1440 px, every link, every action, HTTPS, Lighthouse mobile run; fix everything found | Acceptance list below is green in your own run |
| 6 | Review with the business; the one consolidated revision round | Written list of changes received once, applied once |
| 7 | Launch on their domain; handoff of credentials and the one-page operating guide; acceptance signed | The business confirms acceptance in writing |

## Launch acceptance (same list as the price sheet; put it in the contract)

- [ ] Exactly the agreed pages and features; no placeholder copy; no broken links
- [ ] Responsive at 360, 768 and 1440 px
- [ ] HTTPS on the business's own domain; the business owns domain, source and accounts
- [ ] Every form, call, map and booking action reaches its verified destination
- [ ] Titles, descriptions, local schema, sitemap and robots present
- [ ] Analytics ownership transferred
- [ ] Lighthouse mobile: accessibility ≥ 95; best-practices and SEO ≥ 90; performance ≥ 85 unless an approved third-party embed is the measured constraint
- [ ] Direct handoff with credentials and a one-page operating guide

## Handoff packet

- Domain: registrar account in their name; DNS notes.
- Hosting: where it runs, how to log in, what it costs them per month (pass-through).
- Source: a ZIP or a repository they own.
- Accounts: analytics, search console, any provider; all owned by them, you removed as needed.
- Operating guide (one page): how to change hours, prices, photos; how to see form submissions; who to call.

## Tools you may use

Any site builder, template or AI builder you are comfortable with, as long
as the launch acceptance list passes and the business ends up owning
domain, source and accounts. The demo attachments that ship with this pack
(see `showcase-manifest.json`) show one finished website and one installable
workflow app; use them to show what "finished" means. They are a demo, not
the client's site.

## What you never do

- Launch with placeholder copy or a form that goes nowhere.
- Keep the domain or the accounts in your name after handoff.
- Promise native iOS/Android apps inside this price sheet.
- Start building before the deposit clears.
