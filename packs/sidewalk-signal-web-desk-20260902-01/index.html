Local business websites, fixed from the sidewalk up.

A packaged service business you run yourself from a laptop, two evenings and one Saturday morning a week: find the businesses in your own zip code whose websites show one of nine observable gaps (a quote form that errors, "online booking coming soon", a footer that still says 2018), sell them a finished website at a published price, deliver it in seven days after intake, hand over everything.

Running cost

With free tiers: about $0–$25 per month (domain about $10–$20 per year, free static hosting, forwarded e-mail) plus about two evenings and one Saturday morning a week. With a paid site builder and business e-mail: about $30–$60 per month. Paperwork items are mostly $0–$150 one-time; an LLC and insurance cost more and are your choice. No ads are included or required. Details in running-cost.md.

What is not in the pack

  • No customers, leads, accounts or business lists. The method finds them; you do the finding.
  • No promise of clients, income or results. The only numbers here are prices you charge and hours you spend.
  • No ads setup and no marketing agent. Marketing is the owner's.
  • No payment link on this page until the owner pastes one.

Time budget

Day 0 setup about two hours. Ten site checks about ninety minutes. An evening loop about ninety minutes. A client build is seven days after the deposit clears and the intake packet is complete.
