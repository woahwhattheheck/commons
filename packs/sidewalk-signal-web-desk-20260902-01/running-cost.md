| Item | Needed? | Typical cost | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domain for your own door (for example `sidewalksignal.com`) | optional in week 1; recommended by day 30 | about $10–$20 per year at a major registrar | availability UNMEASURED; you check and buy |

| Paperwork (DBA, licence, EIN, bank) | see `paperwork.md` | mostly $0–$150 one-time; an LLC and insurance cost more and are your choice | not included in the pack price |

| Time | required | about two evenings and one Saturday morning a week | the only input that is not optional |

Pack price: $200 one-time. With free tiers the itemized ranges sum to about $0–$25 per month plus the time above; with a paid builder and business e-mail, about $30–$60 per month.
