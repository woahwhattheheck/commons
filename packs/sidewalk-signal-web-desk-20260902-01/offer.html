The terms in plain words

What a business pack is

A business pack is a ready-to-run business, not a set of instructions. It comes with the brand, the door (the website or booking page), the code or files the business runs on, the price sheet you charge from, the scripts, the checklists, the calendars, the employee-day list, and any starter inventory or printed material the business needs. Nothing in a pack tells you to go buy something elsewhere first; if the business needs it, it is in the pack. The only things you might want to buy beyond the pack are TJLabs supporting products and services. The method behind every pack is published in full so you can read the whole thing before you buy; what you are buying is the build pack itself and the brand.

Sold once, and what that means

Packs labeled sold-once are sold to one buyer. The brand name, the door, the domain suggestion, and that cut of the instructions are not sold again. TJLabs may sell a similar business in the same trade under a different brand with a different door and its own instruction cut; two packs in the same trade are different businesses, not copies. Packs labeled resellable are sold to more than one buyer and say so on the listing.

Delivery after purchase

Digital files (brand assets, door, instructions, scripts, price sheet, checklists, calendars, demo attachments) are delivered within 24 hours of purchase to the email used at checkout. Physical items included with a pack (printed cards, starter inventory) ship after purchase; the listing states what ships. Brand transfer notes and the domain suggestion come with the files.

Support after purchase

Every pack includes the onboarding, training, and daily task lists inside the pack itself. A paid TJLabs support subscription is offered after purchase for direct contact with the people who built the pack; ask the desk for current pricing. TJLabs supporting products and services (engines, tools, site work) are available to pack owners.

Refunds

Digital files are delivered immediately after purchase. For refund questions on a pack, contact tokenjunkielabs@gmail.com with your order details within 7 days of purchase.

The purchase agreement

Every pack sale is an agreement between you and TJLabs. The agreement includes a TJLabs percentage of the business's profit and a partial ownership interest in the business, stated in the agreement you accept at checkout. Read the agreement before you buy; buying means you agree to it.
