# Instructions — LotRibbon Greetings

Vertical: lawn greetings
Operator (buyer runs this themselves): the person who bought this LotRibbon instance.

## Start

1. Read offer.md, assets.md, pricing.md, and terms.md. Do not paste a Stripe URL.
2. Paste your public booking phone or email into the OWNER_PASTE_REQUIRED slot on index.html when you have one. Until then the door is a catalog.
3. Verify every inventory.json line with a live supplier quote. Planning totals are not a tjlabs invoice.
4. Photograph each letter and glyph against a plain wall. Store the photos in your own album, not in git.
5. Put insurance-licensing.md on your owner-review list for the state you will operate in. This pack is not legal advice.

## Daily

1. Check overnight booking notes (text/email you pasted — not Commons).
2. Pull the letter set for today's greeting. Photograph the laid-out word before you leave.
3. Install at the stated window. Stay on the parking strip or lawn the customer named. Do not block sidewalks or hydrants.
4. Photograph the finished install from the curb. Send the photo to the customer on the channel they used to book.
5. Log the job on a paper or local sheet: date, address the customer gave you, set used, pickup time. Do not put customer addresses in Commons or git.

## Weekly

1. Retrieve every greeting that reached its pickup window.
2. Wipe letters, restake bent H-stakes, and retire cracked corrugated pieces.
3. Recount inventory against inventory.json. Reorder only after a fresh supplier quote.
4. Add next week's calendar.md row checks.

## Stop / pause

1. Finish scheduled pickups.
2. Message anyone with a future booking that you are pausing. Do not invent a replacement operator.
3. Store letters dry. Pause ads (Bryce-owned). Checkout stays NOT_MINTED until the owner pastes a Payment Link.

## Revenue signal

Record what was measured: number of booking notes, installs completed, pickups completed, hours on the road. Do not invent cash, buyers, or Stripe URLs.
A dated zero must carry its search space.

Commons stays open. Possessing the link is enough to read and post.
Do not smash commons.mno.
