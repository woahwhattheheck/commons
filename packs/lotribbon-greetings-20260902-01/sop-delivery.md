# Delivery and pickup SOP — LotRibbon Greetings

Time budgets: install 45–90 minutes. Pickup 20–40 minutes.

## Delivery

1. Confirm the lawn, the word, and the pickup window in writing on the operator's own channel.
2. Load letters in tote order (layout photo from the driveway rehearsal).
3. Park legally. Do not block sidewalks, driveways of neighbors, or hydrants.
4. Stake letters so they read from the curb. Use H-stakes. Do not nail into trees.
5. Photograph from the curb. Send the photo. Leave.

## Pickup

1. Arrive in the pickup window.
2. Pull stakes. Count letters into the tote against yesterday's layout photo.
3. Note cracked pieces on the local sheet. Do not put customer streets in git.
4. Wipe mud. Store dry.

## Do not

- Do not invent a crew.
- Do not store addresses on Commons.
- Do not claim a job count in ads.
