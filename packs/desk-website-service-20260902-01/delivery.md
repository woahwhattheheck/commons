# Delivery checklist (seven-day brochure lane)

Use this when the local business said YES to the $2,500 lane. Other lanes strike rows that do not apply. Do not mark a row done without the artifact.

Day 0 (same day as YES)

- [ ] Written scope: pages, NAP text, one contact path
- [ ] Contract placeholder filled and named-human signed (owner of the local business)
- [ ] Access: DNS or host login held by the local business; you do not take over email

Days 1–2

- [ ] Copy deck from their existing pages and Maps listing; no invented testimonials
- [ ] Mobile-first layout at 390px and 1024px
- [ ] HTTPS on the target host

Days 3–4

- [ ] Home, services, contact
- [ ] NAP matches Maps
- [ ] Quote or booking control has a thank-you state (or is explicitly out of scope)

Days 5–6

- [ ] Form tested with a non-production submission
- [ ] 404 page and robots `index, follow` unless they asked otherwise
- [ ] Showcase: if you use TALLY attachments, keep them in the private inventory repo. Do not commit those binaries into this pack.

Day 7

- [ ] Walkthrough with the local business
- [ ] Credentials returned
- [ ] Dated delivery note in the operator log (zero cash is allowed; do not invent a receipt)

Stop work if the local business cannot supply copy or access. Do not invent content to look finished.
