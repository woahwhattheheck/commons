# Outreach script

Sales law for this instance: no price in the subject line. YES first. Then the sheet.

This is a script, not a purchased list. You write to businesses you observed with the gap recipes. You do not get their names from this pack.

## Subject lines (pick one, no dollars)

- Your quote form on [site]
- Quick look at [business]’s booking page
- [Business] site note from a local neighbor

## First note (ask for a YES)

Hello [name if on the site, otherwise the Maps name],

I looked at the public [quote form / booking page / homepage] on [URL] this week. I am a local operator who rebuilds small business sites. I am not selling a list and I am not asking for a card in this note.

Can I send a 15-minute written look at that one page — yes or no?

Thanks,
[your Harborline name]
[phone you actually answer]

## After YES

Send price-sheet.md as a price sheet for work, not as a forecast. Offer one lane that matches the gap you saw. Do not add “limited spots”. Do not add a result inside a time period.

## After NO or silence

Log it. Do not follow up more than once in seven days. Do not invent a reply.
