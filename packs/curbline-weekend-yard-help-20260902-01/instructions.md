# Instructions (SOP) — Curbline Weekend

Vertical: weekend yard-help route (bin-out, tidy, brush pile)
Operator (buyer runs this themselves): you, from a vehicle and a pair of shoes, one Saturday morning plus any same-day jobs.

Everything below is a method. It does not include customers, leads, or a
list of houses. You walk your own block. No outcome is promised; the only
numbers in this pack are prices you charge and hours you spend.

## 1. Start (Friday night, about forty minutes)

1. Print **fifty cards** from `assets/card-copy.md` at a local shop. Black text on cheap card stock is enough. Write your phone number on each card before you leave the shop.
2. Pick **one neighborhood** you can walk in two hours. Stay on public sidewalks. Write the streets on `assets/route-log.md`.
3. Read the price sheet once (`assets/price-sheet.md`). Offer only those three jobs.
4. Put the door on your domain or link this repository's copy. Change only the contact line.
5. Start `assets/paperwork-checklist.md` item 1 (legal form / optional DBA). Read `running-cost.md` so you know what the weekend costs before you spend anything.
6. You are ready when the route log has street names and you have fifty numbered cards.

## 2. Walk a ten-stop Saturday route (two hours)

You are looking for **observable** house-front work: things anyone can see from the sidewalk today. Never put cards in mailboxes. Never enter a backyard. Never climb. Public sidewalks only.

### 2.1 Where to look

- One loop of single-family streets you can finish in two hours.
- Recycle / trash morning if your city publishes a pickup day.
- After wind or a storm: brush on the strip between sidewalk and curb.
- A college-town block of rentals with bins left out.
- Your own street first if you already know the pickup day.

### 2.2 The nine signals (check each house-front for under thirty seconds)

| # | Signal | How to check | It counts when | Offer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Bins still in the garage on pickup morning | Look from the sidewalk on the published pickup day. | Two household bins are inside, not at the curb, and pickup has not happened. | $40 bin-out |
| S2 | Bins left at the curb after pickup | Same sidewalk, after the truck. | Empty bins sit at the curb past noon. | $40 bin-out |
| S3 | Loose brush in the front yard | Look at the lawn and the curb strip. | Twigs, clippings, or litter sit in the front yard, not bagged. | $60 tidy |
| S4 | Litter on the walk | Walk the public sidewalk past the house. | Wrappers, flyers, or cans on the walk or the curb strip. | $60 tidy |
| S5 | Clippings blown onto the sidewalk | After a mow day. | A line of clippings covers the public walk. | $60 tidy |
| S6 | A brush pile already at the edge | Look at the curb strip only. | Branches are stacked, not tied, still in the front yard. | $80 brush pile |
| S7 | Fallen limbs after wind | After a storm, from the sidewalk. | One or two limbs on the front lawn, no roof work, no trees to cut. | $80 brush pile |
| S8 | Cans and bags at the mailbox post | Look at the post, not the box. | Recyclables or bags sit at the post, not in a mailbox. | $60 tidy |
| S9 | Leaf scatter on the driveway apron | Look at the apron from the walk. | A thin leaf sheet on the apron only; no gutter work. | $60 tidy |

One signal is enough for a card drop. Write the **street and a house number range**, not a person's name. Ten houses with a signal is the goal of the first loop; if a block yields fewer, add the next street. A full Saturday still drops all fifty cards.

### 2.3 Name and route (public only)

You do not need a name to leave a card. For a callback, take the street address the caller gives you and write it on `assets/route-log.md`. Never guess a phone number. Never put anything in a mailbox.

### 2.4 Dedupe

One card per door this weekend. If a door already has your card, skip it.

## 3. Daily loop (Saturday, then any callback)

1. Walk two hours. Leave one card at each door. Stay on the sidewalk.
2. Write street names and door count on `assets/route-log.md`.
3. When the phone rings, use `assets/phone-script.md`. Offer only the price-sheet jobs. Same-day windows: morning, midday, or late afternoon.
4. Collect the street address and a callback number. No account, no login.
5. Do the job from `assets/job-checklist.md`. One address. No ladders, no saws, no chemicals. Take a before/after phone photo for you only.
6. Fill `assets/invoice-text.md`. Cash or check on site. A Payment Link is used only after the owner pastes one on this instance.

## 4. Weekly loop (Sunday morning)

1. Count cards left, doors walked, calls, booked stops, and money collected. Write the dated line. Zero is a number.
2. Post the counts in Slack `#business-packs` (`C0BU7JAPUH3`) if you want a public log. That is optional.
3. Read `assets/days-8-30.md` after week 1.

## 5. Stop / pause

Stop the walk if weather makes the sidewalk unsafe. Pause a job if it needs a ladder, a saw, a roof, or a chemical. Those jobs are not on the sheet.

## 6. Revenue signal

A dated zero must carry its search space: streets walked, cards dropped, calls, jobs, money collected. KEEP or SELL stays Bryce's in `keep-vs-sell.md`. Do not invent buyers, cash, or Stripe URLs.
