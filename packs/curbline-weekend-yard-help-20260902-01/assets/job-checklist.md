# Job checklist — one address

Before you start:

1. Confirm the address and the job: bin-out $40, tidy $60, or brush pile $80.
2. Take a before photo on your phone. For you only. Do not post it.
3. Stay in the front yard. No backyard. No roof. No garage interior.

During:

1. Bin-out: roll two household bins to the curb; after pickup, roll them back.
2. Tidy: one hour max. Loose brush and litter in the front yard only. Bag it or stack it at the curb per city rules.
3. Brush pile: stack at the curb. No trees. No saws. No ladders.

After:

1. Take an after photo. For you only.
2. Fill `assets/invoice-text.md`. Cash or check on site.
3. Write the stop on `assets/route-log.md`.
4. Leave. Do not offer a job that is not on the price sheet.

Stop if the work needs a ladder, a saw, a chemical, or a roof.
