# Paperwork checklist — weekend yard-help route

Not legal advice. Nobody files for you. Typical costs are ranges to check, not quotes.

| # | Item | Where to check | Typical cost | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Legal form | Your state Secretary of State "start a business" page | $0 sole prop; LLC is extra and optional | Sole prop is enough for a cash Saturday route in most states |
| 2 | Trade name / DBA | County clerk or Secretary of State | about $10–$100 | Only if you invoice under "Curbline Weekend" |
| 3 | EIN | IRS EIN assistant (official, free) | $0 | Optional; SSN works for a sole prop |
| 4 | Local business licence | City or county business-licence page | about $25–$100 where required | Home-occupation rules vary |
| 5 | Sales tax | State Department of Revenue | $0 if services are not taxable | Yard help is a service in most states; check before you collect tax |
| 6 | Yard-waste / curb rules | City public-works page | $0 | Know what may sit at the curb and when |
| 7 | Mailbox rule | USPS: do not put cards in mailboxes | $0 | Cards stay on the door or under a mat, never in the box |
| 8 | Insurance | Independent agent; ask for lawn / yard service GL | optional; household stops often skip it | Do not invent a premium |
| 9 | Invoice | `assets/invoice-text.md` | $0 | One visit, one address |
| 10 | Bank / cash | Your own account | $0 | Cash or check on site; no invented Payment Link |

Slots in `paperwork.md` stay `OWNER_UNSET` / `HOLD_COUNSEL` until you or counsel fill them.
