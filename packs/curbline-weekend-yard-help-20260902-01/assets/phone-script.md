# Phone script

First line has no price and no payment word. Offer only the three jobs on `assets/price-sheet.md`.

## Pickup

"This is Curbline Weekend. I left a card on your block. I do weekend yard help: bins to the curb, a front tidy, or a brush pile. Which one do you want today?"

If they ask price: "$40 bin-out, $60 tidy, $80 brush pile. One house. Cash or check when I finish."

## Windows

Same-day only: morning, midday, or late afternoon. Pick one. Write the address and a callback number. No account. No login.

## No

"Thanks. Leave the card if you do not want a repeat." Then hang up. One thread per house this weekend.

## After the job

"Done. Invoice is on paper. Cash or check. If you want the same stop next weekend, say so now."

Do not promise a number of stops. Do not say make, earn, or profit with a dollar figure.
