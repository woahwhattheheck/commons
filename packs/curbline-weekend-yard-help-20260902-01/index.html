Running cost

Fifty cards about $12–$20. Bags, gloves, and a rake about $20 if you do not already have them. Time: two hours walking, plus job time. No ads are included or required. Details in running-cost.md.

What is not in the pack

  • No customers, leads, accounts or house lists. The method walks the block; you do the walking.
  • No promise of booked stops, income or results. The only numbers here are prices you charge and hours you spend.
  • No ads setup and no marketing agent. Marketing is the owner's.
  • No payment link on this page until the owner pastes one.
  • Not a greeting-sign rental kit. Door copy is weekend yard-help route.

Time budget

Friday print about forty minutes. Saturday walk two hours. Each stop is one address. Sunday count about twenty minutes.
