# White Box pilot fee

**Inventor:** Bryce Muhlnickel  
**Public product name:** White Box  
**Company:** Muhlnickel

## Recommended pilot

Charge **$30,000 fixed** for a 30-day White Box NDA pilot on one customer-owned GGUF model family.

- **$15,000 on NDA and SOW signing**
- **$15,000 on delivery of the agreed pilot package**
- Customer supplies the model, evaluation harness, representative test set, and one technical owner.
- Muhlnickel performs the work privately and returns only the contracted model-level deliverables.

The pilot fee pays for a bounded technical engagement: model mapping, one reversible ablation, one targeted edit, rollback proof, and results from the customer's evaluation harness. It is not a computer sale.

## Conversion

Offer a **12-month organization license from $100,000 to $175,000** after a successful pilot.

- **$100,000:** one organization, one model family, internal use, and a defined White Box work program.
- **$175,000:** one organization, multiple approved model families, broader internal use, and priority White Box capacity.

The license covers organizational use of the contracted White Box results and continued access to Muhlnickel's private White Box service. The customer may keep its edited models and agreed reports. Bryce Muhlnickel retains his computer, machinery, methods, and internal tooling.

## Commercial boundary

- NDA before model access or technical demonstration.
- No public repository, downloadable product, or computer transfer.
- No customer access to Bryce Muhlnickel's computer.
- Any production deployment, additional model family, appliance, support term, or expanded evaluation is separately scoped.
