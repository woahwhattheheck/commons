# White Box target accounts

These are model owners and on-device or private-deployment companies where a reversible intervention on a customer-controlled model can be worth more than another training cycle. Start with focused model companies and secure-edge operators, not hyperscalers.

## First wave

### 1. Nomic AI

- **Buyer:** CEO, Head of Models, or VP Engineering
- **Why they pay:** Nomic owns model assets and serves developers who care about local control. White Box can make a private, reversible change to a Nomic-controlled GGUF and prove the result in Nomic's own evaluations.

### 2. LM Studio (Element Labs)

- **Buyer:** CEO, Head of Product, or Head of Model Platform
- **Why they pay:** LM Studio sits directly in the local-model workflow. A private White Box capability gives it a high-value path for enterprise customers who need a precise model change without exposing the model or turning the method into a downloadable feature.

### 3. Nous Research

- **Buyer:** CEO, Research Director, or Head of Model Engineering
- **Why they pay:** Nous owns and iterates recognizable model families for demanding local users. Reversible model surgery gives its research team a fast way to isolate and alter a target behavior while preserving a clean rollback.

### 4. Arcee AI

- **Buyer:** CEO, Chief Product Officer, or VP Model Engineering
- **Why they pay:** Arcee builds compact, domain-focused models and enterprise model systems. White Box can help make a narrow, measurable change to a customer-controlled model where retraining would be slower or less exact.

### 5. Hugging Face

- **Buyer:** Head of SmolLM, VP Product, or Head of Enterprise
- **Why they pay:** Hugging Face owns small-model assets and serves organizations deploying models locally. A private engagement can validate reversible changes on a selected internal model without making White Box an open-source component.

### 6. Liquid AI

- **Buyer:** CEO, Head of Model Engineering, or VP Edge AI
- **Why they pay:** Liquid AI is focused on efficient models for constrained and edge environments. A targeted edit with rollback proof is valuable when model behavior must change without adding a large retraining or deployment burden.

## Second wave

### 7. Mistral AI

- **Buyer:** Chief Product Officer, VP Models, or Head of Edge
- **Why they pay:** Mistral owns widely deployed model families used in private environments. White Box offers a controlled intervention on a selected model with acceptance measured by Mistral's own harness.

### 8. Cohere

- **Buyer:** VP Model Engineering, Head of Private Deployment, or Chief Product Officer
- **Why they pay:** Cohere sells enterprise models into controlled environments. Reversible model-level edits can address a customer-specific behavior while keeping the model, evaluation data, and result under enterprise control.

### 9. Aleph Alpha

- **Buyer:** Chief Product Officer, Head of Models, or Head of Sovereign AI
- **Why they pay:** Aleph Alpha serves regulated and sovereign deployments where privacy, traceability, and rollback matter. White Box fits a private engagement tied to an internal evaluation harness.

### 10. Anduril Industries

- **Buyer:** VP Artificial Intelligence, VP Autonomy, or General Manager for Lattice
- **Why they pay:** Anduril deploys software and models at the secure edge, where narrow behavior changes and verifiable rollback can matter more than a broad retraining run. The work can remain under NDA and inside a controlled evaluation process.

### 11. Shield AI

- **Buyer:** Chief Technology Officer, VP AI, or Head of Autonomy
- **Why they pay:** Shield AI operates models in constrained, mission-oriented environments. A reversible, targeted edit gives its team another way to test a behavior change against existing autonomy evaluations without taking possession of Bryce's computer.

### 12. Primer AI

- **Buyer:** Chief Technology Officer, VP Machine Learning, or Head of Federal Products
- **Why they pay:** Primer builds language systems for national-security customers with strict data and deployment controls. A private GGUF intervention, rollback artifact, and customer-run evaluation package match that buying environment.

## Outreach order

Begin with Nomic AI, LM Studio, Nous Research, Arcee AI, Hugging Face, and Liquid AI. They are close enough to local model artifacts to understand the offer quickly and small enough for a technical buyer to authorize a $30,000 pilot. Use the second wave for private-deployment and secure-edge buyers after the pilot package has a concise, customer-safe proof story.
