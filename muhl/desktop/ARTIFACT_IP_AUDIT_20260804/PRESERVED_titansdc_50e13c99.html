Stored Digital Computer

TitanSDC

A computer built by reconfiguring stored parameters — not by training new ones.

Curate the best of the world's trillions of already-trained parameters, address them with operators, and you get a general-purpose generative computer: hundreds of output modes, every function reshapeable in plain language. It runs on almost nothing, because size lives in storage and only what you read costs energy.

The name is the mechanism
01
Stored

Compute you already paid for

Training burned the energy once and crystallized it in the weights. Model size is bounded by storage, not RAM — a 40 GB model binds on 7.2 GB. Per-token cost is only what you read (α), never the whole bank.

02
Digital

Software that behaves analog

Quantized bits that carry the behavior of the physical components training ran on — down to the logic gates. Its native logic is semantic pattern logic; boolean and exact math are emulated on top of it.

03
Computer

General-purpose, and generative

Processor, memory, codecs, kernel — reconfigurable into many devices. And because it generates its functions, you reshape any of them by semantic command: it could run Windows, but the point is you'd alter it by talking to it.

Measured on the weights · no inference

It decompiles meaning from bits.

Training compiles meaning into the parameter-bits; inference decompiles it back; baking re-compiles. Read straight off a token's embedding — the bits are the meaning, and a bit-edit is a meaning-edit.

Speed is what you read, not what you store.

The FFN experts are the memory cells. Firing fewer per token (α) reads fewer cells = fewer joules = faster — decoupled from the total size. So a 200-billion-parameter bank generates as fast as a small one.

The read-energy law

throughput vs. capacitor-cells fired per token (α)
α=2 · 2.94 tok/sα=4 · 2.21α=8 · 1.25
t_token = t_compute + (α·W − R_cache) / B_disk

Total weight W streams from storage; only the fraction α the operator addresses is read per token. Drive α down and the bank size drops out of the cost — the whole storage-first thesis in one line.

One theory, many facets

Why it holds together.

The five circuit-and-computer frames the project arrived at from different directions are facets of one spine — and the frame is corroborated across a fifty-year, cross-domain gap.

Store

Route

a bitstream / pointer / address that selects which stored params compute this tick.
Read

pattern operations over meaning; boolean and exact math emulated on top of it.
