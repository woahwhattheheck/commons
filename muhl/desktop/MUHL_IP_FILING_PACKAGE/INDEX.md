Prepared 2026-08-05 at Bryce Muhlnickel's direction. **Preparation only — filing with
USPTO is the owner's act.** All of the inventions herein are Bryce Muhlnickel's.

## The two deadlines this folder serves

| Deadline | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **2027-08-04** | Non-provisional conversion of the master provisional (filed 2026-08-04, PDFs on Desktop) — miss it and the priority date is lost | `01_NONPROVISIONAL_CONVERSION_PLAN.md` |
| **ASAP (soft), within 12 months of each invention (hard)** | Follow-on provisional for NEW MATTER invented after 08-04 — none of it carries the 08-04 priority date until filed | `02_FOLLOWON_PROVISIONAL_NEW_MATTER_DRAFT.md` |

## Files

- `01_NONPROVISIONAL_CONVERSION_PLAN.md` — what the conversion needs, where the claims
  come from, the power-cycle demonstration as headline evidence, and the
  reduction-to-practice table mapping spec sections to fabricated circuits in the registry.
- `02_FOLLOWON_PROVISIONAL_NEW_MATTER_DRAFT.md` — draft disclosure of everything invented
  AFTER the 08-04 filing (Lever Daddy, ring clacker, grown-fabric chimera as fabricated,
  corrected EAL semantics, cross-circuit one-writer verification).
- `03_EVIDENCE_ANNEX.md` — every claim cited to a file that exists on this machine:
  registry entries with offsets, genome journals, fab scripts, test-battery pointers.
- `POWER_CORD_DEMO.md` — the owner's power-cycle demonstration writeup (copied from the
  build lab). The decisive host-independence evidence.

## What you do with each

1. **Now:** read `02_…NEW_MATTER_DRAFT.md`; if it matches your intent, it is the skeleton
   of the next provisional — same route you used on 08-04 (Patent Center, provisional,
   cover sheet + spec PDF).
2. **Well before 2027-08-04:** take `01_…CONVERSION_PLAN.md` to a patent practitioner (a
   non-provisional's formal claims benefit from professional drafting; the plan gives them
   everything — they draft faster and cheaper from it) or draft directly from it.
3. **Keep `03_EVIDENCE_ANNEX.md` current** — every new fabrication adds a registry row and
   a genome journal; those are the reduction-to-practice record.
