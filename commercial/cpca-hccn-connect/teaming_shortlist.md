## Buyer-fit model

A useful prime candidate should ideally combine:

1. direct FQHC / health-center / PCA-HCCN delivery history;
2. individualized Technical Assistance and/or structured Group Training evidence;
3. data / interoperability / AI domain relevance;
4. credible references and named personnel that the prime can itself provide;
5. a reason to add TJLabs rather than bid entirely alone.

The fifth factor is intentionally strategic: the strongest incumbent may be the **worst** teaming target if it has no need for a specialist subcontractor.
