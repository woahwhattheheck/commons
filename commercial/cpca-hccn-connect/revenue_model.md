The marketplace can create repeated work through 2028-07-31, but qualification guarantees neither referrals nor minimum volume. CPCA describes project-specific SOWs, time-and-materials delivery and monthly payment based on approved hours. Treat this as a channel, not booked revenue.

## Unit economics worksheet

For an owner-approved rate `R` and approved hours `H`:

- gross billings = `R * H`
- delivery labor cost = `loaded_internal_hour_cost * H`
- subcontractor cost = contractually agreed partner cost
- contribution before overhead = `gross billings - delivery labor cost - subcontractor cost - direct travel/tools`

No values are embedded here because the RFP asks the vendor to propose rates and evaluates cost reasonableness at 15%.

## Revenue funnel states

1. `UNQUALIFIED` - hard eligibility evidence incomplete.
2. `READY_FOR_OWNER_SUBMISSION_REVIEW` - deterministic completeness only; not submitted.
3. `SUBMITTED` - human owner actually submitted and retained receipt.
4. `MARKETPLACE_APPROVED` - CPCA approval received.
5. `LEAD_RECEIVED` - a participating health center / CPCA requests or matches work.
6. `SOW_NEGOTIATION` - specific scope, timeline and budget under review.
7. `SOW_APPROVED` - CPCA-approved SOW exists.
8. `DELIVERING` - approved work in progress.
9. `INVOICED` - hours submitted under buyer process.
10. `CASH_LANDED` - payment settled.

Never collapse links, proposals, approvals, invoices or promised work into cash.
