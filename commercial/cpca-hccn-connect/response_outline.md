This section describes a **future delivery method**, not past performance.

### AI governance readiness

1. Establish decision rights, approved/prohibited use boundaries, risk tiers and evidence ownership.
2. Inventory AI uses and vendors; bind each use to data classes, human oversight and measurable outcomes.
3. Build approval, exception, change-control and incident/evidence workflows.
4. Produce a prioritized governance roadmap with owners, milestones and auditable acceptance criteria.

### AI vendor evaluation

1. Define health-center-specific evaluation criteria before vendor scoring.
2. Capture security/privacy, data-use, model-change, monitoring, accessibility, support and exit evidence.
3. Separate vendor assertions from independently verifiable evidence.
4. Deliver a weighted decision record, gap log and procurement questions without certifying legal/compliance conclusions.

### AI use-case education

1. Frame use cases around patient/community benefit, workforce impact, data sensitivity and operational readiness.
2. Teach practical risk/benefit evaluation with health-center scenarios supplied or approved by CPCA.
3. Convert candidate uses into measurable pilot hypotheses and stop conditions.
4. Leave reusable worksheets and decision records.
