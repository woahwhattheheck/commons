# Sasria RFP2026/22 — TJLabs paid specialist workshare

**State:** PROPOSED / NOT ACCEPTED. This is an internal teaming scaffold, not a bid, subcontract, partnership, certification, buyer submission, award, invoice, payment, or revenue claim.

## Prime / training lead owns

A qualified South African prime or training lead must own and truthfully evidence:

- all CSD, B-BBEE, SBD, annexure, confidentiality and bidder-detail returns;
- legal entity, authorized signatory, procurement-portal account, and final bid submission;
- training-body association/accreditation and formal certification capability;
- the required post-training AI-platform access and proof;
- named facilitators, qualifications/CVs, recent AI-training history, regulated/financial-services experience, and client reference letters;
- training delivery, attendance/certification records, guest speakers, advisory meetings, and buyer relationship;
- commercial prime price, taxes, preference-point evidence, subcontracting declarations, and all buyer representations.

TJLabs will not backfill any of those fields with software-generated claims.

## Proposed paid TJLabs seam

Subject to written scope and a qualified prime, TJLabs can own a bounded **paid** technical/evaluation workshare:

1. **Responsible-AI evidence map** — map agreed curriculum/labs to NIST AI RMF and/or the prime-selected recognized framework, with traceable evidence references rather than compliance claims.
2. **Role-pathway acceptance design** — measurable learning outcomes and evaluation criteria for executives, general employees/specialists, AI Navigators, project team, technical team, and business process owners.
3. **Hands-on lab QA** — reproducible scenario cases for GenAI, productivity, governance, safe failure, human escalation, and role-appropriate use.
4. **Evaluation harness** — pre/post assessment schema, evidence-retention rules, deterministic outcome compilation, and exception/HOLD handling.
5. **Governance artefact pack** — decision records, rubric outputs, training-evidence index, and handoff controls suitable for prime review.
6. **Post-training evidence handoff** — adoption/evaluation summary and backlog inputs; no claim that TJLabs issues the buyer-required formal certification.

## Acceptance criteria for TJLabs workshare

A negotiated workshare should not be called complete unless:

- every agreed role pathway has explicit learning outcomes, delivery mode, evaluation method, and retained artefact;
- every scoring/evaluation output can be traced to supplied evidence;
- unsupported facts fail closed to `HOLD` rather than being inferred;
- prime-owned qualification and submission fields remain mechanically separate from TJLabs technical evidence;
- deterministic compiler replay produces the same receipt for identical evidence;
- the prime reviews and accepts the final technical/evidence pack in writing.
