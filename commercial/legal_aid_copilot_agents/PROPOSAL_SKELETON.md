## 3. Training and mentoring product

A practical delivery sequence:

1. **Readiness and use-case framing** — participant goals, approved data boundaries, tenant constraints, use cases, and acceptance criteria.
2. **Agent foundations** — Copilot Studio concepts, instructions, knowledge grounding, actions, environments, and permissions.
3. **Guided build** — participants build a bounded agent against approved non-production or otherwise authorized resources.
4. **Evaluation lab** — score task success, groundedness, permission boundaries, safe failure, and human handoff against retained evidence.
5. **Governance/security review** — approved data sources, least privilege, escalation, logging/audit, and retention/deletion.
6. **Pilot hardening** — remediate failed evaluation cases, test release controls, and document ownership/limitations.
7. **Handoff** — functioning-agent demonstration, evidence pack, operator runbook, adoption metrics, backlog, and governance next steps.

Exact Microsoft tenant, licensing, security, legal, and production-release decisions remain with the buyer and qualified delivery partner.
