## Proposed planning method — response content, not past-performance claim

1. Readiness and use-case inventory.
2. Data classification and information-handling boundaries.
3. Governance, risk, human-oversight, escalation, and change controls.
4. Secure architecture: identity/access, integration, retention, logging, incident handling.
5. Vendor due diligence and procurement strategy.
6. Phased implementation, training, evaluation, adoption, and budget roadmap.

## Third-party AI due-diligence checklist

For candidate tools/vendors, request and record evidence for:

- whether/how customer data is used for model training or improvement;
- retention and deletion behavior;
- subprocessors and supply chain;
- security audits and certifications;
- incident response / breach-notification terms;
- access controls and administrative privileges.

A vendor answer is evidence, not an independent certification. Unknown stays unknown.

## Owner-input red lines

Do not infer or fabricate company age/headquarters/headcount, employees, client engagements, legal-sector experience, client references, certifications, hourly rates, cost ranges, subcontractor commitments, or legal/compliance conclusions from Commons code. Repository artifacts can substantiate methods/deliverable examples, not corporate history.
