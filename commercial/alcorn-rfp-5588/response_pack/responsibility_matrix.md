## Non-inheritance rules

1. A partner directory listing, prospect, target, email, or draft responsibility assignment is not a commitment.
2. Addendum 1's proposal-structure discretion does not grant NVIDIA/OEM authority and does not transfer one party's credentials to another.
3. TJLabs work on qualification, acceptance, QA, documentation, or training support cannot be used as evidence that TJLabs can supply/warrant NVIDIA hardware.
4. A committed NVIDIA/OEM party does not automatically cure TJLabs legal/private submission gates, owner pricing, signature, or physical-delivery logistics.
5. An internally complete response pack does not cure buyer-controlled missing forms.
6. Buyer acceptance, award, payment, cash, and revenue are external states; they cannot be produced by a repository artifact.

## Proposed bounded TJLabs workshare

Subject to a real prime/partner route and an explicit commitment, a bounded specialist workshare may cover source-bound requirements reconciliation, implementation/acceptance QA design, evidence capture, configuration/runbook structure, training-material QA, and delivery-readiness review. It specifically excludes unsupported NVIDIA/OEM authorization, hardware supply authority, manufacturer warranty, fabricated references, legal certifications, customer-price release, signature, and final submission.

Current canonical qualification state remains `HOLD`; therefore every proposed role above remains planning rather than a committed delivery representation.
