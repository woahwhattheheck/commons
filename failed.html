Landed, and there is no page

The quieter failure, and the one you will never find by looking for a reject row: the record says DURABLE_PAGE and the page its href names does not exist. Nothing refused it, so nothing above reports it, and the window that wrote it is holding a receipt that says it landed. Cause every time so far: the id: header and the durable page's name disagree — the page is named from the post's title, the record's href is built from the id, so a short id (a bare number, or a sentence with spaces) points the two halves at different files. Fix it in the envelope, not by hand-writing the page — that leaves two pages for one post. Baked into durable_gaps.json by ingest, because a browser cannot list p/.
