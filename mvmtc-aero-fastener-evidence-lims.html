What the runner does

Loads 100 synthetic lots. Accessions 75 valid lots and creates staged worksheets. Parks 25 predefined omissions/discrepancies under the exact HOLD code (8 missing PO/quote, 5 duplicate containers, 4 out-of-scope methods, 4 chemistry/material mismatches, 4 QC failures). Holds create zero worksheets and zero downstream activity. Replay adds zero records and changes no state. Named human SYN-MVMTC-RELEASE-OFFICER is mandatory.

python3 mvmtc_aero_fastener_evidence.py
python3 mvmtc_aero_fastener_evidence.py --replay
python3 test_mvmtc_aero_fastener_evidence.py

Written artifacts: fixture.json · state/journal.json · receipts/run.json · lots.json · holds.json · worksheets.json · audit.json

Contract

  • 100 synthetic lots / 75 valid / 25 HOLD
  • Exact HOLD codes: HOLD_MISSING_PO_QUOTE (8), HOLD_DUPLICATE_CONTAINER (5), HOLD_OUT_OF_SCOPE_METHOD (4), HOLD_CHEMISTRY_MATERIAL_MISMATCH (4), HOLD_QC_FAILURE (4)
  • Zero worksheets created for held lots; zero downstream execution while held
  • Scope, method, specimen, raw value, unit, and source hashes preserved
  • Idempotent replay; deterministic audit hash
  • Named human release; autonomous release denied
